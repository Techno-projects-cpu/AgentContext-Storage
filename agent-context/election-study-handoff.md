## Maintenance and cautions

- The current-affairs sheet is a snapshot, not a live source. Verify office-holders, results, SIR orders, legislative status and relevant election instructions against current official sources before using them in a competition.
- Exact 2026 result totals varied in preliminary reporting; the pack avoids treating live leads as final tallies. Consult ECI results for exact numbers.
- When editing procedural details, check the current Acts, rules, ECI directions and binding court decisions; older handbooks can be superseded. Preserve the distinction between five-station VVPAT verification and post-result 5% EVM verification.
- No video, rendered assets or external datasets are needed for this pack. The user's broader preferences and film handoff remain in [`HANDOFF.md`](HANDOFF.md).
