## h3-limit — Everyone in this market promises everything. Stating what you will not…

**Hook:** "Four things this developer will refuse to do."

**Promise:** No templates sold as custom work. No framework unless the problem needs one. No invoice for work that was not agreed in writing first.

**CTA:** Read the limits

**Hypothesis:** A negative/limits-first creative earns a higher click-through and a lower bounce than a benefit-first creative, because the destination page states the same limits.
