Launch day · 19 Nov 2026 · IShowSpeed

Get Speed's GTA 6 clips in about twenty minutes.

SupaClip is the right tool and it runs in your browser, where YouTube is not blocked. This pack supplies everything SupaClip cannot know: Speed's credit burned into the frames, the moment list, the caption lines, and a 60-second cap so the clips don't get Content-ID'd on sight.

Why it has to be you clicking

This sandbox can only reach GitHub, PyPI and npm. YouTube, Twitch and SupaClip all fail at the network handshake here, and so did the downloader sites (savefrom is discontinued in the US, ssyoutube returns 502) and the GitHub-Runner route (Actions is off on this repo). Free hosting won't take a 30 GB VOD either. So the pipeline was built, tested and committed — the one step that needs an unblocked browser is the one step only you can do.

Six steps

1

Open SupaClip and paste the VOD link

supaclip.ai → new project → paste the YouTube VOD URL. Choose the stream video type, not podcast — that is what makes it frame the facecam over the gameplay instead of cropping a talking head.

Free tier: 30 AI minutes a month. A 10-hour VOD will not fit, and does not need to: trim the URL range to the first 60–90 minutes (&t=0s and the last 2 h can wait), or run it twice.

2

Let it draft, then keep only what is in this list

SupaClip scores moments on its own rubric. Override it with the five that actually travel (full brief with what to look for is in candidates.json → targets):

MomentWhat to catch
First reactionthe 30 s after he gets control: face, then gameplay
Police stop ragefirst wanted level, him blaming the car
Car-theft failphone out, locked car, alarm going off
Leonida at nightVice City skyline, ideally in a storm
The gatorany animal that bites him — needs no context at all
3

Cut each clip to 40 seconds or less

Speed clips live or die in the first 1.5 seconds, so start on the shout, not before it. Anything over 60 s of someone else's footage is the thing YouTube's Content ID is built to find.

4

Export, then burn his credit in — one command

SupaClip's free export is 720p with no watermark; Pro is 1080p. Fine either way, because the last step happens locally and takes seconds:

cd works/clips/speed-gta6 tools/burn_credit.sh downloads/supaclip/*.mp4

This stamps CLIP: IShowSpeed + his channel into every frame, above the platform caption zone, so it survives re-uploads and anyone stripping your caption text. Re-running is a no-op. assets/credit.png is already rendered.

5

Or cut from the VOD yourself, if SupaClip is out of minutes

Same pack, no AI, no quota, ffmpeg only. Say when you saw it and it cuts immediately:

python3 tools/set_moment.py 01 4823.5 4851.0 \ --hook "First frame of GTA 6" \ --title "IShowSpeed sees GTA 6 for the first time" FR=60 ./capture.sh speed-vod.mp4 01

1080×1920, whole frame over a blurred fill, credit burned, -14 LUFS audio. python3 tools/set_moment.py --list shows what is filled in. Full captions need a transcript: ./build.sh speed-vod.mp4 adds them.

6

Post, with the credit in the text too

Every caption line is written in candidates.json and ends with Clip: @IShowSpeed. Keep it there. Nothing in this pack posts anything on its own — that stays your call, every time.

What was tested here, so you are not debugging my guesses

  1. Crop and encode. capture.sh rendered a real 1080×1920 H.264 clip from a 16:9 source with the entire frame preserved over a blurred fill — frame checked by eye.
  2. The credit burn. Composited into a finished clip, verified on a decoded frame, and made idempotent via a container marker so a second run can't double-stamp.
  3. The caption engine. The vendored video-to-shorts-skill produced a captioned vertical clip end to end, and its rubric caught a hook that repeated the dialogue.
  4. Timing snap. All ten written start/end sentences matched onto a reworded transcript, refusing to guess when an anchor was missing.

Two honest notes

SupaClip will not have this VOD indexed instantly. It reads the video itself, so a stream that finished hours ago is fine — but a stream that is still live has no VOD to read. If it errors, you are early: wait for the VOD, or use step 5 on a Twitch download.

Speed's audio peaks. He screams, and a stream mixed for Twitch will clip on export. If a clip sounds torn, re-cut it with a trim: FF_EXTRA="-af volume=-2dB" — the loudness pass then has headroom to work with.

Everything in this folder is committed: candidates.json (moment brief), tools/ (capture, credit, snap, review), assets/credit.png. The boss pack sits next door in works/clips/gta6-launch/ and uses the same tools.
