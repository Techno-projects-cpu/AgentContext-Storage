## If you have 20 minutes

- 8 min: read one topic card and explain it without looking.
- 8 min: answer three questions in a mock; check explanations.
- 4 min: write one mistake and one question to revisit.

## A realistic seven-day route

Each day can be 30–45 minutes; extend only if convenient. If the test is sooner, do the priority cards and Mock A questions you have time for.

| Day | Learn | Train |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cell parts and plant/animal differences | Draw a labelled cell from memory; explain three functions. |
| 2 | DNA, genes, RNA and proteins | Explain information flow and one limitation of the analogy. |
| 3 | Microbes, immunity and antibiotics | Sort claims into supported, false or needs evidence. |
| 4 | Enzymes, pH, diffusion and osmosis | Explain why changing two conditions makes a test unclear. |
| 5 | Hypothesis, variables, controls and replicates | Design a fair paper-only plant investigation. |
| 6 | Graphs, averages, percentages and limitations | Take [Mock A](04-mock-foundations.md); correct mistakes. |
| 7 | Review weak topics and rest | Take [Mock B](05-mock-research.md), or do half if short of time. |

Repeat a mock after a gap: first without notes, then with explanations. Your score tracks practice, **not a prediction of selection**. Our suggested practice times are not official timings.

## Ten fast cards

1. **Cell membrane:** selective boundary; both plant and animal cells have one.
2. **DNA / gene:** DNA holds information; a gene is a DNA segment yielding a functional product.
3. **DNA → RNA → protein:** a basic information pathway; some genes produce functional RNA.
4. **Microbes:** bacteria can be beneficial or harmful; viruses require host cells to reproduce.
5. **Antibiotics:** act against susceptible bacteria, not influenza virus itself.
6. **Enzyme:** biological catalyst; conditions can affect its activity and shape.
7. **Osmosis:** net movement of water through a selectively permeable membrane.
8. **Independent vs dependent variable:** what is changed vs what is measured.
9. **Control + replicates:** a suitable comparison and several samples improve interpretation, but do not guarantee an unbiased result.
10. **Percentage increase:** change ÷ original × 100. 20 → 40 is a 100% increase; the final amount is 200% of the original.
