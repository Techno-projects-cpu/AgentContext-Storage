# Mock A — Biology and scientific thinking from zero 🧬

30 original practice questions; **not an official CCMB paper or predicted test pattern**. Suggested practice: 35–45 minutes, one point each; no penalty. Cover the answer section. Questions 1–24 are MCQs; 25–30 need a short answer. Explain your reasoning even when you guess. [Start page](README.md)

## Questions

1. Which structure selectively controls movement into and out of plant and animal cells? A) Cell membrane B) Chloroplast C) Bone. D) Cell wall
2. Which structure contains most DNA in a typical plant or animal cell? A) Vacant space outside the cell. B) Stoma C) Nucleus D) Cell wall
3. What is a gene? A) A type of microscope only B) An entire organ C) Always a complete protein. D) A segment of DNA with information for a functional product
4. Which simplified pathway describes common information flow? A) Protein → bone → cell wall B) DNA → RNA → protein. C) RNA → DNA only in every case D) Food → sunlight → chromosome
5. Ribosomes primarily: A) produce sunlight B) make proteins C) filter urine D) pump blood.
6. Which statement is correct? A) Bacteria have no genetic material B) Every bacterium causes disease C) Bacteria are made of rocks. D) Bacteria have DNA but lack a membrane-bound nucleus
7. The gas that helps yeast-raised dough expand is: A) carbon dioxide B) nitrogen C) chlorine. D) helium
8. Antibiotics do not treat influenza itself because: A) antibiotics target bacterial structures or processes, not the virus. B) antibiotics are always vitamins C) influenza virus is a plant D) influenza is caused by a fungus
9. A vaccine generally: A) helps train the immune system B) replaces all medicines C) directly gives a person bacterial resistance to every infection. D) supplies all nutrients
10. An enzyme is best described as: A) a type of organ B) sunlight. C) a unit of temperature D) a biological catalyst
11. Extreme heat can reduce the activity of many enzymes because: A) enzymes become planets. B) their atoms vanish C) their structure can be disrupted D) all water turns into DNA
12. At 25°C, which solution is more acidic? A) pH 7 B) pH 5 C) pH 8 D) pH 3.
13. Water enters a raisin placed in water primarily by: A) nuclear fusion. B) combustion C) sound transmission D) osmosis
14. In simple diffusion, net movement is generally from: A) higher to lower concentration B) solid to light. C) no particles to more particles without motion D) lower to higher concentration in all situations
15. The oxygen released in photosynthesis comes mainly from: A) carbon dioxide B) soil minerals C) sugar. D) water
16. Which statement is true? A) Only animals use energy B) Plants respire day and night. C) Respiration occurs only when plants receive light D) Plants never respire
17. Xylem mainly transports: A) electrical appliances B) water and dissolved minerals C) blood. D) sugar
18. An artery is a vessel that carries blood: A) always rich in oxygen B) always towards the heart C) away from the heart D) only in leaves.
19. A student tests light's effect on growth. The factor intentionally changed is called the: A) measurement error. B) independent variable C) dependent variable D) conclusion
20. What is a useful control group for a fertiliser experiment? A) A completely different species given different water B) No comparison group C) Similar plants without the fertiliser, under otherwise comparable conditions. D) Only the tallest plant
21. Why use several similar plants per group? A) To help assess natural variation B) To avoid measuring anything. C) To guarantee a particular answer D) To eliminate every source of bias automatically
22. A count rises from 20 to 30. Its percentage increase is: A) 10% B) 150%. C) 50% D) 30%
23. The mean of 2, 4 and 6 is: A) 6. B) 4 C) 2 D) 3
24. Two things rise together in a graph. What can you conclude without further evidence? A) No other factor can matter B) Both must be imaginary C) They are associated in the observed data, but cause is not established. D) One definitely causes the other
25. Name two structures found in a typical plant cell but not an animal cell.
26. In one sentence, distinguish a vaccine from antiserum.
27. A study gives fertiliser and extra water to group A, but neither to group B. Why can't it isolate fertiliser's effect?
28. A trial reports 6 germinated seeds out of 10. What percentage germinated?
29. A researcher observes that 4 of 5 plants grow taller in light. Give one reason not to conclude the result will occur in every plant.
30. What is one safe, evidence-based response to a surprising health claim forwarded online?

---

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **A:** both plant and animal cells have a selectively permeable cell membrane.
2. **C:** the nucleus contains most DNA in these cells.
3. **D:** some genes produce proteins, others functional RNA.
4. **B:** DNA → RNA → protein is a useful basic pathway, though biology includes exceptions.
5. **B:** ribosomes assemble proteins.
6. **D:** bacteria contain DNA, but no membrane-bound nucleus.
7. **A:** yeast fermentation releases CO₂.
8. **A:** bacterial-targeted antibiotics do not act against the virus itself.
9. **A:** vaccines stimulate immune responses; protection depends on the vaccine and circumstances.
10. **D:** enzymes accelerate reactions without being consumed overall.
11. **C:** excessive heat can disrupt the structure needed for function.
12. **D:** lower pH represents greater hydrogen-ion activity.
13. **D:** net water movement through a selectively permeable membrane is osmosis.
14. **A:** random motion produces net movement down a concentration gradient.
15. **D:** photosynthetic oxygen comes from splitting water.
16. **B:** plants respire day and night.
17. **B:** xylem transports water and minerals; phloem carries sugars.
18. **C:** vessel names refer to direction relative to the heart, not always oxygen content.
19. **B:** independent = deliberately changed; dependent = measured.
20. **C:** a comparable no-fertiliser group helps isolate the effect.
21. **A:** replication helps assess variability; design can still be biased.
22. **C:** (30 − 20)/20 × 100 = 50%.
23. **B:** (2 + 4 + 6)/3 = 4.
24. **C:** an association alone does not prove causation.
25. **Cell wall and chloroplasts** are suitable examples; not every plant cell has chloroplasts.
26. **Vaccination stimulates an immune response; antiserum provides preformed antibodies.**
27. **Two factors vary:** water or fertiliser, or their combination, could explain the difference.
28. **60%:** 6/10 × 100.
29. **Small sample / limited conditions / biological variation** are valid reasons. A suitable comparison is also needed.
30. **Check trustworthy evidence and seek qualified advice before sharing or changing treatment.**

For short answers, award a point for the essential correct idea rather than exact wording. Review any question you guessed, even if correct.
