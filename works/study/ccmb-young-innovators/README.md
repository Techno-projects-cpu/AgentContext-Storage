# CCMB Young Innovators: From Zero to Scientist Mode 🔬⚔️

A preparation pack for a curious Class 10 student. **This is independent practice, not an official CCMB syllabus, sample paper, test pattern, selection criterion or promise of selection.** We do not know the current test's question type, length, marking, or exact topic weight. Focus on understanding and ask your teacher to confirm the current notice.

## Start here

1. [Life from zero: cells, DNA, microbes and enzymes](01-life-from-zero.md) — learn the concepts with analogies, questions and Telugu keyword glosses.
2. [Think like a scientist: experiments, data and evidence](02-think-like-a-scientist.md) — controls, variables, charts, probability and research reasoning.
3. [Quick revision, daily plan and sources](03-revision-plan.md) — priority cards and a manageable schedule alongside Chekumuki.
4. [Mock test A: Foundations](04-mock-foundations.md) — 30 original questions with answers and explanations.
5. [Mock test B: Research reasoning](05-mock-research.md) — 30 original questions with answers and explanations.

**Strategy:** first learn the idea, then solve without looking, then explain why the right answer works and why a tempting wrong answer does not. Do not worry if maths is a weak point: our arithmetic is accessible, with worked examples.

## Safety

These pages are for reading, thinking and hypothetical data analysis. **Do not culture microbes, collect bodily samples, handle hazardous chemicals or use mains electricity for home experiments.** Any real lab activity must follow the organiser's trained supervision and safety rules. Never taste laboratory materials.
