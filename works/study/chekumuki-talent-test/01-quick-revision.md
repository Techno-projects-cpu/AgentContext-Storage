# Quick Revision: Start Here

Study the first 25; cover the answers and explain **why**. English is primary; Telugu glosses help recall.

1. **Speed = distance ÷ time.** SI unit: metre per second (m/s). వేగం.
2. **Velocity** includes direction; speed does not.
3. **Force = mass × acceleration.** SI unit: newton (N). బలం.
4. **Mass** measures inertia; **weight** is gravitational force and can change with location.
5. Friction can oppose relative motion and helps us walk without slipping.
6. Pressure = force ÷ area. For the same force, a smaller area produces greater pressure.
7. **Sound needs a material medium**; it cannot travel through vacuum.
8. Light travels through vacuum. Reflection is bouncing; refraction is a change in direction due to a change in speed at a boundary.
9. A plane mirror makes a **virtual, upright** image, equal in size to the object.
10. A convex lens can converge parallel light rays; a concave lens diverges them.
11. Electric current is charge flow; SI unit is ampere. Current = voltage ÷ resistance for an ohmic conductor at constant temperature.
12. In a **series** circuit the same current passes through each component; in **parallel**, each branch has the same potential difference.
13. Power = voltage × current. Electrical energy = power × time. A commercial unit of electricity is 1 kWh, not 1 kW.
14. An element has one kind of atom; a compound has elements chemically combined in fixed proportions.
15. Atomic number = number of **protons**. A neutral atom has equal numbers of protons and electrons.
16. Acids turn blue litmus red; bases turn red litmus blue. A neutral solution at 25°C has pH 7.
17. Rusting of iron needs oxygen and water; paint can help by blocking exposure.
18. **Photosynthesis:** plants use light to produce carbohydrates from carbon dioxide and water; oxygen is released.
19. Respiration releases energy from food in cells; breathing is the movement of air.
20. Xylem transports water and minerals; phloem carries sugars and other organic nutrients.
21. The heart pumps blood; arteries carry blood **away from the heart**, veins towards it—not always oxygen-rich versus oxygen-poor.
22. Kidneys filter blood and help form urine. Neurons carry nerve signals.
23. Vaccines train the immune system; antibiotics treat certain bacterial infections, not viral infections like the common cold.
24. DNA carries genetic information; genes are stretches of DNA involved in producing functional products.
25. Producers capture energy; food chains transfer energy. Protecting biodiversity protects varieties of life.

## Extra 20 lightning facts

26. Density = mass ÷ volume; an object floats if its average density is less than the fluid's, under ordinary conditions.
27. Heat moves spontaneously from a hotter body to a cooler one; temperature is not the same as heat.
28. Celsius boiling point of pure water is 100°C at standard atmospheric pressure; it changes with pressure.
29. A catalyst speeds a reaction without being consumed overall; enzymes are biological catalysts.
30. An atom's nucleus contains protons and, usually, neutrons; hydrogen-1 has no neutron.
31. Isotopes: same proton number, different neutron numbers.
32. Acids + carbonates commonly give carbon dioxide; test with limewater (turns milky).
33. Washing soda = Na₂CO₃·10H₂O; baking soda = NaHCO₃; plaster of Paris = CaSO₄·½H₂O.
34. Liquid metal at room temperature: mercury; liquid non-metal: bromine.
35. Ozone in the stratosphere absorbs much harmful ultraviolet radiation; ground-level ozone is a pollutant.
36. Mitochondria are important sites of aerobic cellular respiration; chloroplasts carry out photosynthesis in plant cells.
37. Red blood cells carry oxygen mainly using haemoglobin; platelets assist clotting.
38. Insulin helps regulate blood glucose; it is produced by the pancreas.
39. A balanced diet includes appropriate quantities of nutrients, fibre and water.
40. The Moon shines mainly by reflecting sunlight; its phases depend on changing viewing geometry.
41. Solar eclipse: Moon between Sun and Earth; lunar eclipse: Earth between Sun and Moon. Never look directly at the Sun.
42. Earth rotates roughly once per day; it revolves around the Sun roughly once per year. Seasons are mainly due to Earth's axial tilt.
43. ISRO: Indian Space Research Organisation; Chandrayaan-3 landed near the Moon's south polar region in 2023.
44. CPU executes instructions; RAM is working memory; phishing tricks people into revealing information.
45. In a fair experiment change one variable, keep relevant conditions the same and repeat measurements.
