## Answer key with explanations

1. **B** — Speed = 60 ÷ 3 = 20 km/h.
2. **C** — Velocity describes speed with direction.
3. **B** — Force is measured in newtons.
4. **A** — The passenger tends to continue moving when the bus slows.
5. **B** — Sound is a mechanical wave that requires a medium.
6. **A** — A smooth surface produces more regular reflection.
7. **B** — A plane-mirror image is virtual, upright and the same size.
8. **A** — A diverging lens helps correct myopia.
9. **B** — I = V/R = 12/4 = 3 A for an ohmic conductor.
10. **C** — Parallel branches share the same potential difference.
11. **A** — 100 W = 0.1 kW; 0.1 × 10 = 1 kWh.
12. **B** — Higher pressure raises the boiling point.
13. **B** — Pressure = force/area; smaller area gives greater pressure.
14. **B** — Evaporation carries energy away from the skin.
15. **A** — Frequency corresponds to pitch.
16. **B** — Atomic number is the proton count.
17. **B** — Isotopes of an element differ in neutron number.
18. **C** — Air is a mixture of gases.
19. **A** — Acids turn blue litmus red.
20. **B** — At 25°C pH 7 is neutral.
21. **C** — Acid + carbonate usually releases carbon dioxide.
22. **A** — Baking soda is sodium hydrogen carbonate.
23. **A** — Plaster of Paris is calcium sulfate hemihydrate.
24. **B** — Zinc coating helps protect iron or steel.
25. **A** — Oxygen and moisture are needed; salt can accelerate corrosion.
26. **C** — Rusting is a chemical change producing iron oxides.
27. **A** — Enzymes catalyse biological reactions.
28. **B** — Mercury is liquid at ordinary room temperature.
29. **A** — Cellular respiration releases energy from nutrients.
30. **A** — Light energy helps plants make carbohydrates from CO₂ and water.
31. **A** — Plants respire day and night; photosynthesis requires light.
32. **B** — Xylem carries water and dissolved minerals.
33. **A** — Phloem transports sugars and other organic substances.

57. **A** — Density = 100/20 = 5 g/cm³.
58. **A** — Vary the amount of water while controlling relevant other conditions.
59. **B** — OTPs are private; verify suspicious requests independently.
