## Explained answer key

1. **C** — Displacement depends only on change in position; distance measures the full path.
2. **A** — For 60 km each: total 120 km in 1+2=3 h; average 40 km/h.
3. **B** — Velocity includes direction, which changes on the circular path.
4. **D** — The wall pushes the person with equal magnitude and opposite direction.
5. **C** — Average force is change in momentum divided by time.
6. **D** — Density = mass/volume = 150/50 = 3 g/cm³.
7. **D** — The ship displaces enough water for buoyancy to balance its weight.
8. **B** — The objects can be at the same temperature but transfer heat at different rates.
9. **D** — 60 W = 0.06 kW, so 0.06 × 5 = 0.3 kWh.
10. **D** — A perpendicular ray travels along the normal.
11. **B** — Frequency stays constant; speed and wavelength change.
12. **A** — Both the direction of travel and the critical angle condition matter.
13. **A** — The sound travels to the reflector and back: 340 × 0.2 / 2.
14. **A** — 1/R = 1/6 + 1/6 = 1/3, so R = 3 Ω.
15. **B** — Neutral means net charge is zero, not absence of charges.
16. **B** — The neutral atom has 11 electrons; Na⁺ has lost one.
17. **C** — The number of protons defines an element; isotopes differ in neutrons.
18. **B** — Mobile ions transport charge in the liquid.
19. **D** — Both are forms of carbon with different structures.
20. **D** — Aluminium oxide can react with acids and bases under suitable conditions.
21. **D** — Iron is more reactive than copper and displaces it from solution.
22. **C** — Plaster of Paris is calcium sulfate hemihydrate; gypsum is the dihydrate.
23. **A** — Catalysts change the reaction pathway and are regenerated overall.
24. **B** — Osmosis is the net movement of water due to a water-potential difference.
25. **D** — Arteries carry blood away from the heart; the pulmonary artery goes to the lungs.
26. **D** — Plant cells respire both in light and darkness.
27. **C** — Bile produced by the liver helps disperse fat; it is not itself a digestive enzyme.
28. **C** — Nephrons filter blood and reabsorb useful substances.
29. **C** — The expected genotypes are TT, Tt, Tt and tt; only tt is recessive.
30. **B** — Dominance concerns expression in a heterozygote, not frequency or value.
31. **B** — The parasite causes malaria; certain female Anopheles mosquitoes transmit it.
32. **B** — Antibiotic resistance involves microbes becoming less susceptible to drugs.
33. **B** — Some persistent pollutants can become more concentrated along food chains.
34. **A** — Magnitude characterises the earthquake; shaking effects vary by location.
35. **A** — Tilt changes solar angle and daylight through the year; hemispheres differ.
36. **D** — Aditya-L1 is an Indian solar observatory mission.
37. **D** — Raman scattering involves energy exchange between light and a material.
38. **B** — A kilowatt-hour equals power multiplied by time.
39. **B** — Changing more than one relevant factor makes causal attribution difficult.
40. **A** — Both can increase because of the weather; correlation is not proof of causation.
41. **D** — Each independent fair coin toss has a 1/2 probability of heads.
42. **C** — R = V/I = 12/3 = 4 Ω for an ohmic resistor.
43. **B** — The dependent variable is the measured response.
44. **B** — Scientific theories explain evidence and are tested and refined.
45. **D** — Gravity remains significant in orbit; shared free fall produces weightlessness.
