## 2. Paris 2024 Olympics: India’s six medals

Paris was the host city in France. India earned one silver and five bronze medals:

| Medal | Athlete or team | Sport / event |
|---|---|---|
| Silver | **Neeraj Chopra** | Men's javelin throw, athletics |
| Bronze | **Manu Bhaker** | Women's 10 m air pistol, shooting |
| Bronze | **Manu Bhaker and Sarabjot Singh** | Mixed-team 10 m air pistol, shooting |
| Bronze | **Swapnil Kusale** | Men's 50 m rifle three positions, shooting |
| Bronze | **India men's team** | Hockey |
| Bronze | **Aman Sehrawat** | Men's freestyle 57 kg, wrestling |

Manu Bhaker appears twice because she won two medals in different events. A team medal counts as one entry in the medal table, not one medal per team member. [Official Indian medal list](https://www.olympics.com/en/news/paris-2024-olympics-india-medals-tally-winners-table)

**World picture:** The United States and China each earned 40 gold medals at Paris 2024; the US earned 126 total medals and China 91. Medal-table rankings commonly compare gold first, then silver and bronze; sorting by total is a different method. [Olympics Paris 2024](https://www.olympics.com/en/paris-2024/profile/china)

## 3. Paris 2024 Paralympics: athletes and events

The **Paralympic Games** are an elite international multi-sport event for athletes with eligible impairments. Sports use classification systems to organise competition; the letters and numbers in event names identify sport-specific classes, not medal positions.

India's Paris 2024 total was **29**: **7 gold, 9 silver and 13 bronze**. A few examples:

- **Avani Lekhara:** gold, women's 10 m air rifle standing SH1, shooting.
- **Sumit Antil:** gold, men's javelin throw F64, para athletics.
- **Harvinder Singh:** gold, men's individual recurve open, archery.
- **Nitesh Kumar:** gold, men's singles SL3, badminton.
- **Preethi Pal:** bronze in both women's 100 m T35 and women's 200 m T35, athletics.
- **Sheetal Devi and Rakesh Kumar:** bronze, mixed-team compound open, archery.

Source: [India's full Paris Paralympic medal list](https://www.indiatoday.in/sports/paralympics/story/india-historic-campaign-record-29-medals-paris-full-list-2595957-2024-09-08). The Olympics and Paralympics are distinct events: never combine their medal totals when asked about just one.

## 4. Winter Olympics and Paralympics 2026

- **Milano Cortina 2026 Winter Olympics:** held in Italy from **6 to 22 February 2026**. Winter sports include skiing, skating and ice hockey. Norway won **18 gold, 12 silver and 11 bronze**, for **41 medals**. [Olympics official page](https://www.olympics.com/en/olympic-games/milano-cortina-2026)
- **Ski mountaineering** made its Olympic debut at Milano Cortina 2026. [Olympics](https://www.olympics.com/en/olympic-games/milano-cortina-2026)
- **Milano Cortina 2026 Winter Paralympics:** held separately, **6–15 March 2026**, also in Italy. [International Paralympic Committee media guide](https://www.paralympic.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/IPC%20Media%20Guide%20-%20Milano%20Cortina%202026%20Paralympic%20Games.pdf)

**Remember:** Milano is Milan in Italian; Cortina refers to Cortina d'Ampezzo. The Olympic and Paralympic winter events were not held on the same dates.

## 5. Asian Games 2026: India

- The **20th Asian Games** took place in **Aichi-Nagoya, Japan**, from **19 September to 4 October 2026**.
- India's total: **21 gold + 27 silver + 37 bronze = 85 medals**.
- India's men and women both won gold in cricket and kabaddi. India's men's hockey team also won gold.
- **Gulveer Singh:** silver in men's 10,000 m and 1,500 m, plus bronze in men's 5,000 m athletics.
- **Vithya Ramraj:** part of the gold-winning women's 4 × 400 m relay; also won mixed 4 × 400 m relay silver and 400 m hurdles bronze.

[India medal list — Olympics](https://www.olympics.com/en/news/asian-games-2026-medal-tally-india-winners-table-list) · [Athletics details — Olympics](https://www.olympics.com/en/news/asian-games-2026-athletics-results-scores-india-schedule)

**Don't mix them up:** The Asian Games bring together athletes from Asia; the Commonwealth Games involve participating Commonwealth nations and territories. Neither is the same competition as the Olympics.

## 6. Commonwealth Games 2026: India

The **Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games** ran from **23 July to 2 August 2026** in Scotland. India finished fourth in the published standings with **39 medals: 13 gold, 17 silver and 9 bronze**. The medal count includes results from the Games' integrated para-sport programme.

Examples:

- **Mirabai Chanu:** gold, women's 48 kg weightlifting.
- **Sharmila Dhankar:** gold, women's shot put F57, para athletics.
- **Dilip Gavit:** gold, men's 100 m T47, para athletics.
- **Neeraj Chopra:** silver, men's javelin throw.
- **Gulveer Singh:** silver, men's 10,000 m, and bronze, men's 5,000 m.

[Official-style Olympics medal list](https://www.olympics.com/en/news/commonwealth-games-2026-india-medals-tally-winners-table-cwg) · [Athletics and para-athletics](https://www.olympics.com/en/news/commonwealth-games-2026-glasgow-athletics-cwg-india-schedule-results-times)

The 2026 programme was smaller than some previous editions and excluded several sports, so raw totals from different editions need context.

## 7. Football and cricket World Cups

### Men's FIFA World Cup 2026 — football

- Co-hosts: **Canada, Mexico and the United States**; **48 teams** competed.
- Final: **Spain 1–0 Argentina after extra time**, on 19 July 2026 in New York New Jersey.
- **Ferran Torres** scored the winning goal. This was Spain's **second men's World Cup title**.
- Spain had also won the **2023 women's FIFA World Cup**. The men's and women's competitions are separate tournaments.

[FIFA final report](https://www.fifa.com/en/tournaments/mens/worldcup/canadamexicousa2026/articles/spain-argentina-final-report-highlights) · [FIFA tournament summary](https://inside.fifa.com/organisation/news/spain-crowned-world-cup-2026-champions-new-york-new-jersey)

### ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026 — cricket

- Hosts: **India and Sri Lanka**; the final was played in **Ahmedabad**.
- India beat **New Zealand by 96 runs** on 8 March 2026.
- It was India's **third men's T20 World Cup title**, after 2007 and 2024, and the first successful defence of a men's T20 World Cup title.
- **Sanju Samson** was player of the tournament; **Jasprit Bumrah** was player of the final.

[ICC final report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/live-india-new-zealand-chase-world-cup-glory) · [ICC team and awards](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/mens-t20-world-cup-2026/news/team-of-the-tournament-announced-for-t20-world-cup-2026)

### ICC Women's Cricket World Cup 2025 — ODI cricket

- India beat **South Africa by 52 runs** in the final at Navi Mumbai on 2 November 2025.
- This was India's **first women's ODI World Cup title**. **Harmanpreet Kaur** captained India.
- **Shafali Verma** was player of the final; **Deepti Sharma** took five wickets in the final.

[ICC final report](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/womens-cricket-worldcup-2025/news/live-india-south-africa-eye-maiden-title-at-cwc25-final) · [ICC on Shafali](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/womens-cricket-worldcup-2025/news/shafali-s-comeback-saga-that-inspired-india-s-world-cup-win)

**Cricket formats:** T20 normally permits up to 20 overs per side; a women's ODI normally permits up to 50 overs per side. Rain and tournament rules can change match conditions. A T20 title and an ODI title are not interchangeable.
