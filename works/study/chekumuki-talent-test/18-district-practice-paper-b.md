## Multiple choice: 1–28

1. When an inflated balloon is released, air rushes backward and the balloon moves forward. The best explanation is: A) conservation of momentum B) light reflection C) osmosis D) magnetic repulsion only.
2. A book rests on a table. The upward table force and downward gravitational force on the book: A) act on the same object and can balance B) form a third-law pair C) are both zero D) act on different planets.
3. A 12 V source is connected to a 4 Ω ohmic resistor. The current is: A) 0.33 A B) 3 A C) 8 A D) 48 A.
4. Two 6 Ω resistors are in series across an ideal 12 V source. The current in the single loop is: A) 1 A B) 2 A C) 4 A D) 12 A.
5. Two identical 6 Ω resistors are in parallel across an ideal 12 V source. Their total current is: A) 1 A B) 2 A C) 4 A D) 12 A.
6. Why can an LED lamp use less power than an incandescent lamp at similar brightness? A) It can convert a larger fraction of electrical energy to visible light B) It makes energy from nothing C) It has no resistance D) Current never flows in it.
7. A fuse breaks a circuit under excessive current because: A) its element heats up and melts B) it increases the battery voltage C) it produces water D) it stores all the charge.
8. A sound echo returns after 0.2 seconds. If sound travels at 340 m/s, the wall is about: A) 17 m B) 34 m C) 68 m D) 340 m.
9. A light ray enters glass from air. At the boundary, which remains unchanged? A) Frequency B) Speed C) Wavelength D) Direction in every case.
10. An astronaut in orbit experiences apparent weightlessness mainly because: A) there is no gravity at all B) the astronaut and spacecraft fall together C) the astronaut has no mass D) the Moon stops attracting Earth.
11. Two atoms have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons. They are: A) isotopes of the same element B) always different elements C) identical in mass D) necessarily oppositely charged.
12. Which can be separated from water by ordinary filtration? A) Insoluble sand B) Dissolved salt C) Dissolved sugar D) All dissolved ions.
13. Adding a small amount of dilute acid to an aqueous sodium hydroxide solution lowers its pH when other relevant conditions are controlled. Why? A) Neutralisation reduces hydroxide ions B) Acid makes hydroxide ions without limit C) A base cannot react with an acid D) pH measures only colour.
14. Which material is best described as a thermosetting plastic? A) Bakelite B) Polythene C) A cotton fibre D) Aluminium.
15. A packet uses nitrogen gas to reduce spoilage. One reason is that nitrogen is relatively unreactive and can: A) reduce oxidation of food B) make oxygen heavier C) turn food into metal D) eliminate every possible cause of spoilage.
16. Which organelle is mainly responsible for photosynthesis in green plant cells? A) Chloroplast B) Nucleus C) Ribosome D) Cell wall.
17. The oxygen released in photosynthesis comes primarily from: A) water B) the sugar produced C) soil nitrogen D) chlorophyll alone.
18. Which structure conducts nerve impulses away from a neuron's cell body? A) Axon B) Root hair C) Xylem D) Alveolus.
19. Which blood component is primarily responsible for carrying oxygen? A) Red blood cells B) Platelets C) White blood cells D) Blood plasma alone.
20. Which of the following is an example of directional plant growth in response to touch? A) A tendril coiling around a support B) A rock falling C) A leaf reflecting light D) Blood clotting.
21. A vaccine and an antiserum differ because: A) vaccines stimulate an immune response while antiserum supplies preformed antibodies B) both are antibiotics C) antiserum makes every infection impossible forever D) vaccines contain no biological information.
22. A farmer notices poor crop yields after repeatedly growing the same crop. Which is a useful response? A) Consider crop rotation, including legumes where appropriate B) Always burn the soil C) Add unlimited pesticide regardless of need D) Remove all organic matter.
23. A solar eclipse is occurring. Which statement is most scientific? A) Follow safe solar viewing guidance; an eclipse does not automatically poison cooked food B) Look directly at the Sun C) The Sun stops emitting light forever D) Every meal becomes poisonous.
24. Which scientist is associated with wireless communication experiments and plant responses? A) Jagadish Chandra Bose B) Srinivasa Ramanujan C) Marie Curie only D) Charles Babbage.
25. A ₹1,000 item is discounted by 50%, and then the reduced price is discounted by 60%. Final price: A) ₹200 B) ₹300 C) ₹500 D) ₹800.
26. In triangle ABC, D lies on AB and E on AC, with DE parallel to BC. If AD = 6 cm, DB = 9 cm and AE = 4 cm, find EC. A) 4 cm B) 6 cm C) 9 cm D) 10 cm.
27. A shopkeeper receives a message claiming an unexpected prize and asking for an advance fee. Best action: A) Verify independently and do not share financial information B) Pay immediately C) Share an OTP D) Send the password to everyone.
28. Which activity helps students' wellbeing as well as teamwork? A) Regular, inclusive sports and games B) Excluding students who are not athletes C) Replacing all learning with a single competition D) Avoiding all physical movement.

## Assertion–reason: 29–32

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** In a simple series circuit, the same current passes through each resistor in steady operation. **Reason:** There is one path for charge flow through the components.
30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first five positive odd numbers is 25. **Reason:** A triangle has three sides.
31. **Assertion:** A 100 W device running for two hours at constant power uses 0.2 kWh. **Reason:** 1 kWh equals 100 Wh.
32. **Assertion:** The pulmonary artery carries blood towards the heart from the lungs. **Reason:** An artery carries blood away from the heart.

## Fill in the blanks: 33–40

33. The SI unit of potential difference is the __________.
34. A gas produced by yeast fermentation that makes dough rise is __________.
35. The upward force exerted by a fluid on an immersed object is called __________ force.
36. The plant tissue that carries water and dissolved minerals from roots is __________.
37. A sum of ₹240 is divided in the ratio 1:3. The larger share is ₹__________.
38. The difference between the highest and lowest values in the set 8, 12, 17, 23 is __________.

---

## Answers and explanations

1 **A:** the escaping gas and balloon gain opposite momentum; external forces can affect the real motion. 2 **A:** both forces act on the book, so they are not a third-law pair. 3 **B:** I = 12/4 = 3 A. 4 **A:** total resistance 12 Ω; I = 12/12 = 1 A. 5 **C:** 2 A in each branch, 4 A total. 6 **A:** LEDs can provide similar light with less electrical power. 7 **A:** excess current heats the designed fuse element until it melts. 8 **B:** 340 × 0.2 / 2 = 34 m, because sound travels out and back. 9 **A:** frequency is unchanged; speed and wavelength change. 10 **B:** both spacecraft and astronaut are in free fall; gravity remains significant.

11 **A:** proton count identifies the element; neutrons distinguish isotopes. 12 **A:** an ordinary filter retains insoluble sand. 13 **A:** acid neutralises hydroxide ions, reducing basicity in this setting. 14 **A:** Bakelite is a thermosetting plastic. 15 **A:** nitrogen can help reduce oxidative spoilage, but it does not prevent every kind. 16 **A:** chloroplasts perform photosynthesis. 17 **A:** oxygen gas originates from splitting water. 18 **A:** the axon carries impulses away from the cell body. 19 **A:** haemoglobin in red blood cells carries oxygen. 20 **A:** the tendril's directional growth is thigmotropism.

21 **A:** antiserum provides antibodies; vaccination stimulates immune responses. Both require appropriate medical guidance. 22 **A:** crop rotation can help manage soil fertility and pests; conditions matter. 23 **A:** protect your eyes and use evidence-based safety guidance. 24 **A:** J. C. Bose studied radio waves and plant responses. 25 **A:** 50% off ₹1,000 leaves ₹500; 60% off ₹500 leaves ₹200. Successive discounts are not added. 26 **B:** similarity gives AD/DB = AE/EC, so 6/9 = 4/EC and EC = 6 cm. 27 **A:** independently verify and never share OTPs. 28 **A:** inclusive physical activity promotes health and teamwork.

29 **A:** one path explains why current is the same in steady operation. 30 **B:** 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + 9 = 25, but the triangle fact is unrelated. 31 **C:** 100 W × 2 h = 200 Wh = 0.2 kWh; 1 kWh is 1,000 Wh, not 100 Wh. 32 **D:** the pulmonary artery takes blood from the heart to the lungs; arteries carry blood away from the heart.

33 **volt (V)**. 34 **carbon dioxide (CO₂)**. 35 **buoyant** (or upthrust). 36 **xylem**. 37 **180:** four parts, each ₹60; three parts = ₹180. 38 **15:** 23 − 8. 39 **18:** (12 + 18 + 24)/3. 40 **water (H₂O)**.
