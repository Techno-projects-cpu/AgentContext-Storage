## What we could and could not verify

The corrected Drive link opened through a page extraction service and returned text from a **Jana Vignana Vedika Chekumuki question paper**, including 40 numbered questions, English and Indic-language text and GSR INFO / apbadi.net marks. The extraction is badly corrupted in places, especially the Indic script, header, diagrams and question numbering around 31–37. It mentions 2022 in some questions, but **the exact year, level and official provenance cannot be confirmed from this extraction alone**. We cannot call it a confirmed district paper, CCMB selection paper or official answer key. The document also contains an apparently separate National Science Day page with a 2015 theme: do not assume that dates the question paper.

**We have not saved an exact copy of the PDF in the repository**: the retrieval provided extracted text, not the original PDF file. This is a source link and analysis, not a facsimile. If you upload the PDF directly, we can preserve the exact file and inspect every page and figure. ## Selected worked checks — **unofficial**, not a full answer key

The numbers below refer to the source extraction, not the new model. Since OCR or the original may be wrong, confirm against the actual PDF before relying on them.

- **Q1: C, statements 3 and 4:** opposing superstition and encouraging scientific temper are the stated options.
- **Q2: D, orange:** aircraft flight recorders are generally bright orange for visibility despite the nickname “black box”.
- **Q3: C, 2:** x ≠ 0 and x + 1/x = 2 imply x² − 2x + 1 = 0, so x = 1; therefore x²⁰²² + 1/x²⁰²² = 2.
- **Q8: B:** if M = n² for a nonnegative integer n, the next square is (n + 1)² = M + 2√M + 1.
- **Q9: D:** rats **may** increase if snakes decline; real food webs and other limiting factors matter.
- **Q10: A only under stated assumptions:** for a uniform coil cut in half at unchanged supply voltage and constant resistivity/temperature, resistance halves and P = V²/R doubles. Real heater temperature changes and the exact time interval affect actual heat; the source's missing assumptions matter.
- **Q12: C:** 2022 = 2 × 3 × 337 = 6 × 337, so 3, 6 and 337 are factors; 672 is not.
- **Q13: A:** the hypothalamus plays a central role in body temperature regulation.
- **Q14: no valid option as extracted:** each listed group contains noise, vacuum, sunlight or light, none of which is matter in the standard school-level sense. Check the PDF/official key; don't invent an answer.
- **Q16: D:** CCMB is in Hyderabad.
- **Q19: C if the table really reads X = (8p, 8n, 8e), Y = (7p, 9n, 8e), Z = (7p, 9n, 7e):** X and Y have mass number 16 and different proton counts, so they are isobars; Y has one extra electron compared with neutral Z, so Y is an ion of Z. English OCR also prints “818” for X's protons while another extracted table shows 8: verify the actual figure.
- **Q22: A:** during banana ripening starch is converted into sugars; exact taste depends on other changes too.
- **Q23: B:** holding mass constant and using W = GMm/R², doubling R makes surface weight W/4 in the simplified spherical model.
- **Q29: D:** centroid G is one-third of the triangle's altitude above BC, so area GBC is one-third of ABC = 20 cm².
- **Q30: C, 480 N average magnitude:** take the initial direction as positive: Δp = 0.150 × (−20 − 12) = −4.8 kg m/s, and |F_avg| = 4.8/0.01 = 480 N. The reversal is essential.
- **Q36: A, 0** if the extracted formula is accurate: x + y = 2c − a − b = −z, so (x + y)² − z² = 0.
- **Q38: D:** comet nuclei contain ice, dust and organic materials; other materials may also be present.
- **Q39: A:** spreading colour demonstrates diffusion, although stirring or convection can also contribute in a real cup.

## Don't blindly trust a plausible letter

- **Q6:** both pancreas and parathyroid are not straightforwardly controlled by pituitary tropic hormones in the way the question suggests. This makes a unique answer questionable; endocrine regulation is more complex. Check wording/key.
- **Q7:** the meniscus depends on liquid–surface adhesion, cohesion and surface tension; curvature in plastic depends on the specific plastic and surface. The simplified option “surface tension” does not establish that all plastic has an upward meniscus.
- **Q24:** sound loudness at equal distances depends on source directivity, room acoustics and orientation; source information is insufficient to prove a unique answer.
- **Q31–35 and Q37:** numbering, graphics and/or English text are too corrupted to reconstruct reliably. **Do not grade these from OCR.**
- **Q40:** pH changes depend on acid consumed, quantities, concentration, surface area, reaction products and other conditions. Without controlled assumptions, ordering intrinsic metal reactivity from one final pH is unsafe.
- Other questions, including old current-affairs items, have not all been independently checked. **This is not a 40/40 answer key and cannot grade your attempt.**

## Training implication

Practise mixed short facts, patterns, real-world reasoning and **a few tougher calculations**, while explicitly checking assumptions. The [new original 40-MCQ model](paper-workout/model-old-paper-style-01.md) follows that broad style, but its difficulty is an approximation, not a validated match. Keep your CCMB research-reasoning preparation separate: this old Chekumuki paper does not reveal the YIP test syllabus.
