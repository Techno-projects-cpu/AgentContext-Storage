# Search for the Chekumuki monthly magazine 🔎

## What we sought

The user suggested that questions in their Chekumuki papers come from JVV's monthly science magazine and asked for the **October 2026 issue**, so they could compare it against their own school and mandal papers. This is a promising hypothesis; **we cannot yet confirm that any particular question came from the magazine**.

## What we found

- JVV Andhra Pradesh identifies a Telugu children's science monthly called **Vidyarthi Chekumuki**: [JVV AP science-fest description](https://jvvap.org/activites/science-samburalu/). This establishes the magazine's existence, not that all test questions are drawn from it or that AP's edition is identical to Telangana's.
- [JVV AP's Chekumuki magazine page](https://jvvap.org/chekumuki/) shows magazine advertisements and lists **8309965083**, **9705333305** and **gsjvvap@gmail.com** as general JVV AP contacts. Ask a parent or teacher before contacting them; they are AP contacts, not verified Telangana circulation or magazine-subscription numbers. Ask about the right regional edition, October 2026 issue, official purchase or access, and whether the test uses the magazine.
- JVV AP also links [Janavignanam](https://janavignanam.com/) as an online science magazine. **Do not assume it is the requested October issue of Vidyarthi Chekumuki**; its contents were not accessible in the page extraction.
- A [user-uploaded Scribd document labelled “చెకుముకి”](https://www.scribd.com/document/735361689/%E0%B0%9A%E0%B1%86%E0%B0%95%E0%B1%81%E0%B0%AE%E0%B1%81%E0%B0%95%E0%B0%BF-Chekumuki-1) has 36 pages and extracted text referring to **2022**, including English snippets about hydrogen and heavy water. Most Telugu extracted text is corrupted. **It is not the requested October 2026 issue; edition, scan, publisher rights and full contents are not independently confirmed.** Scribd may require sign-in or payment; do not pay just to investigate.
- Search of Telugu and English queries did **not produce a verified readable October 2026 issue**. The magazine was not downloaded, stored, analysed or compared; no PDF copy or link to the desired issue is claimed.

## Buying the current issue: follow-up search (12 November 2026)

- The user believes one issue costs **₹20**. We found **no current official single-copy price, stock listing, delivery fee, or purchase link**, so ₹20 is not yet verified. The current calendar issue to ask for is **November 2026**; also request October 2026 for comparison with earlier papers. Ask whether the appropriate Telangana and AP editions differ.
- Best practical lead: the teacher or JVV volunteer who organised the user's school/mandal round may know the **local distributor or school-library copy**. No local shop has been verified.
- [Wanaparthy district government's Telangana Chekumuki festival page](https://wanaparthy.telangana.gov.in/telangana-chekumuki-science-talent-festival/) lists **89191 54196** and **94418 95618** for festival details. These are **festival contacts, not verified magazine sellers**; the page's image URLs indicate 2022, so ask a teacher/parent to verify the numbers remain current and whether they can direct you to a seller.
- [JVV AP's magazine page](https://jvvap.org/chekumuki/) lists **8309965083**, **9705333305**, and **gsjvvap@gmail.com** as general AP contacts, not a verified Telangana distributor. Ask for a single November issue, October back issue, local distribution, total price and whether a regional edition matters.
- An [author's 2023 description](https://ky.linkedin.com/posts/vbsowmya_evaluating-gpt-4-and-chatgpt-on-japanese-activity-7048592402374123520-OLy2) says the magazine was print-only then; this is historical, **not proof of current availability**.
- Before paying, independently verify the recipient with a teacher or the organisation, and do not share OTPs or bank credentials. Don't post your address publicly.

## Best next step

1. Ask your science teacher or school library for **విద్యార్థి చెకుముకి — అక్టోబర్ 2026 సంచిక**, and ask whether the Telangana edition has a different publisher or local distributor. The issue's cover should show month, year and edition.
2. If the school does not have it, have a parent/teacher use the [JVV AP magazine page](https://jvvap.org/chekumuki/) to request the October issue or a Telangana contact; verify availability, price and permission before purchasing or sharing.
3. Upload the PDF or clear photos of cover, contents page and articles (and the official keys if available). Avoid sharing private personal details in screenshots. A paid subscription is not necessary for us to start with a library copy.
4. We can then compare each article to the [school and mandal originals and worked checks](paper-workout/README.md): exact wording, matching concept, issue date, article/page, paper/question and confidence. A topic overlap does **not** prove the magazine caused a question; distinguish exact or distinctive matches from common textbook knowledge. Do not predict district questions from a small number of matches.
