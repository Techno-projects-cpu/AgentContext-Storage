# Easy Maths and Reasoning — No Panic!

You do not need to be a maths Olympiad champion. Build confidence with everyday arithmetic, units and careful reading. Try the example yourself before looking at the steps.

## 1. Fractions and percentages

**Fraction** = part / whole. ½ = 0.5 = 50%; ¼ = 0.25 = 25%; ¾ = 75%.

**Example:** 15 out of 60 questions are maths. What percentage? 15/60 × 100 = **25%**.

**Example:** 20% of 150 = 20/100 × 150 = **30**. Shortcut: 10% is 15, so 20% is 30.

If a ₹200 item gets 10% off, the discount is ₹20 and the price becomes **₹180**. Percentage increase and decrease use the original reference value unless the question specifies otherwise.

## 2. Ratios and proportions

A ratio 2:3 means 2 parts to 3 parts, **5 parts in total**. If 25 students are split 2:3, each part is 25/5 = 5. The groups are **10 and 15**.

If 3 notebooks cost ₹60 at the same price each, one costs ₹20 and 5 cost **₹100**.

## 3. Averages and simple data

Average = sum of values / number of values. Scores 5, 7 and 9: (5+7+9)/3 = **7**. An average is not necessarily the most common value.

From a chart: read the title, units and axes before comparing bars. A graph showing 10 to 20 doubles, but an increase from 10% to 20% is **10 percentage points** and a 100% relative increase.

## 4. Basic equations

Solve x + 7 = 15: subtract 7 from both sides, so **x = 8**. Solve 3x = 21: divide both sides by 3, so **x = 7**. Check by substitution.

## 5. Speed, distance and time

Speed = distance/time. Distance = speed × time. Time = distance/speed.

A cycle travels 60 km in 3 h: speed = 60/3 = **20 km/h**. At constant 20 km/h for 2 h, distance = **40 km**.

Units: 1 km = 1,000 m; 1 h = 3,600 s; 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h. Example: 10 m/s = 36 km/h.

## 6. Science calculations — gentle practice

- Ohm's law: V = IR. V = 12 V, R = 4 Ω → I = 12/4 = **3 A**.
- Power P = VI. V = 6 V, I = 2 A → **12 W**.
- Density = mass/volume. 100 g in 20 cm³ → **5 g/cm³**.
- Energy = power × time. 100 W = 0.1 kW; 0.1 kW × 5 h = **0.5 kWh**.
- Pressure = force/area. 20 N over 2 m² = **10 Pa**.

First write the formula, put in numbers with units, then check whether the answer makes sense.

## 7. Shapes and measurement

- Rectangle area = length × breadth. 5 cm × 3 cm = **15 cm²**; perimeter = 2(5+3) = **16 cm**.
- Triangle area = ½ × base × perpendicular height. Base 8 cm, height 3 cm → **12 cm²**.
- Circle circumference = 2πr; area = πr². Use π ≈ 3.14 if needed; r is radius, not diameter.
- 1 litre = 1,000 millilitres; 1 kg = 1,000 g; 1 m = 100 cm.

## 8. Probability and logic

For equally likely outcomes, probability = favourable outcomes / total outcomes. One fair six-sided die: probability of a 6 = **1/6**. One fair coin: probability of heads = **1/2**. Past independent tosses do not force the next toss to be tails.

Series: 2, 4, 6, 8, __ → **10**, adding 2 each time. Check that a proposed pattern is consistent with all terms; more than one continuation can sometimes be possible.

## 9. Eight confidence boosters — try first

1. 25% of 80 = ?
2. 3/4 of 20 = ?
3. 5 pens cost ₹50; cost of 2 at the same price?
4. Average of 4, 6 and 8?
5. 90 km in 3 h: average speed?
6. 10 V across 5 Ω: current (assume Ohm's law)?
7. Rectangle 4 cm by 6 cm: area?
8. Fair die: chance of an even number?

**Answers:** 1) 20; 2) 15; 3) ₹20; 4) 6; 5) 30 km/h; 6) 2 A; 7) 24 cm²; 8) 3/6 = 1/2 (2, 4, 6).
