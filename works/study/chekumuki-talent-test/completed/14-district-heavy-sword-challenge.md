## 2. How to carry the “heavy sword” safely

1. **Think independently** for a minute. If stuck, write what you know and check units or a simple example.
2. **Check the explanation.** A guessed correct answer is not yet a mastered concept.
3. **Keep a mistake log:** question number → why I chose it → correct idea → one similar example.
4. **Retry later:** return the next day and explain without looking. Repeated recall is more useful than only rereading.
5. **Team round?** Agree on roles and check local rules. One person can explain, another can question assumptions, and another can check units. Listen to one another; do not let speed silence a teammate.
6. **Difficulty is a tool, not a verdict.** These questions deliberately include traps. Take breaks and seek your teacher's help when an explanation is unclear.

## 3. ★ Ten concepts that unlock tricky questions

- **Current is not voltage:** current is charge per second (A); potential difference is energy per charge (V). In a simple DC circuit, power P = VI. [Beginner guide](12-electricity-from-zero.md)
- **Same current or same voltage?** In one series loop, current is the same through components. Parallel branches across the same two points have the same voltage.
- **Balanced forces:** zero net force means zero acceleration, not necessarily zero velocity. An object can move in a straight line at constant speed.
- **Energy and power:** energy is an amount; power is the rate of transfer. 1 kWh is energy, not power.
- **Particles matter:** filtering removes insoluble sand, not dissolved salt. Solid salt's ions are fixed in its lattice; dissolved ions can move and carry charge.
- **Plant life:** photosynthesis requires light, but plants respire day and night. Xylem moves water and minerals; phloem moves sugars.
- **Blood vessels:** arteries take blood away from the heart; pulmonary arteries carry oxygen-poor blood.
- **Experiment:** change one main variable, keep others similar, use a comparison and repeat. A single result or correlation does not automatically establish a cause.
- **Space:** an orbiting astronaut is in free fall, not outside gravity. Seasons mainly arise from axial tilt and Earth's revolution.
- **Maths:** include units. Average speed is total distance ÷ total time, and 30 minutes is 0.5 hour.

## 4. District challenge: 40 original MCQs

**How to attempt:** These are intentionally more reasoning-focused than basic recall. Do them in groups of 10; you can time yourself only after you understand the ideas. Choose **one best answer** and check section 5. A calculator is not needed for the intended arithmetic. Some examples simplify real conditions, as stated.

### Electricity and scientific thinking

1. A 12 V ideal battery is connected to a 6 Ω resistor. What is the current? A) 0.5 A B) 2 A C) 6 A D) 72 A.
2. Two 6 Ω resistors are connected in series to an ideal 12 V battery. The current through each is: A) 1 A B) 2 A C) 4 A D) 12 A.
3. Two 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel across an ideal 12 V source. The total current is: A) 0.5 A B) 1 A C) 2 A D) 4 A.
4. A simple circuit has an open switch. Which can be true? A) Sustained current flows around the broken loop B) The battery has voltage across its terminals but no external loop current C) The battery has no chemical energy D) Resistance must be zero.
5. A 100 W lamp works for 2 hours at constant power. Energy used is: A) 0.2 kWh B) 2 kWh C) 20 kWh D) 200 kWh.
6. A DC device has 3 V across it and carries 2 A. How much power does it transfer? A) 1.5 W B) 5 W C) 6 W D) 9 W.
7. Which is true of current in a simple series loop with a battery and bulb? A) The bulb uses up the charge B) Current is the same through the battery and bulb in steady operation C) Current only flows through one wire D) The bulb must supply more energy than the battery.
8. Which instrument and connection measure the potential difference across a resistor? A) Ammeter in series B) Ammeter across it C) Voltmeter in series D) Voltmeter across it.
9. A student wants to test the effect of fertiliser on similar plants. Which is the best comparison? A) Change fertiliser, species, soil and water B) Change fertiliser while keeping other conditions similar and using several plants in each group C) Measure a single plant only D) Select only the tallest plants afterwards.
10. An observation shows ice-cream sales and drowning incidents both increase in summer. What is justified? A) Ice cream causes drowning B) Drowning causes ice-cream sales C) A seasonal factor may affect both; correlation alone doesn't prove cause D) The two measurements must be identical.
11. In an experiment, a conclusion disagrees with a prediction. What should a good scientist do? A) Hide the result B) Change measurements to match C) Check methods and evidence, and revise the explanation if needed D) Assume the hypothesis is automatically true.
12. Which statement is a testable hypothesis? A) All beautiful ideas are true B) Plants receiving 6 hours of light daily grow taller on average than similar plants receiving 2 hours, under specified conditions C) Science is wonderful D) A plant secretly wishes to grow.

### Physics and everyday life

18. A sound echo returns in 0.2 s. If sound speed is 340 m/s, the reflecting wall is approximately: A) 17 m B) 34 m C) 68 m D) 170 m.

### Chemistry

20. To separate sand from a mixture of sand and dissolved salt in water, which step works first? A) Ordinary filtration B) A magnet in every case C) Sieving out dissolved ions D) Evaporation of sand alone.

## 5. Answers with reasons

### Electricity and thinking

1. **B — 2 A.** Ohm's law gives I = V/R = 12/6. This assumes the resistor behaves ohmically under the stated conditions.
2. **A — 1 A.** Series resistance is 6 + 6 = 12 Ω, so I = 12/12. The same current flows through each component in one loop.
3. **D — 4 A.** Each branch gets 12 V, so each carries 12/6 = 2 A. Add branch currents: 2 + 2 = 4 A.
4. **B.** The battery can maintain a potential difference even when the external path is broken. No sustained current flows around that open loop.
5. **A — 0.2 kWh.** 100 W × 2 h = 200 Wh = 0.2 kWh. A watt is power; a watt-hour measures energy.
6. **C — 6 W.** P = VI = 3 × 2. A watt is a joule per second.
7. **B.** Charge is not used up; the bulb transfers energy. In steady operation the same current flows through each part of one series loop.
8. **D.** A voltmeter is placed across the two points being compared. Never experiment with household mains.
9. **B.** Change one main factor, keep other conditions similar, and use multiple plants to reduce the influence of individual variation.
10. **C.** Both could rise because of summer weather or other factors. The association does not establish that one causes the other.
11. **C.** Unexpected evidence calls for checking methods and revising explanations when justified—not hiding data.
12. **B.** It specifies a measurable comparison under stated conditions; the other choices are opinions or unfalsifiable claims.

### Physics

13. **B.** Velocity includes direction. Turning changes velocity even if speed stays constant.
14. **B.** Both forces act on the book; a third-law pair acts on two different bodies. These two can balance if the book is at rest.
15. **A.** Displacement is change in position, so it is zero after returning to the start. Distance is the full path length.
16. **B.** A floating ship displaces water whose weight balances its own; its overall average density includes enclosed air.
17. **B.** Light's speed and wavelength change on entering glass; its frequency remains the same at the boundary.
18. **B — 34 m.** Total sound travel = 340 × 0.2 = 68 m, but this is the outward and return journey. Divide by two.

### Chemistry

19. **B.** Same proton number means the same element; a different neutron number means different isotopes.
20. **A.** Insoluble sand can be filtered out. The dissolved salt passes through an ordinary filter and can be separated later by another suitable method.
21. **B.** Ions can move in the molten salt and carry charge; in the solid lattice they are not free to move through the solid.
22. **B.** Both sides contain four hydrogen atoms and two oxygen atoms. Balance with coefficients, not by changing chemical formulas.
23. **B.** Oxygen enters from the surroundings. Total mass is conserved when the full closed system is accounted for.
24. **B.** A catalyst offers another reaction pathway and is regenerated overall; it does not create matter or change equilibrium position under the same conditions.

### Biology

25. **A.** An artery carries blood away from the heart. The pulmonary artery is an important oxygen-poor exception.
26. **B.** Plants respire both day and night. Photosynthesis needs light.
27. **B.** Xylem carries water and minerals; phloem transports sugars and other organic substances.
28. **B.** Antibiotics act against certain bacteria, not influenza viruses. Consult a qualified health professional about treatment.
29. **A.** Decomposers break down material and return nutrients. Nutrients cycle; energy flows and is dispersed.
30. **B.** Many traits have contributions from several genes and the environment. Simple single-gene examples are useful but not universal.

### Earth, GK and maths

31. **B.** Earth's axial tilt and revolution change sunlight angle and day length over the year.
32. **B.** The Moon's orbit is tilted, so the Sun, Earth and Moon generally do not align closely enough at full moon.
33. **B.** A geostationary satellite is in an equatorial orbit, travelling with Earth's rotation and matching its rotational period.
34. **A — IMD.** The India Meteorological Department provides weather and climate services.
35. **B — ₹200.** 20% of ₹250 is ₹50; subtract it from the original price.
36. **C — 60.** There are 8 parts, 96/8 = 12 each, so 5 parts = 60.
37. **B — 40 km/h.** Total distance 120 km divided by total time 3 h. Do not simply average the two speeds here.
38. **C — 24 cm².** Area = length × width = 8 × 3; perimeter would be 22 cm.
39. **A — 2/5.** Two blue outcomes out of five equally likely balls.
40. **A — 0.5 kWh.** 30 minutes = 0.5 h; energy = 1 kW × 0.5 h.
