## Multiple choice: 1–28

1. A bus starts suddenly. A standing passenger tends to fall backwards because: A) gravity stops B) the feet move with the bus while the upper body tends to remain at rest C) mass disappears D) the air pushes only backwards.
2. A 6 V battery is connected to an ohmic 3 Ω resistor. Current is: A) 0.5 A B) 2 A C) 3 A D) 18 A.
3. Two lamps are connected in parallel. If one branch opens, the other can remain lit because: A) it has a separate conducting path B) both lamps must have equal power C) resistance is zero D) the battery has no voltage.
4. A 100 W lamp runs for 3 hours at constant power. Energy used is: A) 0.03 kWh B) 0.3 kWh C) 3 kWh D) 30 kWh.
5. A pressure cooker cooks food faster mainly because: A) pressure lowers boiling temperature B) higher pressure raises water's boiling temperature C) water boils without heat D) the lid produces food energy.
6. A steel ship floats because: A) steel has no mass B) its overall average density including enclosed air can be lower than water's C) buoyancy works only on wood D) the sea has no gravity.
7. A shiny polished surface mainly increases: A) regular reflection B) nuclear fission C) sound frequency D) osmosis.
8. A prism disperses white light mainly because: A) light has no energy B) its refractive index varies with wavelength C) all colours travel at identical speeds in glass D) the prism makes light from darkness.
9. The upthrust on an immersed object is equal to the: A) object's volume B) weight of fluid displaced C) object's density D) pressure at the top alone.
10. During an earthquake inside a classroom, the safest immediate action among these is: A) run to a window B) take cover under a sturdy desk, protect your head and hold on C) rush down stairs while shaking D) use a lift.
11. Which substance can conduct electricity when dissolved in water because its ions can move? A) Sodium chloride B) Sugar C) Pure oil D) Wax.
12. A mixture contains sand and dissolved salt in water. What removes the sand first? A) Filtration B) Ordinary filtration removes the salt only C) A magnet always attracts both D) Freezing the sand at room temperature.
13. Which formula represents plaster of Paris? A) CaSO₄·½H₂O B) CaSO₄·2H₂O C) NaCl D) CO₂.
14. Rusting generally requires: A) oxygen and moisture B) nitrogen only C) sunlight only D) a vacuum.
15. Which household substance is used to help cakes rise? A) Sodium bicarbonate B) Sodium chloride only C) Iron filings D) Sand.
16. A farmer rotates cereals with leguminous crops. A useful reason is: A) legumes can help improve soil nitrogen through associated bacteria B) legumes eliminate the need for all care C) every soil becomes identical D) roots create nitrogen atoms.
17. Water enters dry raisins placed in water primarily by: A) osmosis B) combustion C) reflection D) nuclear fusion.
18. Which tissue transports sugars from leaves to other parts of a plant? A) Xylem B) Phloem C) Epidermis only D) Root hairs only.
19. A student touches a touch-me-not plant and its leaves fold. This demonstrates: A) response to a stimulus B) a human nervous system C) reproduction D) loss of gravity.
20. A person has a knee-jerk response after a tendon is tapped. It is best described as: A) a spinal somatic reflex B) a planned voluntary action C) digestion D) photosynthesis.
21. Which vessel carries oxygen-poor blood from the heart to the lungs? A) Pulmonary artery B) Pulmonary vein C) Aorta D) Vena cava.
22. Which statement is correct? A) Plants respire only in sunlight B) Plants respire day and night C) Plants never need oxygen D) Respiration and photosynthesis are identical.
23. A message says you won a prize but must pay a fee immediately. You should: A) pay quickly B) share bank passwords C) independently verify and tell a trusted adult D) forward private details to strangers.
24. Who is associated with India's space programme? A) Vikram Sarabhai B) Gregor Mendel only C) Euclid only D) William Shakespeare.
25. A shop offers 20% off an item marked ₹500. The price after discount is: A) ₹100 B) ₹400 C) ₹480 D) ₹600.
26. A student's scores in four tests are 10, 14, 16 and 20. The average is: A) 12 B) 15 C) 16 D) 20.
27. A community restricts a student from school during menstruation. The most appropriate response is to: A) support exclusion B) provide accurate health information and support inclusion C) shame the student D) claim menstruation is contagious.
28. Which statement best describes science? A) Claims never need testing B) Evidence, careful observation and revision of explanations matter C) A popular belief is always correct D) A single experiment proves every hypothesis forever.

## Assertion–reason: 29–32

Use these options for **each** question:

A) Both assertion and reason are true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both are true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion is true, but reason is false.
D) Assertion is false, but reason is true.

29. **Assertion:** A dry raisin generally swells when placed in water. **Reason:** Water enters its cells through selectively permeable membranes by osmosis.
30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first four positive odd numbers is 16. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.
31. **Assertion:** A plane mirror forms a virtual image. **Reason:** Light rays actually meet behind the mirror to form that image.
32. **Assertion:** Sound can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Fill in the blanks: 33–40

33. One ampere is one __________ of charge passing a point per second.
34. The rigid outer structure that helps a plant cell maintain its shape is the cell __________.
35. Green plants release oxygen in photosynthesis mainly from the splitting of __________ molecules.
36. The first words of the Preamble to India's Constitution are “We, the __________ of India”.
37. A 2 kW heater running for 30 minutes at constant power uses __________ kWh.
38. A footballer kicks a ball; the ball pushes back on the foot with an equal and opposite force. This illustrates Newton's __________ law.
39. The pressure exerted by a force equals force divided by __________.
40. Five test scores have an average of 18. Four scores are 12, 15, 20 and 25. The fifth score is __________.

## Answers and explanations

1 **B:** inertia resists a change in motion. 2 **B:** I = V/R = 6/3 = 2 A. 3 **A:** each branch has its own path across the source. 4 **B:** 100 × 3 = 300 Wh = 0.3 kWh. 5 **B:** increased pressure raises boiling temperature. 6 **B:** a floating ship displaces water whose weight balances its own. 7 **A:** a smoother surface gives more regular reflection. 8 **B:** different wavelengths refract by different amounts. 9 **B:** Archimedes' principle. 10 **B:** drop, cover and hold on; follow local safety guidance.

11 **A:** dissolved sodium chloride provides mobile ions. 12 **A:** filtration removes insoluble sand, not dissolved salt. 13 **A:** gypsum is CaSO₄·2H₂O. 14 **A:** ordinary rusting requires oxygen and moisture. 15 **A:** sodium bicarbonate releases gas in suitable baking reactions. 16 **A:** symbiotic bacteria can fix nitrogen; soil still needs proper management. 17 **A:** water enters through selectively permeable membranes. 18 **B:** phloem carries sugars. 19 **A:** plants respond to stimuli without a human nervous system. 20 **A:** knee-jerk is a spinal somatic reflex.

21 **A:** arteries carry blood away from the heart; the pulmonary artery is an oxygen-poor exception. 22 **B:** plants respire day and night. 23 **C:** avoid payment or sharing information; verify independently. 24 **A:** Sarabhai was a leader in India's space programme. 25 **B:** 20% of 500 is 100; 500 − 100 = ₹400. 26 **B:** 60/4 = 15. 27 **B:** menstruation is a normal biological process, not a reason for exclusion. 28 **B:** scientific explanations are tested against evidence.

29 **A:** both are true and osmosis explains the swelling. 30 **B:** 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 = 16; Earth's revolution is true but unrelated. 31 **C:** the image is virtual because reflected rays appear to come from behind the mirror; they do not actually meet there. 32 **D:** sound cannot travel through a vacuum because it needs a medium.

33 **coulomb**: 1 A = 1 C/s. 34 **wall**, mainly cellulose in plant cells. 35 **water (H₂O)**. 36 **people**. 37 **1 kWh**: 2 × 0.5. 38 **third**: the forces act on different objects. 39 **area**. 40 **18**: total = 5 × 18 = 90; four scores sum to 72; 90 − 72 = 18.
