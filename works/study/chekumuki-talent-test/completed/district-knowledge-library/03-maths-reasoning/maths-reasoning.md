# Friendly maths and reasoning ➗🧠

Write one line for the rule, substitute numbers and check units. All examples are original practice—not a claim about the official difficulty.

## Numbers and percentages

1. **Fraction:** 3/4 of 80 = 80 ÷ 4 × 3 = 60.
2. **Decimal:** 0.25 = 25/100 = 1/4 = 25%.
3. **Percent:** 10% of 250 = 25. 20% of 250 = 50.
4. **Increase trap:** 20 → 40 means increase = 20. Percentage increase = 20/20 × 100 = 100%. The final 40 is 200% **of** 20.
5. **Decrease:** 80 → 60 means decrease 20/80 × 100 = 25%.
6. **Discount:** ₹600 at 10% off → ₹60 discount → pay ₹540.
7. **Profit:** buy ₹80, sell ₹100 → profit ₹20; profit % = 20/80 × 100 = 25%, based on cost price.
8. **Ratio:** divide 35 in 2:3 → 5 parts, 7 each → 14 and 21.
9. **Mean:** (5 + 7 + 9 + 11)/4 = 8.
10. **Probability:** a fair die has three even outcomes: 2, 4, 6. Probability = 3/6 = 1/2. A fair coin gives heads with probability 1/2.

## Measurement

11. **Speed:** 150 km in 3 h = 50 km/h. Time = distance/speed: 120 km at a constant 40 km/h takes 3 h.
12. **Average speed:** 60 km in 2 h plus 30 km in 1 h → total 90 km / 3 h = 30 km/h; don't average stage speeds blindly.
13. **Rectangle:** 8 cm × 5 cm → area 40 cm², perimeter 2(8+5) = 26 cm.
14. **Square:** side 4 cm → area 16 cm², perimeter 16 cm. Same numeric value here is coincidence; units differ.
15. **Triangle:** area = 1/2 × base × perpendicular height. Base 10 cm and height 4 cm → 20 cm².
16. **Conversion:** 2.5 km = 2500 m; 750 mL = 0.75 L; 90 min = 1.5 h.
17. **Electricity:** 100 W for 3 h = 300 Wh = 0.3 kWh; watt is power, kilowatt-hour is energy.
18. **Echo:** sound speed 340 m/s, time there-and-back 2 s → path 680 m, reflecting wall 340 m away.

## Reasoning and graphs

19. **Pattern:** 3, 6, 12, 24 → 48 if the rule is doubling. Short sequences can support more than one rule, so explain it.
20. **Odd one out:** copper, iron, aluminium, wood → wood is not a metal. State the property, not merely the item.
21. **Classification:** all squares are rectangles; not all rectangles are squares. The converse does not follow automatically.
22. **Direction:** facing north, turn right → east; turn right again → south. Sketch arrows if unsure.
23. **Data reading:** find the title, axis labels, units and scale. A truncated vertical axis can exaggerate differences visually.
24. **Fair comparison:** test whether stirring changes dissolving speed using equal sugar and equal water at the same temperature. Change stirring, measure time and repeat.
25. **Correlation:** two things changing together doesn't establish that one caused the other. Consider a third factor, such as hot weather affecting both ice-cream sales and cooling demand.
26. **Evidence:** one observation is a starting point, not necessarily proof of a universal rule. Repeat, check measurement and seek alternative explanations.

### Mini-check

- 25% of 120? **30**. A 60 W bulb on for 5 h? **0.3 kWh**. Increase from 20 to 40? **100%**. A die result greater than 4? **2/6 = 1/3**, assuming a fair die.
