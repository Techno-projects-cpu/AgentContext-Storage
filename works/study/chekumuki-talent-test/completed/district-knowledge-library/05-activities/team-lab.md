# Activity + experiment + quiz relay 🤝🔬

These are **practice ideas**, not a description of the actual district tasks or marking. Ask your teacher what equipment, rules and team size apply. Physical activities require teacher supervision and local safety rules. Never use mains electricity, flames, unknown chemicals, bodily samples or cultured microbes; never look directly at the Sun.

## Scientific explanation template

1. **Question:** What do we want to find out?
2. **Prediction:** What might happen, and why? It is not a result.
3. **Change:** Which one factor are we deliberately varying? (independent variable)
4. **Measure:** What outcome will we observe or measure? (dependent variable)
5. **Fair test:** Which relevant conditions stay similar? Repeat where possible.
6. **Report:** State the real observations, uncertainty, and one improvement. Don't invent results.

### Practice 1: dissolving speed

Two equal cups, same water volume and temperature, same amount and grain size of sugar. Stir one consistently and leave the other still. Time until no sugar is visible; repeat. **Change:** stirring. **Measure:** time. This tests rate, not necessarily how much will ultimately dissolve. Do not taste the samples.

### Practice 2: absorption comparison

Cut equal areas of two paper-towel brands. Dip for equal time, let drip equally and measure mass gain with a scale if available. Repeat with fresh pieces. The products differ in more than one material property, so a difference does not isolate a single cause.

### Practice 3: shadow observation

A teacher places a stick vertically outdoors and records its shadow tip at different times, keeping the stick fixed. Record time, length and direction; weather and measurement technique affect results. Earth's rotation changes the Sun's apparent position. Never look directly at the Sun.

### Practice 4: circuit on paper

Sketch a battery, lamp and switch in one loop. A closed switch allows current in a working circuit; an open switch interrupts it. Compare with a diagram with two parallel lamp branches. If a teacher provides a low-voltage commercial kit, follow its instructions, avoid hot/damaged batteries and never short the terminals. A diagram alone is enough to practise.

### Practice 5: comparing paper shapes

With a teacher, drop two paper shapes from the same safe, reachable height. Time repeated falls. Same material and mass help; changing shape can also change area and orientation, so don't claim a single cause without controlling those factors. No standing on furniture or sharp tools.

## 10-minute quiz relay

- **2 min:** A asks 5 author/song or grammar questions; B answers; C confirms using the language guide.
- **3 min:** Rotate. B reads 5 three-picture descriptions from [Connection Lab](../04-picture-connections/connection-lab.md); C explains all clues; A checks.
- **3 min:** Rotate. C presents a fair-test question; A names the changed factor and measured outcome; B identifies two controls.
- **2 min:** Each person explains one error in a sentence. Listen and share time.
