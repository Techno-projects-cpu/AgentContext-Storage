## Time-based route

- **30 minutes:** read the first 10 science cards, 10 Telugu pairs, and 5 picture connections. Explain 3 answers aloud.
- **One night:** scan science and Telugu, take Paper A, correct your mistakes, then practise 10 picture clues. Sleep.
- **With teammates:** use the quiz relay in 05. Rotate who names clues, proposes a connection and checks why all pictures fit.
