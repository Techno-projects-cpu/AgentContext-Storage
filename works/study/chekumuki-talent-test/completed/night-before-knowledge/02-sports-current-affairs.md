## Olympics and Paralympics: don't mix them up

- **Olympics:** international multi-sport games; summer and winter editions cover different sports.
- **Paralympics:** elite competitions for eligible athletes with disabilities; classification groups athletes according to sport-specific rules. It is not the same as the Special Olympics.
- **Olympiad numbering vs actual date:** Tokyo **2020** was held in **2021** after postponement.
- Olympic rings represent the union of the five inhabited continental regions and athletes meeting; the colours are not an official one-colour-per-continent code.

- **Sheetal Devi and Rakesh Kumar:** bronze, mixed-team compound open archery.

## ★ Cricket: identify format and year

- **2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup:** India won the final against South Africa. The event was hosted by the West Indies and USA.
- **2025 ICC Women's Cricket World Cup:** India beat South Africa in the final to win its first **women's ODI World Cup** title; Harmanpreet Kaur captained India.
- **2026 ICC Men's T20 World Cup:** India beat New Zealand in the final in Ahmedabad. The ICC describes it as India's third men's T20 World Cup title, after 2007 and 2024.
- ★ Trap: 2025 **women's ODI** and 2026 **men's T20** are separate tournaments; don't mix opponents or formats.

## Chess and other terms

- **Gukesh D** defeated Ding Liren in Singapore in 2024 to become the youngest undisputed world chess champion at 18. This describes the **2024 match**, not the result of a later match.
- FIDE is the international chess federation; a **Chess Olympiad** is a team event, not the Olympic Games.

## ★ 2026 Nobel prizes: science and peace

- **Physics:** Francis Halzen — contributions to the **IceCube Neutrino Observatory** and discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin.
- **Chemistry:** Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai — non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis.
- **Physiology or Medicine:** Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel — discoveries concerning light-gated ion channels and **optogenetics**.
- **Peace:** Navanethem “Navi” Pillay — efforts to promote peace and international law.
- In plain words: **neutrinos** are very weakly interacting particles; **optogenetics** uses light-sensitive components to control selected cells in research; some molecules have different **mirror-image forms**.
- ★ Trap: your 2024 school paper asks about **Hopfield and Hinton and the 2024 Physics Nobel** for work enabling machine learning with artificial neural networks. Do not substitute the 2026 winners into that 2024 question.
