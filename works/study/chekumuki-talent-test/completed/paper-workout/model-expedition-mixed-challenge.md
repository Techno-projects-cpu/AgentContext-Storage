# Expedition Paper B — Old-paper mixed challenge

**Original unofficial practice**, inspired by the readable portions of the user-linked [2024 school-level paper](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1u1OVsz5Ru0zqW1r_5ygieDYA72iFRFoy/view) and [older mixed paper](../19-old-paper-link-analysis.md). These are not official papers or verified district rules. Forty multiple-choice questions, one point each, no penalty **for practice**. Suggested time: 60 minutes. The 2024 paper shows 60 minutes; the older paper's extracted time is unreadable. Some real source questions use pictures; this model uses written descriptions instead. Covers related ideas and some familiar topics in new questions; not a guarantee of identical difficulty. Do not try hazardous experiments. [Workout index](README.md)

## Questions

1. A student claims a bracelet cures all illnesses. What is the best first response?
   A) Share bank details to buy it  B) Stop prescribed treatment  C) Accept because a friend said so  D) Seek reliable evidence and advice from a qualified health professional

2. An aircraft's so-called black box is commonly painted orange mainly to:
   A) make it invisible  B) make it easier to find  C) stop a crash  D) absorb all sound

3. If x + 1/x = 2 for a nonzero real x, what is x³ + 1/x³?
   A) 6  B) 3  C) 2  D) 0

4. If M = 36 is a square number, what is the next square number?
   A) 49  B) 37  C) 72  D) 42

5. Which is a factor of 2022?
   A) 672  B) 337  C) 100  D) 11

6. In a food chain grain → rat → snake → eagle, a fall in snakes may initially cause:
   A) all grain to increase immediately  B) energy to disappear  C) eagles to become plants  D) more rats, other factors being equal

7. Why do a glass surface and water form a curved meniscus?
   A) Water has no molecules  B) Adhesion, cohesion and surface tension influence the liquid surface  C) Glass emits sound  D) Only gravity changes the colour of water

8. A uniform resistor is cut into two equal lengths. One half is connected across the same ideal voltage. With constant resistivity, power becomes:
   A) unchanged  B) half the original  C) twice the original  D) one quarter the original

9. Which brain region is central to temperature regulation?
   A) Retina  B) Cerebellum  C) Skin only  D) Hypothalamus

10. Which set consists entirely of matter?
   A) Sound, water and glass  B) Light, wood and air  C) Air, water and wood  D) Vacuum, light and sand

11. Which metal reacts strongly with cold water and requires trained handling?
   A) Gold  B) Sodium  C) Silver  D) Copper

12. A neutral atom has 8 protons and 8 electrons. If it gains an electron, its net charge becomes:
   A) −1  B) 0  C) +1  D) +8

13. X has 6 protons and 8 neutrons; Y has 7 protons and 7 neutrons. They are:
   A) isobars  B) identical atoms  C) isotopes of the same element  D) both carbon atoms

14. What usually helps a green banana become sweeter on ripening?
   A) Conversion of oxygen into metals  B) Photosynthesis in the kitchen produces salt  C) Conversion of starch into sugars  D) Loss of all carbon atoms

15. If a planet's mass stays fixed and its radius becomes three times as large, surface gravitational force on the same object becomes:
   A) three times  B) one ninth  C) one third  D) nine times

16. Which example involves diffusion?
   A) A magnet attracting iron  B) A stone falling  C) Water freezing into ice  D) Food colouring spreading through water

17. A triangle has area 45 cm² and centroid G. What is the area of the triangle formed by G and one whole side?
   A) 22.5 cm²  B) 5 cm²  C) 15 cm²  D) 30 cm²

18. A 0.1 kg ball moving at 4 m/s hits a wall and rebounds at 6 m/s. What is the magnitude of its momentum change?
   A) 0.4 kg·m/s  B) 10 kg·m/s  C) 0.2 kg·m/s  D) 1 kg·m/s

19. If the momentum change in the previous question takes 0.02 s, the magnitude of average force is:
   A) 5 N  B) 20 N  C) 100 N  D) 50 N

20. Which optical device can form a real image of a suitably placed real object?
   A) Concave lens alone  B) Plane mirror alone  C) Concave mirror  D) Convex mirror alone

21. Which material is a fungus?
   A) Fern  B) Moss  C) Mushroom  D) Grass

22. Which structure controls what enters or leaves a cell?
   A) Stomach  B) Cell membrane  C) Cell wall alone  D) Bone

23. Which statement about a comet is reasonable?
   A) It is made only of radio waves  B) It is always a burning ball of fire  C) It has no material nucleus  D) Its nucleus contains ice and dust

24. Which one is an effect of higher temperature on many enzymes beyond their useful range?
   A) They turn into light  B) Their structure may be disrupted and activity may fall  C) They create energy from nothing  D) They always work faster forever

25. What is the percentage increase from 30 to 45?
   A) 50%  B) 150%  C) 15%  D) 30%

26. What is the mean of 10, 14 and 18?
   A) 14  B) 12  C) 15  D) 42

27. Which evidence most strongly suggests a medicine works?
   A) A single unverified social media message  B) A claim that everyone already agrees  C) Its bright packaging  D) A properly controlled study with reliable measurements

28. Which blood vessel carries blood away from the heart towards the lungs?
   A) A leaf vein  B) Pulmonary artery  C) Pulmonary vein  D) A vein from the legs

29. What is 25% of 240?
   A) 25  B) 80  C) 60  D) 40

30. A shop offers 20% off ₹500 and then 10% off the reduced price. Final price:
   A) ₹450  B) ₹360  C) ₹400  D) ₹350

31. Match P xylem, Q phloem, R stomata with 1 gas exchange, 2 water transport, 3 sugar transport.
   A) P–2, Q–3, R–1  B) P–1, Q–2, R–3  C) P–3, Q–1, R–2  D) P–2, Q–1, R–3

32. Which radiation can reveal fluorescent features on some currency notes?
   A) Ultraviolet  B) Water waves  C) Sound  D) Seismic waves

33. Which Indian institution is located in Hyderabad and studies cellular and molecular biology?
   A) ISRO headquarters  B) UN headquarters  C) CSIR-CCMB  D) RBI headquarters

34. If a square has side 5 cm, its area is:
   A) 20 cm²  B) 10 cm²  C) 25 cm²  D) 125 cm²

35. Which group provides a better baseline for testing fertiliser?
   A) A different species given different soil and water  B) A group whose results are ignored  C) Only one plant with fertiliser  D) Similar plants without fertiliser under otherwise comparable conditions

36. Which of these is a renewable energy source?
   A) Coal  B) Sunlight  C) Natural gas  D) Petroleum

37. A fair die is rolled once. Probability of an even number:
   A) 1/6  B) 2/3  C) 1/3  D) 1/2

38. An LED and an incandescent lamp give similar brightness. Why might the LED use less power?
   A) It generally converts more electrical energy into visible light  B) It violates conservation of energy  C) It requires no energy  D) It destroys electrical charge

39. A population of bacteria becomes resistant to an antibiotic. What has become resistant?
   A) Bacteria in the population  B) The glass container  C) All viruses  D) The patient's body to all medicines

40. A plant cell is placed in a concentrated salt solution. What can happen to the cell contents?
   A) They can shrink away from the wall as water leaves  B) Water must enter forever  C) The cell becomes a metal  D) The cell wall becomes DNA

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **D — Seek reliable evidence and advice from a qualified health professional.** Anecdotes do not establish a cure; changing medical treatment requires qualified advice.
2. **B — make it easier to find.** A bright colour helps recovery crews locate the recorder.
3. **C — 2.** x + 1/x = 2 gives (x − 1)² = 0, so x = 1 and the sum is 2.
4. **A — 49.** 36 = 6² and the next square is 7² = 49.
5. **B — 337.** 2022 = 6 × 337, so 337 is a factor.
6. **D — more rats, other factors being equal.** Reduced predation may allow rats to increase; other ecological effects matter.
7. **B — Adhesion, cohesion and surface tension influence the liquid surface.** The balance of adhesive and cohesive forces affects the meniscus and depends on the surfaces involved.
8. **C — twice the original.** Half the resistance gives twice the power at fixed voltage, P = V²/R, under the stated assumptions.
9. **D — Hypothalamus.** The hypothalamus plays an important role in maintaining body temperature.
10. **C — Air, water and wood.** Matter has mass and occupies space; light, sound and a vacuum are not matter.
11. **B — Sodium.** Sodium reacts vigorously; never attempt the reaction at home.
12. **A — −1.** Adding one electron gives one more negative charge than positive charges.
13. **A — isobars.** Both have mass number 14 but different atomic numbers, making them isobars.
14. **C — Conversion of starch into sugars.** Ripening converts stored starch into sugars among other changes.
15. **B — one ninth.** At the surface F = GMm/R², so tripling R divides force by 9 in the simplified model.
16. **D — Food colouring spreading through water.** Particles spread by random motion; convection can also contribute in actual water.
17. **C — 15 cm².** The centroid is one third of the altitude from the base, so the area is one third of 45.
18. **D — 1 kg·m/s.** Choosing the initial direction positive: Δp = 0.1 × (−6 − 4) = −1 kg·m/s.
19. **D — 50 N.** Magnitude of average force is magnitude of Δp/Δt = 1/0.02 = 50 N.
20. **C — Concave mirror.** A concave mirror can converge reflected rays to form a real image for a real object beyond its focal point.
21. **C — Mushroom.** A mushroom is the fruiting body of certain fungi.
22. **B — Cell membrane.** The cell membrane is selectively permeable.
23. **D — Its nucleus contains ice and dust.** Comet nuclei contain ices and dust and may contain organic compounds.
24. **B — Their structure may be disrupted and activity may fall.** Excessive heat can denature many enzymes; conditions differ by enzyme.
25. **A — 50%.** The increase is 15; 15/30 × 100 = 50%.
26. **A — 14.** (10 + 14 + 18)/3 = 14.
27. **D — A properly controlled study with reliable measurements.** A well-designed controlled study is stronger evidence than an unsupported claim.
28. **B — Pulmonary artery.** Arteries carry blood away from the heart; pulmonary arteries go to the lungs.
29. **C — 60.** 25% is one quarter; 240/4 = 60.
30. **B — ₹360.** ₹500 × 0.8 × 0.9 = ₹360.
31. **A — P–2, Q–3, R–1.** Xylem transports water; phloem sugars; stomata enable gas exchange.
32. **A — Ultraviolet.** Some security inks fluoresce under ultraviolet light; avoid exposing eyes or skin.
33. **C — CSIR-CCMB.** CCMB is the Centre for Cellular and Molecular Biology in Hyderabad.
34. **C — 25 cm².** Area = side × side = 5 × 5 = 25 cm².
35. **D — Similar plants without fertiliser under otherwise comparable conditions.** A suitable comparison helps isolate fertiliser's effect; use replicates and comparable conditions.
36. **B — Sunlight.** Solar energy is replenished naturally on human timescales.
37. **D — 1/2.** The favourable outcomes 2, 4 and 6 make 3/6 = 1/2.
38. **A — It generally converts more electrical energy into visible light.** Greater light-producing efficiency can mean less power for similar brightness.
39. **A — Bacteria in the population.** Resistance is a property of microorganisms; it can spread and evolve.
40. **A — They can shrink away from the wall as water leaves.** Water can leave by osmosis, causing plasmolysis in a suitable solution.
