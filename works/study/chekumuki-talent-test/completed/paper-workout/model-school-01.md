# School-style model paper 1 — calibrated practice

**New unofficial practice**, based on the photographed school paper, not a past or official paper. Suggested: 60 minutes; 40 questions, 1 mark each, no negative marking, matching the photographed school instructions only. Answer on a separate sheet; explanations follow Q40. The actual district format may differ. Some graphics are described in text. [Analysis and original photos](paper-analysis-calibration.md)

## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. A person claims that a charm alone cured an illness. Which response best shows scientific temper? A) Check evidence and seek medical advice B) Accept every claim C) Stop all prescribed treatment D) Forward the claim without checking.
2. Two positive integers have LCM 60 and GCD 5. One is 15. What is the other? A) 15 B) 10 C) 25. D) 20
3. A person uses lime juice to remove some chalky deposits. Which component of lime juice helps react with the deposit? A) Metal B) Oil. C) Acid D) Starch
4. Who led the team associated with the Green Revolution in India? A) Vikram Sarabhai B) C. V. Raman. C) Homi Bhabha D) M. S. Swaminathan
5. A shop's sales increased from 20 units in January to 30 units in April. What was the percentage increase? A) 10% B) 150%. C) 25% D) 50%
6. Why does a runner breathe faster after exercise? A) Lungs produce blood B) The heart makes oxygen C) Muscles need more oxygen and produce more carbon dioxide D) Breathing prevents all sweating.
7. A pond receives untreated sewage. Fish may die mainly because: A) decomposers use up dissolved oxygen B) sewage always adds unlimited oxygen. C) fish turn into plants D) the water becomes pure
8. The roots of x² − 7x + 12 = 0 are: A) 2 and 6 B) 5 and 7. C) 3 and 4 D) 1 and 12
9. Vegetable peels and dry leaves from a school garden are best managed by: A) mixing with glass shards B) composting C) burning plastic with them. D) throwing into a pond
10. Which organelle-function pair is correct? A) Chloroplast—pumping blood B) Mitochondrion—making bones C) Ribosome—filtering urine. D) Nucleus—cell control
11. A sharp knife cuts more easily than a blunt one with the same force because its edge: A) makes force zero. B) exerts greater pressure on a smaller area C) has no mass D) reduces gravity
12. Which statement is correct? A) All plastics conduct like copper B) Glass is a gas at room temperature. C) Graphite conducts electricity D) Rubber is a metal
13. Arrange these from smallest to largest biological level: P organ, Q cell, R tissue, S organ system. A) P–Q–R–S B) S–P–R–Q. C) R–S–Q–P D) Q–R–P–S
14. A seat belt helps protect a passenger when a car stops suddenly because it: A) makes the car massless. B) applies a force that slows the passenger with the car C) prevents gravity D) removes inertia
15. A simple pinhole camera forms an image of a candle on a screen. The image is generally: A) formed without light B) upright and always enlarged C) inverted D) a sound wave.
16. Which combination provides strong evidence for evolutionary relationships? A) Weather on one day. B) Fossils, anatomy and DNA comparisons C) Animal names alone D) Similar favourite foods of researchers
17. A cold metal spoon and a wooden spoon are in the same room for a long time. Metal may feel colder mainly because: A) it transfers heat from the hand faster B) metal has no particles. C) wood creates heat D) its temperature must be below zero
18. Which mission made India's first soft landing near the Moon's south polar region in 2023? A) Mangalyaan B) Chandrayaan-3 C) Aditya-L1 D) AstroSat.
19. A family has a grandfather, his son and his grandson. How can there be two fathers and two sons but only three people? A) It is impossible. B) A person must be counted twice as a separate person C) The middle person is both a father and a son D) One is unrelated
20. Which phenomenon most directly shows that light can travel in a straight line? A) Rusting of iron B) Fermentation. C) Evaporation D) Formation of a shadow
21. Two identical bulbs are in series with an ideal battery. If one bulb's filament breaks, the other bulb: A) goes out B) stays lit with the same current C) becomes a battery D) gains a new circuit path automatically.
22. Match P xylem, Q phloem, R stomata with 1 gas exchange, 2 water transport, 3 sugar transport. A) P–2, Q–1, R–3. B) P–1, Q–2, R–3 C) P–2, Q–3, R–1 D) P–3, Q–1, R–2
23. Which food is a useful source of protein? A) Pulses B) Water. C) Table salt D) Cooking oil alone
24. You receive a sensational health message. What should you do first? A) Check trustworthy evidence before sharing B) Accept it because it is popular C) Share it to everyone D) Give the sender your password.
25. In grass → grasshopper → frog → snake, if frogs decrease sharply, what may happen initially, other things being equal? A) Grasshoppers may increase B) Grass becomes a predator C) All energy immediately disappears. D) Snakes make food by photosynthesis
26. In a controlled experiment, a destarched plant receives light but no available carbon dioxide. Little starch forms. What does this support? A) Roots make all sunlight. B) Carbon dioxide is needed for photosynthesis C) Light is never needed D) Plants do not respire
27. Which is a physical change? A) Rusting iron B) Melting of ice C) Burning paper D) Cooking an egg.
28. Match P Chandrayaan-3, Q Mangalyaan, R Aditya-L1 with 1 Sun, 2 Mars, 3 Moon. A) P–1, Q–3, R–2 B) P–3, Q–1, R–2. C) P–2, Q–1, R–3 D) P–3, Q–2, R–1

## Assertion and reason (29–32)

Choose A) Both true, and reason correctly explains assertion. B) Both true, but reason does not correctly explain assertion. C) Assertion true, reason false. D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. Assertion: Antibiotics do not treat influenza virus infections. Reason: Antibiotics target bacterial structures or processes rather than influenza viruses.
30. Assertion: The Sun is a star. Reason: Earth revolves around the Sun.
31. Assertion: Earth's axial tilt contributes to seasons. Reason: Summer occurs simply because Earth is always much closer to the Sun in summer.
32. Assertion: Watering plants in the cooler part of the day can reduce water loss. Reason: Higher temperatures generally increase evaporation.

## Fill in the blanks (33–40)

33. A first tablet is taken immediately and then one every 6 hours. The 10th is taken ___ hours after the first. This is arithmetic, not medical advice.
34. The brain region important for balance and coordination is the ___ .
35. The constitutional body that conducts elections to Parliament is the ___ .
36. In a freely falling lift, a person can experience zero ___ weight.
37. The scientist associated with the Indian space programme and ISRO's early development is Vikram ___ .
38. Article 51A(h) asks citizens to develop scientific temper, humanism and the spirit of ___ and reform.
39. The average of 12, 14, 16 and 18 is ___ .
40. A large square is divided by one vertical and one horizontal line through its centre into four equal small squares. The total number of squares of all sizes is ___ .

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **A:** Evaluate evidence and consult qualified healthcare professionals.
2. **D:** product = LCM × GCD = 300; 300/15 = 20.
3. **C:** acids in lime juice can react with some mineral deposits; do not mix cleaners.
4. **D:** M. S. Swaminathan was a leading figure in India's Green Revolution.
5. **D:** (30 − 20)/20 × 100 = 50%. The final amount is 150% of the original, not a 150% increase.
6. **C:** active muscles generally require more oxygen and produce more carbon dioxide.
7. **A:** breakdown of organic matter can deplete oxygen dissolved in water.
8. **C:** (x − 3)(x − 4) = 0.
9. **B:** composting converts suitable biodegradable matter into useful compost.
10. **D:** the nucleus contains genetic material and helps regulate cell activities.
11. **B:** pressure = force/area; the sharp edge has smaller contact area.
12. **C:** graphite has mobile electrons that enable conduction.
13. **D:** cell → tissue → organ → organ system.
14. **B:** the belt provides a force that changes the passenger's motion.
15. **C:** light travelling in straight lines produces an inverted real image.
16. **B:** independent evidence from fossils, anatomy and DNA supports comparisons.
17. **A:** metal generally conducts heat away from the skin faster.
18. **B:** Chandrayaan-3 landed in 2023.
19. **C:** the son is simultaneously a father and a son.
20. **D:** a shadow forms where an opaque object blocks straight-line light paths.
21. **A:** a broken filament opens the series circuit.
22. **C:** xylem carries water, phloem sugars and stomata enable gas exchange.
23. **A:** pulses provide dietary protein.
24. **A:** independently verify before sharing.
25. **A:** fewer frogs may mean reduced predation on grasshoppers; ecosystems are complex.
26. **B:** with an appropriate comparison, reduced starch supports CO₂'s role.
27. **B:** ice melting changes state but remains water.
28. **D:** Chandrayaan-3—Moon; Mangalyaan—Mars; Aditya-L1—Sun.
29. **A:** both are true and the reason explains the assertion.
30. **B:** both are true, but the orbit does not explain why the Sun is a star.
31. **C:** tilt contributes to seasons; the stated distance explanation is false.
32. **A:** both statements are true and evaporation explains some water saving.
33. **54 hours:** 10 tablets have nine intervals: 9 × 6.
34. **Cerebellum.**
35. **Election Commission of India.**
36. **Apparent:** gravity still acts; apparent weight is the support force.
37. **Sarabhai.**
38. **Inquiry.**
39. **15:** (12 + 14 + 16 + 18)/4.
40. **5:** four small squares plus the whole large square.
