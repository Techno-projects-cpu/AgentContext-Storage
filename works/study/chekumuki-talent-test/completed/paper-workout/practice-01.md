## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. Antibiotics are appropriate against

   A) All viral colds  B) Certain bacterial infections  C) Only nutrient deficiency  D) Every disease

2. 25% of 80 is

   A) 25  B) 20  C) 40  D) 10

3. Photosynthesis uses carbon dioxide and

   A) Only oxygen  B) Water and light  C) Salt and sound  D) Nitrogen and darkness

4. The Raman effect is associated with:

   A) Charles Darwin  B) C. V. Raman  C) Gregor Mendel  D) Isaac Newton's apple alone

5. The average of 4, 6 and 8 is

   A) 5  B) 18  C) 6  D) 7

6. A pressure cooker cooks food faster mainly because:

   A) the lid produces food energy  B) higher pressure raises water's boiling temperature  C) pressure lowers boiling temperature  D) water boils without heat

7. Xylem mainly transports

   A) Blood  B) Water and minerals  C) Nerve signals  D) Sugars

8. Which scientist is associated with wireless communication experiments and plant responses?

   A) Marie Curie only  B) Charles Babbage  C) Jagadish Chandra Bose  D) Srinivasa Ramanujan

9. Divide 25 in the ratio 2:3. What is the first share?

   A) 15  B) 25  C) 5  D) 10

10. The ozone layer in the stratosphere is important because it absorbs much

   A) Gravity  B) Visible light only  C) Ultraviolet radiation  D) Sound

11. Chandrayaan-3 landed

   A) Near the lunar south polar region  B) On the Sun  C) On Mars  D) At the exact north pole of the Moon

12. A solar eclipse occurs when

   A) Earth is between Sun and Moon  B) Earth stops rotating  C) Sun is between Earth and Moon  D) Moon is between Sun and Earth

13. A farmer rotates cereals with leguminous crops. A useful reason is:

   A) legumes can help improve soil nitrogen through associated bacteria  B) legumes eliminate the need for all care  C) every soil becomes identical  D) roots create nitrogen atoms

14. Which can travel through a vacuum?

   A) Light  B) Sound in air  C) A wave on a rope  D) A water wave

15. A person travels 90 km in 2 h. Average speed is:

   A) 180 km/h  B) 90 km/h  C) 92 km/h  D) 45 km/h

16. Rusting of iron generally requires:

   A) pure gold  B) oxygen and water  C) sunlight only  D) nitrogen only

17. Which quantity includes direction?

   A) Speed  B) Velocity  C) Mass  D) Distance

18. Which substance can conduct electricity when dissolved in water because its ions can move?

   A) Wax  B) Sodium chloride  C) Pure oil  D) Sugar

19. Isotopes have the same number of protons but different numbers of

   A) Neutrons  B) Elements  C) Electrons in all ions  D) Nuclei

20. A rainbow's formation involves:

   A) refraction and internal reflection  B) only magnetism  C) only conduction  D) only diffraction by soil

21. A farmer notices poor crop yields after repeatedly growing the same crop. Which is a useful response?

   A) Always burn the soil  B) Consider crop rotation, including legumes where appropriate  C) Add unlimited pesticide regardless of need  D) Remove all organic matter

22. A prism disperses white light mainly because:

   A) light has no energy  B) its refractive index varies with wavelength  C) the prism makes light from darkness  D) all colours travel at identical speeds in glass

23. Which activity helps students' wellbeing as well as teamwork?

   A) Avoiding all physical movement  B) Replacing all learning with a single competition  C) Regular, inclusive sports and games  D) Excluding students who are not athletes

24. A shiny polished surface mainly increases:

   A) sound frequency  B) osmosis  C) nuclear fission  D) regular reflection

25. In a food chain, producers are organisms that

   A) Cannot obtain energy  B) Always eat animals  C) Make their own food, often using sunlight  D) Only decompose dead material

26. Divide 50 in the ratio 2:3. The larger part is:

   A) 20  B) 25  C) 10  D) 30

27. Plants at night generally

   A) Stop all cell activity  B) Respire  C) Convert oxygen to nitrogen  D) Photosynthesise without light

28. A book priced ₹1100 has a 20% discount. What is the sale price?

   A) ₹1100  B) ₹220  C) ₹880  D) ₹1320

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 2 Ω resistor connected across 4 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 4/2 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 3 positive odd numbers is 9. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 4 V component transferring 4 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 4 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 100 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

34. The SI unit of potential difference is the ___.

35. The SI unit of electrical resistance is the ___.

36. The SI unit of power is the ___.

37. A 10% discount on ₹150 is ₹___.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **B** — Antibiotics do not treat viral infections and should be used as medically directed.
2. **B** — 25% is one quarter; 80/4 = 20.
3. **B** — Light energy helps plants make carbohydrates from CO₂ and water.
4. **B** — Raman investigated light scattering.
5. **C** — (4+6+8)/3 = 6.
6. **B** — increased pressure raises boiling temperature.
7. **B** — Xylem carries water and dissolved minerals.
8. **C** — J. C. Bose studied radio waves and plant responses.
9. **D** — Total parts = 5; each part is 5; first share = 2 × 5 = 10.
10. **C** — Stratospheric ozone absorbs much harmful ultraviolet radiation.
11. **A** — Its successful 2023 landing was near the lunar south polar region.
12. **D** — The Moon blocks some sunlight from reaching parts of Earth.
13. **A** — symbiotic bacteria can fix nitrogen; soil still needs proper management.
14. **A** — electromagnetic light does not need a medium.
15. **D** — 90 ÷ 2 = 45.
16. **B** — ordinary rusting requires oxygen and moisture.
17. **B** — Velocity describes speed with direction.
18. **B** — dissolved sodium chloride provides mobile ions.
19. **A** — Isotopes of an element differ in neutron number.
20. **A** — droplets refract and disperse light and internally reflect it.
21. **B** — crop rotation can help manage soil fertility and pests; conditions matter.
22. **B** — different wavelengths refract by different amounts.
23. **C** — inclusive physical activity promotes health and teamwork.
24. **D** — a smoother surface gives more regular reflection.
25. **C** — Producers such as green plants make food through photosynthesis.
26. **D** — five parts; one part is 10, so three parts are 30.
27. **B** — Plants respire day and night; photosynthesis requires light.
28. **C** — The discount is 20% of ₹1100 = ₹220; the price is ₹880.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **ampere (A)** — Accepted answer: ampere (A)
34. **volt (V)** — Accepted answer: volt (V)
35. **ohm (Ω)** — Accepted answer: ohm (Ω)
36. **watt (W)** — Accepted answer: watt (W)
37. **15** — Accepted answer: 15
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **200** — Accepted answer: 200
40. **12** — Accepted answer: 12
