**Original practice, not an official district paper.** Follows the photographed 2026 school/mandal structure: 28 MCQs, 4 assertion–reason, 8 fill-ins. Suggested practice only: 60 minutes, 40 marks, one mark each, no negative marks; confirm district rules with the organiser. Papers mix questions from earlier original packs with new numerical questions; concepts and question styles can recur. The final papers are labelled challenge, but difficulty is subjective. ## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. In a fair test of light's effect on plants, which should normally be kept similar?

   A) The hypothesis only  B) All results  C) Water and soil conditions  D) Light exposure in both groups

2. In parallel branches connected across a battery, the branches have the same

   A) Power always  B) Resistance always  C) Potential difference  D) Current always

3. A 60 W lamp operates for 2 h. Energy used is:

   A) 0.12 kWh  B) 1.2 kWh  C) 120 kWh  D) 30 kWh

4. A passenger moves forward when a bus stops suddenly mainly because of

   A) Refraction  B) Inertia  C) Magnetism  D) Buoyancy

5. A 180 W lamp runs at constant power for 3 hours. How much energy does it use?

   A) 180 Wh  B) 540 Wh  C) 183 Wh  D) 5400 Wh

6. A shopkeeper receives a message claiming an unexpected prize and asking for an advance fee. Best action:

   A) Send the password to everyone  B) Verify independently and do not share financial information  C) Share an OTP  D) Pay immediately

7. Which equation is balanced?

   A) 2H₂ + 2O₂ → H₂O  B) H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O  C) H₂ + O₂ → H₂O  D) 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

8. A vaccine and an antiserum differ because:

   A) vaccines stimulate an immune response while antiserum supplies preformed antibodies  B) antiserum makes every infection impossible forever  C) vaccines contain no biological information  D) both are antibiotics

9. A book rests on a table. The upward table force and downward gravitational force on the book:

   A) form a third-law pair  B) are both zero  C) act on different planets  D) act on the same object and can balance

10. An acid turns blue litmus

   A) Black  B) Red  C) Green  D) Blue

11. A book priced ₹800 has a 30% discount. What is the sale price?

   A) ₹240  B) ₹560  C) ₹1040  D) ₹800

12. A pressure cooker raises the boiling point of water by

   A) Making gravity zero  B) Decreasing pressure  C) Removing water  D) Increasing pressure

13. To test how light affects plant growth fairly, which is best?

   A) Change light while keeping water and other conditions similar  B) Change light and water together  C) Measure only the tallest plant once  D) Compare different species and different soils

14. Baking soda formula is

   A) NaCl  B) H₂SO₄  C) NaHCO₃  D) CaCO₃

15. A 100 W lamp runs for 10 hours. Energy used?

   A) 1 kWh  B) 0.01 kWh  C) 10 kWh  D) 100 kWh

16. Which part of a cell is a major site of aerobic respiration?

   A) Cell wall  B) Vacuole only  C) Mitochondrion  D) Chlorophyll alone

17. If voltage is 12 V and resistance is 4 Ω, current is

   A) 3 A  B) 8 A  C) 48 A  D) 0.3 A

18. Two groups are in a 2:3 ratio and have 25 people in total. Smaller group?

   A) 10  B) 15  C) 5  D) 20

19. Arteries are defined as vessels that carry blood:

   A) only to the lungs  B) always towards the heart  C) away from the heart  D) always rich in oxygen

20. A cyclist travels 40 km in 2 hours. What is the average speed?

   A) 42 km/h  B) 40 km/h  C) 20 km/h  D) 22 km/h

21. Which material is best described as a thermosetting plastic?

   A) Polythene  B) Bakelite  C) Aluminium  D) A cotton fibre

22. A bus starts suddenly. A standing passenger tends to fall backwards because:

   A) the air pushes only backwards  B) gravity stops  C) mass disappears  D) the feet move with the bus while the upper body tends to remain at rest

23. Rusting of iron generally requires

   A) Nitrogen only  B) Oxygen and water  C) Sunlight only  D) Salt only

24. On the Moon, compared with Earth, an object's mass is approximately:

   A) zero  B) one-sixth  C) six times greater  D) unchanged

25. A 28 V DC source is connected across an ohmic 7 Ω resistor. Find the current.

   A) 7 A  B) 28 A  C) 4 A  D) 5 A

26. A 4 V DC source is connected across an ohmic 2 Ω resistor. Find the current.

   A) 4 A  B) 2 A  C) 3 A  D) 103 A

27. A neutral atom has 11 protons. How many electrons does it have?

   A) 12  B) 11  C) 22  D) 10

28. Aditya-L1 primarily studies

   A) Deep oceans  B) The Sun  C) Jupiter's surface  D) Earthquakes in soil

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 5 Ω resistor connected across 10 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 10/5 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 6 positive odd numbers is 36. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 10 V component transferring 10 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 10 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 130 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Fill in the blanks (33–40)

33. Sound needs a material ___ to travel.

34. In a metal wire moving ___ carry electric charge.

35. A voltmeter is connected in ___ across a component.

36. An ammeter is connected in ___ with the measured component.

37. A 10% discount on ₹210 is ₹___.

38. A 15 V source across an ohmic 5 Ω resistor gives a current of ___ A.

39. A 130 W device running for 2 hours uses ___ Wh.

40. The average of 13, 15 and 17 is ___.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **C** — Vary light while keeping relevant conditions, including water and soil, similar.
2. **C** — Parallel branches share the same potential difference.
3. **A** — 60 W × 2 h = 120 Wh = 0.12 kWh.
4. **B** — The passenger tends to continue moving when the bus slows.
5. **B** — Energy = power × time = 180 × 3 = 540 Wh.
6. **B** — independently verify and never share OTPs.
7. **D** — both sides contain four hydrogen atoms and two oxygen atoms.
8. **A** — antiserum provides antibodies; vaccination stimulates immune responses. Both require appropriate medical guidance.
9. **D** — both forces act on the book, so they are not a third-law pair.
10. **B** — Acids turn blue litmus red.
11. **B** — The discount is 30% of ₹800 = ₹240; the price is ₹560.
12. **D** — Higher pressure raises the boiling point.
13. **A** — changing one main variable makes the effect easier to interpret; use multiple plants if possible.
14. **C** — Baking soda is sodium hydrogen carbonate.
15. **A** — 100 W = 0.1 kW; 0.1 × 10 = 1 kWh.
16. **C** — Mitochondria play a major role in aerobic respiration.
17. **A** — I = V/R = 12/4 = 3 A for an ohmic conductor.
18. **A** — Five equal parts; each is 5; smaller group is 2 × 5 = 10.
19. **C** — pulmonary arteries show why oxygen content is not the definition.
20. **C** — Average speed = distance/time = 40/2 = 20 km/h.
21. **B** — Bakelite is a thermosetting plastic.
22. **D** — inertia resists a change in motion.
23. **B** — Oxygen and moisture are needed; salt can accelerate corrosion.
24. **D** — mass stays approximately the same; weight changes.
25. **C** — I = V/R = 28/7 = 4 A.
26. **B** — I = V/R = 4/2 = 2 A.
27. **B** — a neutral atom has equal numbers of protons and electrons.
28. **B** — Aditya-L1 is a solar observatory mission.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **medium** — Accepted answer: medium
34. **electrons** — Accepted answer: electrons
35. **parallel** — Accepted answer: parallel
36. **series** — Accepted answer: series
37. **21** — Accepted answer: 21
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **260** — Accepted answer: 260
40. **15** — Accepted answer: 15
