## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 6 Ω resistor connected across 12 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 12/6 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 7 positive odd numbers is 49. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 12 V component transferring 12 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 12 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 140 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **C** — Zinc coating helps protect iron or steel.
2. **D** — Cellular respiration releases energy from nutrients.
3. **A** — influenza is viral.
4. **C** — I = V/R = 18/6 = 3 A.
5. **B** — 10% of 200 is 20; 200 − 20 = 180.
6. **C** — Insulin helps regulate blood glucose levels.
7. **C** — Enzymes catalyse biological reactions.
8. **A** — insoluble sand can be trapped by a filter.
9. **C** — Total parts = 15; each part is 19; first share = 9 × 19 = 171.
10. **C** — The discount is 40% of ₹900 = ₹360; the price is ₹540.
11. **A** — 10% is 20 and 5% is 10, making 30.
12. **B** — C. V. Raman studied changes in wavelength of scattered light.
13. **A** — A plane-mirror image is virtual, upright and the same size.
14. **D** — I = V/R = 6/3 = 2 A.
15. **C** — Area = length × breadth = 24 square centimetres.
16. **A** — phloem transports sugars.
17. **A** — Speed = 60 ÷ 3 = 20 km/h.
18. **C** — Energy = power × time = 200 × 4 = 800 Wh.
19. **A** — The discount is 30% of ₹400 = ₹120; the price is ₹280.
20. **C** — inertia keeps the body moving as the bus slows.
21. **A** — Pressure = force/area; smaller area gives greater pressure.
22. **D** — Atomic number is the proton count.
23. **D** — pressure = force/area.
24. **C** — Total parts = 13; each part is 21; first share = 10 × 21 = 210.
25. **D** — Average speed = distance/time = 165/3 = 55 km/h.
26. **C** — Nephrons filter blood and help regulate water and salts.
27. **D** — protect your eyes and use evidence-based safety guidance.
28. **C** — I = V/R = 10/5 = 2 A.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **motor** — Accepted answer: motor
34. **generator** — Accepted answer: generator
35. **radiation** — Accepted answer: radiation
36. **open** — Accepted answer: open
37. **23** — Accepted answer: 23
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **280** — Accepted answer: 280
40. **16** — Accepted answer: 16
