## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. A DC device has 3 V across it and carries 2 A. How much power does it transfer?

   A) 1.5 W  B) 6 W  C) 5 W  D) 9 W

2. A 60 W lamp operates continuously for 5 hours. Energy?

   A) 0.3 kWh  B) 300 kWh  C) 3 kWh  D) 12 kWh

3. A book priced ₹700 has a 20% discount. What is the sale price?

   A) ₹840  B) ₹140  C) ₹700  D) ₹560

4. Water enters dry raisins placed in water primarily by:

   A) combustion  B) reflection  C) osmosis  D) nuclear fusion

5. Which is the correct formula of plaster of Paris?

   A) NaHCO₃  B) CaSO₄·2H₂O  C) CaSO₄·½H₂O  D) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O

6. Divide 165 in the ratio 7:4. What is the first share?

   A) 105  B) 165  C) 60  D) 15

7. A 220 W lamp runs at constant power for 2 hours. How much energy does it use?

   A) 220 Wh  B) 440 Wh  C) 4400 Wh  D) 222 Wh

8. Graphite and diamond differ in properties mainly because

   A) Diamond is a metal  B) Graphite has no atoms  C) Carbon atoms have different arrangements and bonding  D) One contains no carbon

9. A shop offers 20% off an item marked ₹500. The price after discount is:

   A) ₹600  B) ₹400  C) ₹480  D) ₹100

10. Which formula represents plaster of Paris?

   A) CO₂  B) CaSO₄·2H₂O  C) CaSO₄·½H₂O  D) NaCl

11. Which blood component is primarily responsible for carrying oxygen?

   A) Red blood cells  B) White blood cells  C) Blood plasma alone  D) Platelets

12. Which part of an experiment is the dependent variable?

   A) The outcome measured  B) Every constant condition  C) The name of the scientist  D) The factor deliberately changed

13. Which pair correctly distinguishes earthquake terms?

   A) Magnitude describes event size; intensity describes local effects  B) Both always mean exactly the same thing  C) Intensity measures only earthquake depth  D) Magnitude is local weather; intensity is annual rainfall

14. Which household substance is used to help cakes rise?

   A) Sand  B) Sodium chloride only  C) Sodium bicarbonate  D) Iron filings

15. A student wants to test the effect of fertiliser on similar plants. Which is the best comparison?

   A) Change fertiliser while keeping other conditions similar and using several plants in each group  B) Select only the tallest plants afterwards  C) Measure a single plant only  D) Change fertiliser, species, soil and water

16. Which mission is designed to study the Sun from the Sun–Earth L1 region?

   A) Chandrayaan-3  B) XPoSat only  C) Aditya-L1  D) Mangalyaan only

17. Which equation is balanced?

   A) H₂ + O₂ → H₂O  B) 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O  C) 2H₂ + 2O₂ → H₂O  D) H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

18. Which oxide is amphoteric?

   A) Al₂O₃  B) CO₂  C) Na₂O  D) SO₂

19. In triangle ABC, D lies on AB and E on AC, with DE parallel to BC. If AD = 6 cm, DB = 9 cm and AE = 4 cm, find EC.

   A) 10 cm  B) 6 cm  C) 4 cm  D) 9 cm

20. A cyclist travels 120 km in 3 hours. What is the average speed?

   A) 123 km/h  B) 43 km/h  C) 120 km/h  D) 40 km/h

21. Two 6 Ω resistors are connected in series to an ideal 12 V battery. The current through each is:

   A) 4 A  B) 1 A  C) 12 A  D) 2 A

22. A rectangle is 8 cm long and 3 cm wide. Its area is:

   A) 22 cm  B) 24 cm²  C) 11 cm  D) 48 cm²

23. The Raman effect is associated with

   A) Blood clotting  B) Changes in wavelength of scattered light  C) Water boiling at high pressure  D) Only reflecting radio waves

24. Which blood vessel carries oxygen-poor blood away from the heart towards the lungs?

   A) Pulmonary vein  B) Pulmonary artery  C) Vena cava  D) Aorta

25. A plant in darkness overnight:

   A) carries out photosynthesis at its normal daylight rate  B) continues cellular respiration  C) becomes an animal  D) stops using energy

26. A cyclist travels 70 km in 2 hours. What is the average speed?

   A) 72 km/h  B) 37 km/h  C) 70 km/h  D) 35 km/h

27. A bag contains 3 red and 2 blue balls. One ball is selected at random. Probability it is blue is:

   A) 2/3  B) 2/5  C) 3/5  D) 1/2

28. A 150 g object has a volume of 50 cm³. Its density is

   A) 3 g/cm³  B) 200 g/cm³  C) 100 g/cm³  D) 0.33 g/cm³

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 7 Ω resistor connected across 14 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 14/7 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 8 positive odd numbers is 64. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 14 V component transferring 14 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 14 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 150 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Fill in the blanks (33–40)

33. The plant tissue transporting water and minerals is ___.

34. The plant tissue transporting sugars is ___.

35. Most nutrient absorption occurs in the ___ intestine.

36. Oxygen is mainly transported by ___ blood cells.

37. A 10% discount on ₹250 is ₹___.

38. A 21 V source across an ohmic 7 Ω resistor gives a current of ___ A.

40. The average of 15, 17 and 19 is ___.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **B** — P = VI = 3 × 2. A watt is a joule per second.
2. **A** — 60 W = 0.06 kW, so 0.06 × 5 = 0.3 kWh.
3. **D** — The discount is 20% of ₹700 = ₹140; the price is ₹560.
4. **C** — water enters through selectively permeable membranes.
5. **C** — Plaster of Paris is calcium sulfate hemihydrate; gypsum is the dihydrate.
6. **A** — Total parts = 11; each part is 15; first share = 7 × 15 = 105.
7. **B** — Energy = power × time = 220 × 2 = 440 Wh.
8. **C** — Both are forms of carbon with different structures.
9. **B** — 20% of 500 is 100; 500 − 100 = ₹400.
10. **C** — gypsum is CaSO₄·2H₂O.
11. **A** — haemoglobin in red blood cells carries oxygen.
12. **A** — The dependent variable is the measured response.
13. **A** — Magnitude characterises the earthquake; shaking effects vary by location.
14. **C** — sodium bicarbonate releases gas in suitable baking reactions.
15. **A** — Change one main factor, keep other conditions similar, and use multiple plants to reduce the influence of individual variation.
16. **C** — Aditya-L1 is an Indian solar observatory mission.
17. **B** — Both sides contain four hydrogen atoms and two oxygen atoms. Balance with coefficients, not by changing chemical formulas.
18. **A** — Aluminium oxide can react with acids and bases under suitable conditions.
19. **B** — similarity gives AD/DB = AE/EC, so 6/9 = 4/EC and EC = 6 cm.
20. **D** — Average speed = distance/time = 120/3 = 40 km/h.
21. **B** — Series resistance is 6 + 6 = 12 Ω, so I = 12/12. The same current flows through each component in one loop.
22. **B** — Area = length × width = 8 × 3; perimeter would be 22 cm.
23. **B** — Raman scattering involves energy exchange between light and a material.
24. **B** — An artery carries blood away from the heart. The pulmonary artery is an important oxygen-poor exception.
25. **B** — Plants respire both day and night. Photosynthesis needs light.
26. **D** — Average speed = distance/time = 70/2 = 35 km/h.
27. **B** — Two blue outcomes out of five equally likely balls.
28. **A** — Density = mass/volume = 150/50 = 3 g/cm³.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **xylem** — Accepted answer: xylem
34. **phloem** — Accepted answer: phloem
35. **small** — Accepted answer: small
36. **red** — Accepted answer: red
37. **25** — Accepted answer: 25
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **300** — Accepted answer: 300
40. **17** — Accepted answer: 17
