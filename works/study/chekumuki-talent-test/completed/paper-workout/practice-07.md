**Original practice, not an official district paper.** Follows the photographed 2026 school/mandal structure: 28 MCQs, 4 assertion–reason, 8 fill-ins. Suggested practice only: 60 minutes, 40 marks, one mark each, no negative marks; confirm district rules with the organiser. Papers mix questions from earlier original packs with new numerical questions; concepts and question styles can recur. The final papers are labelled challenge, but difficulty is subjective. Do not use mains electricity.

## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. Which statement is a testable hypothesis?

   A) All beautiful ideas are true  B) A plant secretly wishes to grow  C) Science is wonderful  D) Plants receiving 6 hours of light daily grow taller on average than similar plants receiving 2 hours, under specified conditions

2. Why does solid NaCl not conduct well but molten NaCl does?

   A) Solid NaCl has no ions  B) The atoms vanish when heated  C) Ions can move in the molten state  D) Molten NaCl contains only electrons

3. Which statement is correct?

   A) Plants respire day and night  B) Plants never need oxygen  C) Respiration and photosynthesis are identical  D) Plants respire only in sunlight

4. An apparent link between ice-cream sales and swimming can be explained by

   A) Swimming must cause sales  B) Ice cream must cause swimming  C) A common factor such as hot weather  D) Correlation proves a direct cause

5. When an iron nail rusts in air, the nail and attached rust may gain mass mainly because:

   A) water has no mass  B) iron becomes lighter than air  C) oxygen from the surroundings combines with iron  D) mass is created from nothing

6. A 120 W lamp runs at constant power for 3 hours. How much energy does it use?

   A) 123 Wh  B) 120 Wh  C) 3600 Wh  D) 360 Wh

7. A cyclist travels 120 km in 4 hours. What is the average speed?

   A) 30 km/h  B) 120 km/h  C) 124 km/h  D) 34 km/h

8. A sodium-23 ion Na⁺ has atomic number 11. How many electrons?

   A) 12  B) 10  C) 23  D) 11

9. A 20% discount on ₹250 gives a sale price of:

   A) ₹230  B) ₹200  C) ₹270  D) ₹50

10. Adding a small amount of dilute acid to an aqueous sodium hydroxide solution lowers its pH when other relevant conditions are controlled. Why?

   A) pH measures only colour  B) Neutralisation reduces hydroxide ions  C) Acid makes hydroxide ions without limit  D) A base cannot react with an acid

11. Which tissue transports sugars from leaves to other parts of a plant?

   A) Phloem  B) Root hairs only  C) Xylem  D) Epidermis only

12. A community restricts a student from school during menstruation. The most appropriate response is to:

   A) provide accurate health information and support inclusion  B) support exclusion  C) claim menstruation is contagious  D) shame the student

13. Two 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel across an ideal 12 V source. The total current is:

   A) 2 A  B) 4 A  C) 0.5 A  D) 1 A

14. A resistor has 12 V across it and carries 3 A. Resistance is

   A) 36 Ω  B) 4 Ω  C) 0.25 Ω  D) 9 Ω

15. A light ray moves from air into glass. Which stays constant across the boundary?

   A) Direction at every angle  B) Wavelength  C) Frequency  D) Speed

16. A simple circuit has an open switch. Which can be true?

   A) The battery has no chemical energy  B) The battery has voltage across its terminals but no external loop current  C) Resistance must be zero  D) Sustained current flows around the broken loop

17. Plants placed in darkness generally continue

   A) Cellular respiration  B) All processes stop  C) Photosynthesis requiring light  D) Producing glucose by photosynthesis without light

18. A book rests on a table. The upward force of the table on the book and the gravitational pull of Earth on the book:

   A) must both be zero  B) form a Newton's third-law pair  C) act on the same object and can balance  D) are unrelated to the book

19. A person travels 60 km in 1 h and another 60 km in 2 h. Average speed is:

   A) 40 km/h  B) 45 km/h  C) 30 km/h  D) 60 km/h

20. Which quantity has units of energy rather than power?

   A) Kilowatt  B) Kilowatt-hour  C) Watt  D) Joule per second

21. Two atoms have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons. They are:

   A) always different elements  B) necessarily oppositely charged  C) isotopes of the same element  D) identical in mass

22. In Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu, iron

   A) Forms carbon dioxide  B) Disappears without trace  C) Displaces copper from its compound  D) Is less reactive than copper

23. Divide 117 in the ratio 6:3. What is the first share?

   A) 117  B) 78  C) 13  D) 39

24. To separate sand from a mixture of sand and dissolved salt in water, which step works first?

   A) Sieving out dissolved ions  B) Ordinary filtration  C) Evaporation of sand alone  D) A magnet in every case

25. A cyclist travels 180 km in 4 hours. What is the average speed?

   A) 49 km/h  B) 45 km/h  C) 180 km/h  D) 184 km/h

26. A 9 V DC source is connected across an ohmic 3 Ω resistor. Find the current.

   A) 104 A  B) 4 A  C) 3 A  D) 9 A

27. Bile helps digestion chiefly by

   A) Producing insulin  B) Absorbing oxygen  C) Breaking protein as an enzyme  D) Emulsifying fats

28. A 16 V DC source is connected across an ohmic 8 Ω resistor. Find the current.

   A) 8 A  B) 2 A  C) 16 A  D) 3 A

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 8 Ω resistor connected across 16 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 16/8 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 9 positive odd numbers is 81. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 16 V component transferring 16 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 16 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 160 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Fill in the blanks (33–40)

33. The oxygen released in photosynthesis primarily comes from ___ molecules.

34. The rigid external plant-cell layer is the cell ___.

35. A directional growth response to touch is called ___.

36. Green plants carry out cellular respiration during day and ___.

37. A 10% discount on ₹270 is ₹___.

38. A 24 V source across an ohmic 8 Ω resistor gives a current of ___ A.

39. A 160 W device running for 2 hours uses ___ Wh.

40. The average of 16, 18 and 20 is ___.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **D** — It specifies a measurable comparison under stated conditions; the other choices are opinions or unfalsifiable claims.
2. **C** — Mobile ions transport charge in the liquid.
3. **A** — plants respire day and night.
4. **C** — Both can increase because of the weather; correlation is not proof of causation.
5. **C** — Oxygen enters from the surroundings. Total mass is conserved when the full closed system is accounted for.
6. **D** — Energy = power × time = 120 × 3 = 360 Wh.
7. **A** — Average speed = distance/time = 120/4 = 30 km/h.
8. **B** — The neutral atom has 11 electrons; Na⁺ has lost one.
9. **B** — 20% of ₹250 is ₹50; subtract it from the original price.
10. **B** — acid neutralises hydroxide ions, reducing basicity in this setting.
11. **A** — phloem carries sugars.
12. **A** — menstruation is a normal biological process, not a reason for exclusion.
13. **B** — Each branch gets 12 V, so each carries 12/6 = 2 A. Add branch currents: 2 + 2 = 4 A.
14. **B** — R = V/I = 12/3 = 4 Ω for an ohmic resistor.
15. **C** — Frequency stays constant; speed and wavelength change.
16. **B** — The battery can maintain a potential difference even when the external path is broken. No sustained current flows around that open loop.
17. **A** — Plant cells respire both in light and darkness.
18. **C** — Both forces act on the book; a third-law pair acts on two different bodies. These two can balance if the book is at rest.
19. **A** — Total distance 120 km divided by total time 3 h. Do not simply average the two speeds here.
20. **B** — A kilowatt-hour equals power multiplied by time.
21. **C** — proton count identifies the element; neutrons distinguish isotopes.
22. **C** — Iron is more reactive than copper and displaces it from solution.
23. **B** — Total parts = 9; each part is 13; first share = 6 × 13 = 78.
24. **B** — Insoluble sand can be filtered out. The dissolved salt passes through an ordinary filter and can be separated later by another suitable method.
25. **B** — Average speed = distance/time = 180/4 = 45 km/h.
26. **C** — I = V/R = 9/3 = 3 A.
27. **D** — Bile produced by the liver helps disperse fat; it is not itself a digestive enzyme.
28. **B** — I = V/R = 16/8 = 2 A.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **water** — Accepted answer: water
34. **wall** — Accepted answer: wall
35. **thigmotropism** — Accepted answer: thigmotropism
36. **night** — Accepted answer: night
37. **27** — Accepted answer: 27
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **320** — Accepted answer: 320
40. **18** — Accepted answer: 18
