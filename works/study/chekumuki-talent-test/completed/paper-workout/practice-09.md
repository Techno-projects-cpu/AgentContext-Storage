**Original practice, not an official district paper.** Follows the photographed 2026 school/mandal structure: 28 MCQs, 4 assertion–reason, 8 fill-ins. Suggested practice only: 60 minutes, 40 marks, one mark each, no negative marks; confirm district rules with the organiser. ## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. A message says you won a prize but must pay a fee immediately. You should:

   A) forward private details to strangers  B) independently verify and tell a trusted adult  C) share bank passwords  D) pay quickly

2. Why can molten sodium chloride conduct electricity, unlike the solid under ordinary conditions?

   A) Its atoms disappear  B) Its ions can move  C) It has no ions  D) It becomes a metal

3. An echo returns in 0.2 seconds. Sound speed is 340 m/s. Reflector distance?

   A) 17 m  B) 170 m  C) 34 m  D) 68 m

4. A steel ship can float because

   A) Its overall average density including air spaces can be below water density  B) Gravity stops acting  C) Steel has no mass  D) Water exerts no upward force

5. An astronaut in orbit experiences apparent weightlessness mainly because:

   A) the Moon stops attracting Earth  B) there is no gravity at all  C) the astronaut and spacecraft fall together  D) the astronaut has no mass

6. A 40 W lamp runs at constant power for 2 hours. How much energy does it use?

   A) 800 Wh  B) 42 Wh  C) 80 Wh  D) 40 Wh

7. A 12 V ideal battery is connected to a 6 Ω resistor. What is the current?

   A) 0.5 A  B) 6 A  C) 2 A  D) 72 A

8. The upthrust on an immersed object is equal to the:

   A) object's density  B) object's volume  C) pressure at the top alone  D) weight of fluid displaced

9. Which can be separated from water by ordinary filtration?

   A) Dissolved sugar  B) All dissolved ions  C) Dissolved salt  D) Insoluble sand

10. A book priced ₹1000 has a 10% discount. What is the sale price?

   A) ₹100  B) ₹1000  C) ₹900  D) ₹1100

11. Why can an LED lamp use less power than an incandescent lamp at similar brightness?

   A) It makes energy from nothing  B) Current never flows in it  C) It has no resistance  D) It can convert a larger fraction of electrical energy to visible light

12. Which pairing is correct?

   A) Dengue — bacterium  B) Malaria — Plasmodium parasite  C) Malaria — mosquito is the pathogen  D) Common cold — Plasmodium

13. Biomagnification refers to

   A) All species increasing in size  B) Increasing concentration of some persistent substances at higher trophic levels  C) Water evaporating into clouds  D) All food energy increasing at each level

14. Which statement about antibiotics is correct?

   A) They work equally on all diseases  B) They replace all vaccines  C) They treat certain bacterial infections, but resistance can reduce effectiveness  D) They cure influenza viruses

15. A sound echo returns in 0.2 s. If sound speed is 340 m/s, the reflecting wall is approximately:

   A) 34 m  B) 68 m  C) 17 m  D) 170 m

16. A fuse breaks a circuit under excessive current because:

   A) it stores all the charge  B) it increases the battery voltage  C) its element heats up and melts  D) it produces water

17. In osmosis, the substance moving through a selectively permeable membrane is chiefly

   A) Only oxygen  B) Every dissolved salt equally  C) Water  D) Chlorophyll

18. Which statement about inheritance is most accurate?

   A) Many traits involve multiple genes and environmental influences  B) All offspring from sexual reproduction are genetically identical  C) DNA is made of organs  D) Every trait is controlled by exactly one gene

19. A ray enters glass from air at an angle to the normal. Which generally changes at the boundary?

   A) Frequency only  B) Neither speed nor wavelength  C) Speed and wavelength  D) Frequency and the identity of the source

20. Which instrument and connection measure the potential difference across a resistor?

   A) Ammeter across it  B) Voltmeter in series  C) Voltmeter across it  D) Ammeter in series

21. A student walks one full lap of a circular track back to the starting point. Which is correct?

   A) Both distance and displacement are zero  B) Both must be equal and nonzero  C) Distance is zero, displacement is nonzero  D) Distance is nonzero, displacement is zero

22. Seasons are mainly caused by

   A) Monthly lunar phases  B) Earth being closer to the Sun during summer everywhere  C) Earth’s axial tilt combined with its revolution around the Sun  D) Daily rotation alone

23. A person has a knee-jerk response after a tendon is tapped. It is best described as:

   A) a spinal somatic reflex  B) photosynthesis  C) a planned voluntary action  D) digestion

24. Which vessel carries oxygen-poor blood from the heart to the lungs?

   A) Pulmonary artery  B) Pulmonary vein  C) Vena cava  D) Aorta

25. A person exerts a force on a wall. The third-law reaction acts on

   A) The person, exerted by the wall  B) The wall, exerted by the person  C) A different wall  D) Earth only

26. In a food web, decomposers mainly:

   A) turn all energy back into sunlight  B) return nutrients by breaking down dead material  C) stop all nutrient cycling  D) are always large predators

27. A cyclist travels 100 km in 2 hours. What is the average speed?

   A) 102 km/h  B) 100 km/h  C) 52 km/h  D) 50 km/h

28. Isotopes of the same element have

   A) Same proton number, different neutron number  B) Same mass number always  C) Same neutrons, different protons  D) Different atomic numbers

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 10 Ω resistor connected across 20 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 20/10 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 11 positive odd numbers is 121. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 20 V component transferring 20 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 20 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 180 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Fill in the blanks (33–40)

33. Rusting normally requires oxygen and ___.

34. The formula CaSO₄·½H₂O identifies plaster of ___.

35. A process for recovering crystals from a solution is ___.

36. The Earth completes one rotation in approximately ___ hours.

37. A 10% discount on ₹310 is ₹___.

38. A 30 V source across an ohmic 10 Ω resistor gives a current of ___ A.

39. A 180 W device running for 2 hours uses ___ Wh.

40. The average of 18, 20 and 22 is ___.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **B** — avoid payment or sharing information; verify independently.
2. **B** — Ions can move in the molten salt and carry charge; in the solid lattice they are not free to move through the solid.
3. **C** — The sound travels to the reflector and back: 340 × 0.2 / 2.
4. **A** — The ship displaces enough water for buoyancy to balance its weight.
5. **C** — both spacecraft and astronaut are in free fall; gravity remains significant.
6. **C** — Energy = power × time = 40 × 2 = 80 Wh.
7. **C** — Ohm's law gives I = V/R = 12/6. This assumes the resistor behaves ohmically under the stated conditions.
8. **D** — Archimedes' principle.
9. **D** — an ordinary filter retains insoluble sand.
10. **C** — The discount is 10% of ₹1000 = ₹100; the price is ₹900.
11. **D** — LEDs can provide similar light with less electrical power.
12. **B** — The parasite causes malaria; certain female Anopheles mosquitoes transmit it.
13. **B** — Some persistent pollutants can become more concentrated along food chains.
14. **C** — Antibiotics act against certain bacteria, not influenza viruses. Consult a qualified health professional about treatment.
15. **A** — Total sound travel = 340 × 0.2 = 68 m, but this is the outward and return journey. Divide by two.
16. **C** — excess current heats the designed fuse element until it melts.
17. **C** — Osmosis is the net movement of water due to a water-potential difference.
18. **A** — Many traits have contributions from several genes and the environment. Simple single-gene examples are useful but not universal.
19. **C** — Light's speed and wavelength change on entering glass; its frequency remains the same at the boundary.
20. **C** — A voltmeter is placed across the two points being compared. Never experiment with household mains.
21. **D** — Displacement depends only on change in position; distance measures the full path.
22. **C** — Tilt changes solar angle and daylight through the year; hemispheres differ.
23. **A** — knee-jerk is a spinal somatic reflex.
24. **A** — arteries carry blood away from the heart; the pulmonary artery is an oxygen-poor exception.
25. **A** — The wall pushes the person with equal magnitude and opposite direction.
26. **B** — Decomposers break down material and return nutrients. Nutrients cycle; energy flows and is dispersed.
27. **D** — Average speed = distance/time = 100/2 = 50 km/h.
28. **A** — The number of protons defines an element; isotopes differ in neutrons.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **water / moisture** — Accepted answer: water / moisture
34. **Paris** — Accepted answer: Paris
35. **crystallisation** — Accepted answer: crystallisation
36. **24** — Accepted answer: 24
37. **31** — Accepted answer: 31
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **360** — Accepted answer: 360
40. **20** — Accepted answer: 20
