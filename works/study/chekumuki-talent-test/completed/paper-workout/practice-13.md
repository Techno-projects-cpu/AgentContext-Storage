# Practice Paper 13 — Heavy Sword: District Simulation ⚔️

**Original challenge, not an official district question paper.** Suggested practice: 60 minutes, 40 marks, one mark each and no negative marks. The actual district format, marking and quiz rules may differ. This paper has 28 MCQs, 4 assertion–reason and 8 fill-ins. Answers and reasoning are at the end. [Workout index](README.md)

## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. A 12 V ideal battery is connected to a 2 Ω resistor in series with a 4 Ω resistor. Current is: A) 1 A B) 2 A C) 3 A D) 6 A.
2. In question 1, the voltage across the 2 Ω resistor is: A) 2 V B) 4 V C) 6 V D) 8 V.
3. A 4 Ω resistor is connected across 8 V DC. Its power is: A) 2 W B) 8 W C) 16 W D) 32 W.
4. A 50 W device runs at constant power for 6 hours. Energy used is: A) 0.03 kWh B) 0.3 kWh C) 3 kWh D) 30 kWh.
5. A student says the bulb in a series circuit uses up the electric current. Best correction: A) the bulb transfers energy; charge current is the same in the steady single loop B) all charge vanishes in the bulb C) current flows only into the bulb D) voltage and current are identical.
6. An object moves in a circle at constant speed. Which is necessarily changing? A) Velocity direction B) Mass C) Speed D) Distance from the circle's centre.
7. A force of 30 N acts perpendicular to a surface of 0.2 m². Average pressure is: A) 6 Pa B) 30 Pa C) 60 Pa D) 150 Pa.
8. A floating block displaces 500 g of water. If it is floating at rest, its mass is: A) 250 g B) 500 g C) 1,000 g D) unknown because floating does not involve forces.
9. Why can a metal spoon feel colder than a wooden spoon at the same temperature? A) Metal generally transfers heat from the skin faster B) Metal must always be at a lower temperature C) Wood creates energy D) Temperature of metal is always zero.
10. A ray enters glass from air at nonzero incidence. Compared with air, in glass its frequency: A) remains the same B) doubles C) becomes zero D) changes with thickness alone.
11. Which contains atoms of the same element but different neutron counts? A) Isotopes B) Isobars necessarily of same element C) Unrelated compounds D) Mixtures only.
12. A solution's pH changes from 6 to 4. Hydrogen-ion activity changes by a factor of: A) 2 B) 10 C) 100 D) 1,000.
13. Which process can recover water from salt solution by evaporation and condensation? A) Distillation B) Ordinary filtration alone C) Magnetic separation D) Hand picking.
14. A catalyst increases a reaction rate. Which is the best explanation? A) It supplies an alternative pathway with lower activation energy B) It creates matter C) It makes every equilibrium favour products D) It changes elements into nothing.
15. An iron nail is painted. The coating can slow rusting mainly by: A) reducing contact with oxygen and moisture B) eliminating gravity C) changing all iron into copper D) creating water inside the nail.
16. In a neuron, which structure typically carries impulses away from the cell body? A) Axon B) Dendrite C) Stoma D) Root hair.
17. Which pairing is correct? A) Xylem—water and minerals B) Phloem—oxygen only C) Red blood cells—blood clotting as their primary role D) Platelets—photosynthesis.
18. Which statement best distinguishes a vaccine from antiserum? A) Vaccination stimulates immune responses; antiserum provides preformed antibodies B) Both kill all viruses directly C) Both are antibiotics D) Antiserum always provides lifelong immunity.
19. During exercise, breathing and heart rate often increase because: A) active muscles generally require more oxygen and produce more carbon dioxide B) the heart manufactures oxygen C) the lungs stop exchanging gas D) muscles stop using energy.
20. A plant leaf receives light but is deprived of carbon dioxide in a properly controlled test. Starch production would generally: A) decrease B) increase without limit C) be unaffected by CO₂ availability D) prove the leaf is not alive.
21. In a food chain, energy available for the next trophic level generally decreases because: A) organisms use energy for life processes and energy is dispersed as heat B) matter disappears C) producers have no energy D) heat becomes food automatically.
22. A viral message claims a solar eclipse poisons all cooked food. What is the best response? A) Check evidence; an eclipse does not by itself poison food B) Share immediately C) Look directly at the Sun to check D) Decide by number of forwards.
23. Which comparison is a fairer test of fertiliser's effect on seedlings? A) Similar seedlings, same water and soil, different fertiliser, with several in each group B) Different plants, soils and water C) Only one plant with no comparison D) Keep only results that agree with the hypothesis.
24. A paper says a medal was won in a particular year. Which check is most useful? A) Consult the dated organiser's results for the exact event B) Assume all years have the same winners C) Rely only on an unsigned viral post D) Guess from the athlete's popularity.
25. A shop marks an item at ₹1,200 and offers 25% off. Price after discount: A) ₹300 B) ₹600 C) ₹900 D) ₹1,000.
26. Three scores have an average of 18. Two scores are 15 and 20. The third is: A) 17 B) 18 C) 19 D) 20.
27. A rectangle has perimeter 30 cm and length 9 cm. Its area is: A) 36 cm² B) 45 cm² C) 54 cm² D) 90 cm².
28. A fair die is rolled twice. Probability of two sixes is: A) 1/6 B) 1/12 C) 1/18 D) 1/36.

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true; reason correctly explains assertion. B) Both true; reason does not correctly explain assertion. C) Assertion true, reason false. D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. Assertion: A passenger tends to move forward when a moving bus brakes suddenly. Reason: The body tends to maintain its state of motion when the bus slows.
30. Assertion: Plants respire during the night. Reason: The Moon reflects sunlight.
31. Assertion: Sound needs a material medium. Reason: Sound is an electromagnetic wave that travels through vacuum.
32. Assertion: A 1 kW appliance running for 1 h uses 1 W of energy. Reason: One kilowatt-hour is a unit of energy.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **B:** series resistance = 2 + 4 = 6 Ω; I = 12/6 = 2 A.
2. **B:** V = IR = 2 × 2 = 4 V across the 2 Ω resistor.
3. **C:** I = 8/4 = 2 A; P = 8 × 2 = 16 W.
4. **B:** 50 × 6 = 300 Wh = 0.3 kWh.
5. **A:** the bulb transfers electrical energy; charge is not used up.
6. **A:** direction changes even when speed stays the same.
7. **D:** pressure = 30/0.2 = 150 Pa.
8. **B:** in floating equilibrium displaced water's weight equals block weight; in the same gravitational field this gives equal masses.
9. **A:** thermal conductivity affects the rate of heat transfer from skin.
10. **A:** frequency remains the same; speed and wavelength change.
11. **A:** isotopes have the same proton number and differing neutron numbers.
12. **C:** a change of 2 pH units corresponds to 10² = 100 times hydrogen-ion activity.
13. **A:** evaporation followed by condensation collects water; treatment quality depends on the contaminants.
14. **A:** the alternative pathway lowers activation energy.
15. **A:** a protective coating reduces exposure to water and oxygen.
16. **A:** axons usually carry impulses away from the neuron cell body.
17. **A:** xylem transports water and minerals; phloem carries sugars.
18. **A:** antiserum gives passive antibodies; vaccines stimulate immune responses.
19. **A:** muscles' metabolic demand rises during exercise.
20. **A:** carbon dioxide is a raw material for photosynthesis; a suitable control is important.
21. **A:** organisms use energy and energy is transferred to the surroundings as heat.
22. **A:** test claims with evidence; never view the Sun directly without appropriate certified protection and expert guidance.
23. **A:** alter one main factor and keep others similar; replication helps.
24. **A:** dates and exact events matter for current affairs.
25. **C:** 25% of 1,200 is 300; 1,200 − 300 = 900.
26. **C:** total = 3 × 18 = 54; 54 − 15 − 20 = 19.
27. **C:** 2(L + W) = 30 gives L + W = 15; W = 6; area = 9 × 6 = 54 cm².
28. **D:** independent rolls give 1/6 × 1/6 = 1/36.
29. **A:** both true, and inertia explains the observation.
30. **B:** both true, but the Moon's reflected light does not explain respiration.
31. **C:** sound is mechanical, not electromagnetic.
32. **D:** 1 kW for 1 h is 1 kWh; watt is a power unit, not an energy amount.
33. **2:** 1 A = 1 C/s.
34. **36 W:** P = VI = 12 × 3.
35. **36:** first n odd positive integers sum to n² = 6².
36. **₹400:** 20% of 500 is 100; 500 − 100.
37. **18 h:** four tablets mean three intervals; 3 × 6.
38. **Buoyant** (upthrust).
39. **Cerebellum:** other brain areas can also affect coordination.
40. **Carbon dioxide / CO₂.**
