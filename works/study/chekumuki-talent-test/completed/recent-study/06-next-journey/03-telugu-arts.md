# తెలుగు: జానపదం, సాహిత్య రూపాలు, అర్థం 🎭

These are introductory distinctions and examples, not a complete history of Telugu literature. Regional traditions vary; classifications and spellings may vary between textbooks.

## సాహిత్యం మరియు ప్రదర్శన

1. **జానపదం (folk tradition):** stories, songs, sayings and arts carried among communities, often orally. They can have many local versions; not every folk work has a known single author.
2. **జానపద గీతం:** a folk song associated with community life, work, festivals or storytelling. Oral transmission can change words and tune across places and generations.
3. **బుర్రకథ:** a Telugu narrative performance tradition using spoken narration, song and music, often performed by a small group. The story, dialogue and rhythm work together.
4. **ఒగ్గుకథ:** a traditional storytelling performance associated especially with Telangana, combining narration, song and musical accompaniment. Performers tell stories including those of Mallanna and other deities; traditions differ.
5. **తోలుబొమ్మలాట:** leather shadow-puppet theatre. Puppets between a light source and a screen create moving shadows while performers narrate and provide music.
6. **నాటకం:** drama written or performed through characters, dialogue and action. A script is not necessarily a novel; the same story can be adapted into different forms.
7. **ఏకపాత్రాభినయం:** a performance in which one performer portrays a role or roles. Don't assume that one performer means a story must have only one character.
8. **వ్యంగ్యం (satire):** expression that uses irony or ridicule to expose a fault, often social. The intended meaning may differ from a sentence's literal words; context matters.
9. **ఆత్మకథ (autobiography):** an account of a person's own life written by that person. **జీవిత చరిత్ర (biography):** an account of a person's life written by another person.
10. **అనువాదం:** transferring meaning from one language into another. Good translation attends to context and idiom rather than replacing words mechanically.
11. **అవధానం:** a literary performance involving attention to several simultaneous tasks or questioners. **అష్టావధానం** commonly involves eight questioners; the performance's detailed rules and tasks require further study.
12. **ఛందస్సు (prosody):** principles of rhythm and metrical structure in verse. Not all poetry must follow the same traditional metre; free verse may use other patterns.

## అర్థం వాక్యంలో కనిపిస్తుంది

13. **ఉపసర్గం:** an element placed before a base word in certain word formations. Example: **అతి + వేగం = అతివేగం**, excessive speed. Don't split every word at random.
14. **పర్యాయపదం:** a word with a similar meaning in context. **వ్యతిరేకపదం:** a word with opposite meaning. Example: వెలుగు ↔ చీకటి (light ↔ darkness).
15. **నానార్థం:** one word can have multiple meanings; a sentence provides clues. A dictionary meaning that makes no sense in the passage is not a good answer just because the word can have it elsewhere.
16. **సంధి:** joining sounds/word boundaries according to grammatical rules. **సమాసం:** a compound formed by combining words with a relationship between their meanings. They are distinct concepts, though a particular expression may involve both.
17. **సారాంశం (summary):** state the main idea briefly without adding unsupported details. “చెట్లు నీడనిస్తాయి; పక్షులకు ఆశ్రయమిస్తాయి” → “చెట్లు మనకూ పక్షులకూ ఉపయోగపడతాయి.”
18. **అభిప్రాయం vs వాస్తవం:** an opinion expresses a judgement; a factual claim can be checked against evidence. “ఈ పాట నాకు బాగుంది” is an opinion; a specific publication date is a checkable claim.

## Quick identification

- A lit screen and leather figures → **తోలుబొమ్మలాట**.
- A person writes their own life story → **ఆత్మకథ**.
- A community's orally transmitted song → **జానపద గీతం**.
- Eight questioners in a literary attention performance → **అష్టావధానం**.
