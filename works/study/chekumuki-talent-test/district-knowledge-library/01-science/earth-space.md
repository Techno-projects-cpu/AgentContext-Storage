# Earth, environment and space 🌍🚀

1. **Rotation:** Earth spins on its axis in about 24 hours. Day and night occur as different areas face the Sun.
2. **Revolution:** Earth orbits the Sun in about 365¼ days. The calendar uses leap-year rules to keep dates aligned with seasons.
3. **Seasons:** mainly Earth's tilted axis combined with its orbit, not a simple change in distance from the Sun.
4. **Moon phases:** we see different portions of the Moon's sunlit half. Ordinary phases are not caused by Earth's shadow.
5. **Eclipses:** solar eclipse requires Moon between Sun and Earth; lunar eclipse requires Earth between Sun and Moon. Exact alignment matters.
6. **Sun safety:** never look directly at the Sun or through unfiltered binoculars, a telescope or camera viewfinder. Follow teacher and scientific safety guidance.
7. **Planet vs star:** the Sun is a star that produces energy by fusion; Earth is a planet orbiting it. The Moon is Earth's natural satellite.
8. **Chandrayaan-3:** ISRO's Indian lunar mission achieved a soft landing in 2023. It is not the Mars Orbiter Mission.
9. **Mangalyaan:** India's Mars Orbiter Mission orbited Mars; it did not land on Mars.
10. **Aditya-L1:** an Indian mission to study the Sun. Its name is a clue to the Sun, not a Mars mission.
11. **Water cycle:** evaporation and transpiration add water vapour; cooling helps condensation form cloud droplets or ice; precipitation returns water.
12. **Weather / climate:** weather describes short-term conditions; climate describes long-term patterns. One cold day does not disprove climate change.
13. **Wind:** air moves because pressure varies; warm air can rise and changes in atmospheric heating help drive circulation.
14. **Ozone:** stratospheric ozone absorbs much harmful ultraviolet radiation. Ground-level ozone is an air pollutant.
15. **Greenhouse effect:** certain gases absorb and re-emit infrared radiation. Increasing greenhouse gas concentrations contribute to global warming; the ozone hole is a different issue.
16. **Renewable energy:** sunlight, wind and flowing water can generate electricity; infrastructure still has environmental effects. Coal and petroleum are non-renewable.
17. **Soil:** contains mineral particles, organic matter, water, air and organisms. Plants benefit from healthy soil; erosion can remove topsoil.
18. **Water treatment:** filtration can remove some particles; boiling kills many germs, but neither necessarily removes dissolved harmful chemicals. Follow local guidance.
19. **Biodiversity:** preserving a range of habitats supports plants, animals and microbes. An ecosystem includes living things and their physical environment.
20. **Scientific method:** observations inform a question; test a prediction, measure and repeat. Results can be surprising; don't change them to fit expectations.

### Why questions

- Why can we see lightning before hearing thunder? **Light travels much faster than sound.**
- Why can't a spacecraft crew hear sound directly through empty space? **Sound needs a material medium.**
- Why are ozone depletion and global warming not identical? **They involve different atmospheric mechanisms**, though some substances can affect both.
