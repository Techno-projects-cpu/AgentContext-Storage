# Next-journey practice: 24 original questions

Independent practice—not an official paper or district-format prediction. Answers and reasoning are in [a separate file](07-answers.md).

1. Eight bits make one: A) byte B) metre C) litre D) kilogram
2. Binary 101 represents decimal: A) 8 B) 10 C) 3 D) 5
3. Which is generally volatile working memory? A) SSD storage B) Printed book C) RAM D) Paper file
4. A web browser and a search engine are: A) unrelated to finding web resources B) different tools with different functions C) both physical cables D) always the same program
5. A message says “share your OTP to claim a prize.” Best response? A) Refuse and independently verify B) Send your password instead C) Share immediately D) Post it publicly
6. Which is a useful backup? A) An unverified screenshot of a password B) A deleted original with no copy C) The only existing copy D) A separate copy that can be restored
7. Rock broken down in place is an example of: A) evaporation B) vaccination C) weathering D) erosion by transport
8. Soil carried away by a stream is an example of: A) condensation B) erosion C) multiplication D) pasteurization
9. An area draining toward a common outlet is a: A) watershed B) byte C) simile D) discount
10. Excess nutrients in water can lead to: A) guaranteed drinking safety B) creation of new elements C) stopping all algae D) eutrophication
11. Which can help bread dough rise? A) A barometer B) A printed receipt C) Carbon dioxide from yeast fermentation D) Zinc coating
12. Pasteurization primarily aims to: A) make milk a metal B) reduce harmful microbes by controlled heating C) remove all water D) guarantee food never spoils
13. తోలుబొమ్మలాటలో ప్రధానంగా ఏమి ఉపయోగిస్తారు? A) తోలుబొమ్మలు, వెలుగు, తెర B) వర్షమాపకం C) రక్త నమూనా D) కంపాస్ మాత్రమే
14. ఒక వ్యక్తి తన జీవితాన్ని తానే రాసుకుంటే అది: A) వాతావరణ పటం B) గణిత పట్టిక C) నిఘంటువు మాత్రమే D) ఆత్మకథ
15. జానపద గీతానికి సరిపోయే వివరణ? A) గ్రహం B) రసాయన మూలకం C) సమాజంలో తరతరాలుగా పాడబడే పాట D) తప్పనిసరిగా ఒకే రచయిత ఉన్న కంప్యూటర్ కోడ్
16. అష్టావధానంలో సాధారణంగా ఎంతమంది ప్రశ్నకర్తలు? A) నాలుగు B) ఎనిమిది C) రెండు D) ఒకరు
17. Latitude is measured north or south of the: A) Equator B) Prime Meridian C) local road D) Tropic of Cancer only
18. The Prime Meridian is: A) 90° latitude B) 180° latitude C) the Equator D) 0° longitude
19. On a map, 1 cm represents 5 km. Four centimetres represents: A) 40 km B) 9 km C) 20 km D) 1.25 km
20. On the same contour map, closely spaced contours generally show: A) a railway station B) steeper slope C) lower rainfall always D) flat ground
21. A 500 g pack costs ₹60. Its price per kilogram is: A) ₹120 B) ₹500 C) ₹30 D) ₹60
22. ₹200 discounted by 10% twice successively becomes: A) ₹180 B) ₹190 C) ₹160 D) ₹162
23. Sales of two products rise in summer. This alone proves: A) heat is impossible B) neither caused the other C) neither causal direction is established D) one caused the other
24. Pictures show a leather puppet, a lamp and its shadow on a screen. Intended connection? A) longitude B) తోలుబొమ్మలాట C) aquifer D) byte
