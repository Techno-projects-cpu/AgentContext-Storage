🚌 *CHEKUMUKI BUS BOOST 5 — THINK, DON'T GUESS!*

Copy this standalone message for the bus. A different format: compact comparisons, worked traps, picture links and a mini quiz. Some fundamentals intentionally overlap earlier packs. Independent practice, not an official paper or prediction.

*1️⃣ SIMILAR WORDS, DIFFERENT ANSWERS*

1. Mass vs weight: mass measures inertia/amount of matter; weight is gravitational force and changes with gravitational field.
2. Speed vs velocity: speed is distance/time; velocity includes direction. A change in direction changes velocity.
3. Distance vs displacement: distance is total path length; displacement is directed change in position.
4. Heat vs temperature: heat is energy transferred due to a temperature difference; temperature describes thermal state.
5. Conduction vs convection: energy transfer through matter vs transfer involving bulk fluid motion. Radiation can cross a vacuum.
6. Sound vs light: sound requires a medium; electromagnetic light can travel through vacuum.
7. Pitch vs loudness: frequency mainly determines pitch; amplitude generally influences loudness, along with frequency and hearing.
8. Reflection vs refraction: light bounces at a boundary vs changes direction as speed changes between media (with exceptions for direction).
9. Power vs energy: watts measure rate of energy transfer; kilowatt-hours measure energy.
10. Series vs parallel: simple series components have one path; parallel components have separate paths and the same voltage across each branch.
11. Element vs compound: one kind of atom vs elements chemically combined in fixed proportions.
12. Compound vs mixture: chemically combined in fixed proportions vs substances physically combined in variable proportions.
13. Evaporation vs boiling: evaporation at liquid surface below boiling; boiling throughout liquid at its boiling conditions.
14. Filtration vs distillation: separates insoluble particles vs boils and condenses to collect a liquid.
15. Atom vs molecule: atom is a unit of an element; molecule is a group of bonded atoms, which can be the same or different elements.
16. Mutation vs adaptation: mutation is a DNA sequence change; adaptation is an inherited feature that can aid survival/reproduction in an environment.
17. Pollination vs fertilisation: pollen reaches stigma vs reproductive cells fuse.
18. Photosynthesis vs respiration: photosynthesis makes sugars using light energy; respiration releases energy from food molecules.
19. Artery vs vein: carries blood away from heart vs toward heart. These are definitions by direction.
20. Breathing vs cellular respiration: moving air in/out vs chemical processes that release energy in cells.
21. Weather vs climate: short-term conditions vs long-term patterns.
22. Focus vs epicentre: earthquake starts inside Earth vs point vertically above on surface.
23. Magnitude vs intensity: source size vs local shaking/effects.
24. Asteroid vs comet: generally rocky/metallic small body vs body containing ice and dust that can develop a coma and tail near the Sun.
25. Meteor vs meteorite: light streak in atmosphere vs material reaching ground.
26. Latitude vs longitude: north/south of Equator vs east/west of Prime Meridian.
27. Endemic species vs invasive species: native and restricted to a place vs introduced, spreading and causing harm.
28. Food chain vs food web: one feeding pathway vs interconnected pathways.
29. Bioaccumulation vs biomagnification: build-up within one organism over time vs increasing concentration across trophic levels for some pollutants.
30. Observation vs inference: recorded evidence vs an interpretation of it. Don't mistake a guess for a measurement.

*2️⃣ QUESTION TRAPS — NOTICE THE WORDING*

31. “40 is 200% OF 20” is true. “20 to 40 increased by 200%” is false: increase is 100%.
32. Rate 40% → 50%: +10 percentage points, or +25% relative to the original 40%.
33. ₹200 +10% −10% → ₹220 − ₹22 = ₹198, not ₹200.
34. A journey at 40 km/h and another at 60 km/h does not always have average speed 50 km/h: time/distance on each leg matter.
35. Equal times at 40 and 60 km/h → 50 km/h average. Equal distances → 48 km/h average (total 120 km divided by 1.5+1 hours, if each leg is 60 km).
36. Five values 2, 3, 3, 4, 18: mean 6; median 3; mode 3. A large outlier shifts the mean.
37. Ten students average 60; twenty average 75 → combined average (600+1500)/30 = 70, not 67.5.
38. Perimeter of 8 cm × 5 cm rectangle = 26 cm; area = 40 cm². Don't mix units.
39. 1 kg of cotton and 1 kg of iron have equal mass. Their volumes differ because their densities differ.
40. More massive object does not always have greater density: density = mass ÷ volume.
41. “All metals are magnetic” is false: copper is not attracted to a magnet like iron.
42. Moon phases are not caused by Earth's shadow. A lunar eclipse involves Earth's shadow.
43. Bats are mammals that fly; whales are mammals that live in water. Habitat alone does not decide classification.
44. Not all bacteria cause disease; some help digestion, food production or nutrient cycling.
45. Antibiotics do not kill viruses like the common cold virus. Take medicines only as advised by a qualified professional.
46. A disease vector transmits a pathogen; it is not always the pathogen itself. Malaria is caused by a parasite.
47. A single fever symptom is not a diagnosis; seek qualified medical advice.
48. A higher-pitched sound is not necessarily louder. Frequency and amplitude answer different questions.
49. Satellite navigation and satellite photography are different uses of satellites.
50. A prediction that fits one event does not establish a general rule; repeat observations and consider alternatives.

*3️⃣ TELUGU MINI-RECALL*

51. సుమతీ శతకం → బద్దెన. మకుటం = recurring word or phrase in verses.
52. దాశరథీ శతకం → కంచర్ల గోపన్న / రామదాసు, not దాశరథి కృష్ణమాచార్య.
53. ఆంధ్ర మహాభాగవతం → పోతన. ఆముక్తమాల్యద → శ్రీకృష్ణదేవరాయలు.
54. మహాప్రస్థానం → శ్రీశ్రీ. కన్యాశుల్కం → గురజాడ అప్పారావు. గబ్బిలం → గుర్రం జాషువా.
55. నా గొడవ → కాళోజీ నారాయణరావు. బసవ పురాణం → పాల్కురికి సోమనాథుడు.
56. ఉపమాలంకారం = explicit comparison: “ముఖం చంద్రుని వలె ఉంది.”
57. రూపకంలో one thing is described as another figuratively. Context matters.
58. “రాము బంతిని విసిరాడు” → కర్త రాము; కర్మ బంతిని; క్రియ విసిరాడు.
59. ఆత్మకథ = own life written by oneself; జీవిత చరిత్ర = account of someone's life by another.
60. బుర్రకథ / ఒగ్గుకథ = narrative performance traditions; తోలుబొమ్మలాట = leather shadow-puppet theatre.

*5️⃣ QUICK PRACTICE: TRY BEFORE LOOKING*

1. 1 kg cotton vs 1 kg iron: which has greater mass?
2. 20 to 40: what is the percentage increase?
3. What is the median of 2, 3, 3, 4, 18?
4. What is average speed for equal times at 40 and 60 km/h?
5. A lens or a mirror reflects light?
6. Is a mosquito the malaria pathogen or a vector?
7. What is the difference between pollen transfer and cell fusion called?
8. What does an epicentre mark?
9. “ముఖం చంద్రుని వలె” indicates which అలంకారం?

*✅ ANSWERS*
1. Equal mass. 2. 100%. 3. 3. 4. 50 km/h. 5. Mirror. 6. Vector; Plasmodium parasite causes malaria. 7. Pollination vs fertilisation. 8. Surface point vertically above earthquake focus. 9. ఉపమాలంకారం. 10. Numismatics.

Keep questioning: look for words like “always”, “only”, “increase”, “of”, “at the surface” and “per unit”.
