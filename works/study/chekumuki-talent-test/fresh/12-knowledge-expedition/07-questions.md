# Knowledge Expedition: 30 practice questions

Original independent practice, not an official paper. Choose one answer and then check the [separate explained key](08-answers.md). Some picture questions describe imagined images.

1. A 10-tooth gear drives a 20-tooth gear. How many driver revolutions give one driven revolution in an ideal pair? A) Two B) One C) Four D) Half
2. Two externally meshing gears rotate: A) Always in the same direction B) Without touching C) Only clockwise D) In opposite directions
3. What helps reduce wear between appropriate moving parts? A) Increasing rubbing B) Removing bearings in all cases C) Lubrication D) Adding sand
4. A lever balances 10 N at 2 m from a pivot against: A) 40 N at 1 m B) 20 N at 1 m C) 10 N at 1 m D) 5 N at 1 m
5. A refrigerator normally moves heat: A) From its interior to the room using energy B) From the room into the interior without power C) Into nowhere D) Only into outer space
6. During melting at normal pressure, ice near 0°C: A) Must immediately reach 100°C B) Loses all its mass C) Stops absorbing energy D) Can absorb energy while its temperature stays near 0°C
7. Evaporation of sweat can cool skin because: A) Heat cannot move B) Water becomes solid C) Evaporating water carries energy away D) Water creates cold energy
8. A solar cell primarily converts light into: A) A new element B) Electrical energy C) Soil D) Sound only
9. Antibodies are: A) Proteins that can bind specific targets B) Types of metal C) Units of distance D) Weather instruments
10. Antibiotic resistance occurs when: A) The person's body becomes resistant to all medicines B) Viruses become bacteria C) All antibiotics treat common colds D) Bacteria change so antibiotics become less effective
11. A zoonosis is an infectious disease that can pass between: A) Two planets only B) Machines and magnets C) Animals and people D) Clouds and rocks
12. Which reduces cross-contamination? A) Assume invisible microbes cannot spread B) Separate raw food from ready-to-eat food C) Use the same unwashed board for both D) Keep cooked food under raw meat
13. A randomised trial assigns participants to groups: A) By chance B) By alphabetical order only C) According to favourite colour D) Without a study plan
14. Risk falls from 4 in 100 to 2 in 100. Absolute reduction? A) 50 percentage points B) 4 percentage points C) 2 percent of 2 D) 2 percentage points
15. At a divergent plate boundary, plates generally: A) Become stationary forever B) Disappear without movement C) Move apart D) Move towards each other
16. An estuary is a coastal area where: A) Every drop is equally salty always B) Freshwater and seawater mix C) Only ice is found D) No tides are possible
17. A tributary joins a larger river. The meeting place is a: A) Confluence B) Epicentre C) Convex lens D) Algorithm
18. A rain shadow can arise when: A) Rivers turn into steel B) Wind always stops at mountains C) All rain falls only at sea D) Moist air rises over mountains and descending air becomes drier
19. Coral bleaching means corals: A) Become plants B) Produce snow C) Can lose symbiotic partners or pigments under stress D) Always die instantly
20. దేవ + ఆలయం gives: A) దీవాలయం B) దేవాలయం C) దేవలయం మాత్రమే D) ఆలయదేవ
21. దేవ + ఆలయం → దేవాలయం is an example of: A) సవర్ణదీర్ఘ సంధి B) ప్రశ్నార్థకం C) క్రియ D) సంఖ్య
22. రాముడు మరియు లక్ష్మణుడు → రామలక్ష్మణులు is: A) వాక్యాంత చిహ్నం B) ఉపమాలంకారం C) ఒక సంఖ్య D) ద్వంద్వ సమాసం
23. “పచ్చని చెట్టు”లో విశేషణం ఏది? A) లో B) ఏది C) పచ్చని D) చెట్టు
24. “పక్షి ఎగిరింది”లో క్రియ ఏది? A) ఏది B) ఎగిరింది C) పక్షి D) లో
25. 1/3 + 1/6 = ? A) 1/2 B) 2/9 C) 1/9 D) 2/6
26. ₹350 in ratio 2:5 gives: A) ₹150 and ₹200 B) ₹70 and ₹280 C) ₹175 each D) ₹100 and ₹250
27. ₹240 after 20% discount means the original price was: A) ₹200 B) ₹260 C) ₹300 D) ₹288
28. Tap A fills 1/6 tank/hour and B fills 1/3 tank/hour. Together at constant rates they fill a tank in: A) 9 hours B) 2 hours C) 3 hours D) 6 hours
29. A rectangle is 12 m by 5 m. Its area is: A) 60 m² B) 34 m² C) 17 m² D) 60 m
30. Picture clues: moist air + mountain + drier far side. Intended connection? A) Blood group B) Chromatography C) Refraction only D) Rain shadow
