# Knowledge Expedition: a new regular study pack 🧭

This is **reading material**, not another WhatsApp bus sheet. The existing bus packs stay at their old paths. We searched the archived and active text for candidate topics and selected relatively under-covered angles. A word search cannot detect every synonym, image or question, so overlap is possible. Examples build on essential foundations when useful.

3. [Land, rivers and coasts](03-land-water.md) — plate movement, rain shadows, rivers, estuaries and conservation.

## How to read

Each card gives an idea, an example and a limitation. Read one section at a time; try the questions before opening the key. Competition formats and current facts can change: use current official sources and your teacher's syllabus. Medical and safety notes do not replace professional advice.
