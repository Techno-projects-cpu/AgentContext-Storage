# Everyday science: 24 practice questions

Independent practice based on observations and mechanisms; **not an official paper or key**. Pick one answer before opening the [separate explained answers](05-answers.md).

1. The formula `K₂SO₄·Al₂(SO₄)₃·24H₂O` corresponds to twice which potash-alum formula unit? A) KAl(SO₄)₂·12H₂O B) FeCl₃ C) NaCl D) CaCO₃
2. The 12H₂O in `KAl(SO₄)₂·12H₂O` denotes: A) Oxygen gas B) 12 potassium ions C) Water pumped into blood vessels D) Water of crystallisation
3. In the mandal alum question, the intended colloid idea is: A) All iron turns into gold B) Water boils inside the wound C) Al³⁺ can help destabilise colloidal particles and promote aggregation D) Alum pumps blood upstream
4. Which is a safer first-aid principle for a bleeding injury? A) Apply unknown chemicals B) Apply direct pressure with a clean dressing and seek help if needed C) Put laboratory reagents in the wound D) Ignore heavy bleeding
5. Methyl orange in sufficiently acidic lemon juice can appear: A) Red B) Metallic silver C) Blue in all conditions D) Invisible in every condition
6. Why can an acid–base indicator change colour? A) Its atoms turn into blood B) It becomes a magnet C) All acids contain red dye D) Its protonated and unprotonated forms absorb light differently
7. In the methyl-orange mechanism, iron(III) chloride is: A) The same chemical as citric acid B) Produced from every lemon C) Not required D) Always required
8. Which ions can form a reddish complex in the other mechanism? A) Calcium and pure sugar only B) Fe³⁺ and SCN⁻ C) Na⁺ and Cl⁻ alone D) H₂ and O₂ alone
9. Ferric chloride is: A) FeCl₃ B) Fe₂O₃ C) KSCN D) C₆H₈O₇
10. Which conclusion is justified by seeing a red liquid alone? A) It must be human blood B) It must be FeSCN²⁺ C) No chemical could be involved D) A red appearance alone does not identify the substance
11. Citric acid's formula is: A) NaOCl B) KSCN C) C₆H₈O₇ D) FeCl₃
12. Aluminium-containing flocs can help muddy water by: A) Removing every dissolved chemical automatically B) Trapping and settling some suspended particles C) Guaranteeing all water is safe D) Turning all microbes into oxygen
13. Bacteria help form curd by: A) Producing acid that changes milk-protein interactions B) Turning milk into iron C) Producing sand D) Removing all water
14. Which gas helps yeast dough rise? A) Chlorine B) Helium in all cases C) Ozone D) Carbon dioxide
15. In soap micelles, the oil-attracted portions help: A) Make water cease to flow B) Ensure every pathogen dies instantly C) Associate with oily dirt D) Convert oil into metal
16. Hard water can reduce ordinary soap lather because of: A) Absence of all dissolved material B) Calcium and magnesium ions forming poorly soluble salts C) Pure oxygen only D) Nitrogen fixing in soap
17. Reddish-brown turmeric in soapy water is consistent with: A) Curcumin responding to a sufficiently basic medium B) Turmeric becoming blood C) A magnetic reaction D) Water becoming an element
18. Why can an earthen pot cool water? A) It manufactures cold B) It heats water by friction C) Gravity destroys heat D) Water evaporating from its porous exterior carries energy away
19. Drops outside a cold glass usually come from: A) Oxygen turning into water without hydrogen B) Ice travelling through the table C) Water vapour in air condensing on the surface D) Water passing through solid glass
20. A metal spoon and wood at the same room temperature can feel different because: A) Temperature is identical to thermal conductivity B) They transfer heat from your hand at different rates C) Metal is always colder by definition D) Wood contains no atoms
21. Which household mixture is unsafe? A) Bleach with acid or ammonia cleaners B) Soap and water used as directed C) Rice and water for cooking D) Air and daylight
22. The school's urea/urease question suggests red litmus turns blue because: A) Urease makes iron B) Urea creates a magnet C) Litmus detects all food hazards specifically D) Ammonia can make the mixture basic
23. Which is an appropriate scientific control question? A) Does red always mean blood? B) Is an explanation unnecessary? C) Does an untreated otherwise similar sample show the effect? D) Can the performer repeat the story louder?
24. Picture clues: raw food + unwashed board + salad. What is the risk? A) Weathering B) Cross-contamination C) Solar eclipse D) Magnetic attraction
