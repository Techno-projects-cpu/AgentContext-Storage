# Everyday science: what is REALLY happening? 🔍

You were right: JVV-style science is not just space facts or definitions. It asks you to see a familiar event—a lemon turning red, a wound, soap, milk, a pressure cooker—and reason about **particles, chemistry, observations and misleading claims**.

This is a **regular reading pack**, not another bus pack. Start here:

1. [Your actual paper questions: alum and the “bleeding” lemon](01-paper-questions.md) — formula, ions, reactions, two different red-colour mechanisms and honest limits.
2. [Kitchen and household science](02-home-and-food.md) — curd, milk, soap, food reactions, rust, fire and water with molecular explanations.
3. [Apparent miracles and scientific tests](03-miracles-evidence.md) — indicators, adulteration questions, controls and how not to fall for a claim.
4. [Practice: 24 everyday-science questions](04-practice.md) · [Separate explained answers](05-answers.md).

**How to read a card:** What do you observe? → What particles or forces could explain it? → What evidence would distinguish alternatives? → What is the limit or safety warning?

The school and mandal originals and their analyses are preserved in [completed/paper-workout](../../completed/paper-workout/README.md). The original papers show school/mandal question styles, not proof of district topics or rules. This pack is independent, not an official answer key. Some topics appeared in earlier work but are now developed from observations to mechanisms, because this connection matters.

**Safety:** chemical demonstrations are explanations, not instructions to perform them. Never put laboratory reagents on skin or food, taste or consume treated items, or handle sharp blades for a trick. First aid and drinking-water treatment need qualified guidance; clear water is not necessarily safe.
