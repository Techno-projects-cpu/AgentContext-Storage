### Telugu language warm-up

The notice names Telugu but does not specify grammar or difficulty. Practise reading a short paragraph from your state textbook, give its main idea, find synonyms/antonyms from context and explain one science word in Telugu. Do not assume these are the actual Telugu questions.

## 10-second oral clues

1. What shows the smallest unit of a living organism? **Cell.**
2. Which lens is thicker at the edges? **Concave.**
3. Which graph feature can mislead a reader? **A truncated or uneven scale.**
4. Which flower part receives pollen? **Stigma.**
5. What do two guard cells surround? **A stomatal pore.**
6. Why is an eye shown on a warning? **Read its actual symbol and label; the eye alone is not a universal hazard category.**
7. What are the components between nodes in separate circuit paths called? **Parallel branches.**
8. Which way does an artery carry blood? **Away from the heart.**
