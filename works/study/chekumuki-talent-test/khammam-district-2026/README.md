## Pick your route

1. **45 minutes:** [Knowledge cards](01-knowledge.md) → 15 minutes; [visual lightning](02-visual-and-telugu.md) → 10; [safe practicals](03-activities-experiments.md) → 10; explain five mistakes aloud → 10.
2. **Two hours:** Read all three guides, take the [40-question mock](04-mock.md), and use its [separate answer sheet](05-answers.md) to review.
3. **Team rehearsal:** One person reads each clue, another answers, a third gives a reason; rotate. Try activities in 03 and visual clues in 02. This rotation is a practice method, not an official rule.
4. **Final morning:** Review weak answers, formula cards, Telugu glosses and safety. Sleep; bring materials your teacher requests.
