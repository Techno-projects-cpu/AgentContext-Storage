## Life and health

1. **Cell (కణం)** = a basic unit of living organisms; plants and animals are made of cells.
2. **Cell membrane** = selectively controls movement into and out; plant and animal cells both have it.
3. **Cell wall** = extra support outside plant-cell membranes; plant walls contain cellulose.
4. **Nucleus** = contains most DNA in a typical plant or animal cell. Bacteria have DNA but no membrane-bound nucleus.
5. **Mitochondria** = help cells release usable energy from food; plants have them too.
6. **Ribosomes** make proteins; chloroplasts conduct photosynthesis in cells that have them.
7. **DNA → RNA → protein** = a useful simplified information pathway; some genes produce functional RNA instead of protein.
8. **Photosynthesis:** plants use light, CO₂ and water to make sugars; released oxygen comes from water.
9. **Respiration:** cells release usable energy from food; plants respire day and night.
10. **Xylem** carries water/minerals; **phloem** carries sugars; **stomata** help gas exchange.
11. **Osmosis** = net movement of water through a selectively permeable membrane; raisins can swell in water.
12. **Diffusion** = net spreading from higher to lower concentration; particles still move in both directions.
13. **Artery** carries blood away from heart; **vein** towards heart. Pulmonary artery is an oxygen-poor exception to “arteries carry oxygen-rich blood”.
14. **Red blood cells** carry oxygen mainly using haemoglobin; **platelets** help blood clot.
15. **Cerebellum** helps balance and coordination; **hypothalamus** helps regulate body temperature.
16. **Antibiotics** act against susceptible bacteria, not influenza virus itself. A doctor decides treatment.
17. **Vaccines** stimulate immune responses; **antiserum** supplies preformed antibodies.
18. **Menstruation** is a normal biological process; it is not a reason to exclude a student from school.
19. **Food chain:** fewer frogs may mean more grasshoppers and less food for predators, other factors being equal.
20. **Fungi:** mushroom and yeast are examples; yeast releases CO₂ during fermentation.

## Physics and chemistry

21. **Inertia (జడత్వం):** a body tends to maintain its motion unless a net force changes it; passengers lean forward when a bus brakes.
22. **Pressure = force ÷ area.** A sharp knife can exert greater pressure with the same force because its edge contacts less area.
23. **Buoyancy / upthrust** = upward force from a fluid; a floating object's weight equals the weight of displaced fluid when at rest.
24. **Speed = distance ÷ time;** average speed = total distance ÷ total time.
25. **Sound needs a medium;** light can travel through vacuum.
26. **Echo:** distance to wall = speed × total echo time ÷ 2, because sound goes out and back.
27. **Light in a uniform medium** travels in straight lines; shadows and pinhole images illustrate this.
28. **Frequency stays the same** when light crosses into another medium; speed and wavelength change.
29. **Current (విద్యుత్ ప్రవాహం)** = charge per second, measured in amperes (A).
30. **Voltage (వోల్టేజ్)** = energy transferred per unit charge; 1 volt = 1 joule per coulomb.
31. **Resistance:** V = IR for an ohmic conductor under suitable constant conditions; I = V/R.
32. **Power (శక్తి వినియోగ రేటు)** = energy per time; P = VI for a DC circuit. Watts measure power, not total energy.
33. **Electricity bill:** energy in kWh = power in kW × time in hours. 100 W for 5 h = 0.5 kWh.
34. **Series circuit:** one path and same steady current; a break stops the loop. Parallel branches have the same voltage across each branch in the ideal circuit.
35. **Acid + base:** neutralisation forms salt and water in simple school examples; acids can be hazardous. Never mix cleaners.
36. **pH:** lower means more acidic; at 25°C neutral pure water is pH 7. A one-unit difference means tenfold hydrogen-ion activity.
37. **Physical change:** ice melts but remains water. **Chemical change:** rusting forms new substances.
38. **Catalyst** changes reaction rate without being consumed overall; enzymes are biological catalysts.
39. **Graphite** conducts electricity; solid salt generally does not, but dissolved salt conducts because its ions can move.
40. **Alum** can help suspended particles clump in water treatment; that alone does not guarantee safe drinking water.

## Maths, evidence and safety

41. **Percentage:** part/original × 100. 20 → 40 is a **100% increase**, but 40 is **200% of** 20.
42. **Successive discounts:** ₹1,000 reduced by 50% becomes ₹500; 60% off that leaves **₹200**, not a 110% discount.
43. **Average** = total ÷ number of observations. Five marks average 18 → total 90.
44. **Intervals:** 15 tablets starting immediately and then every 6 h → 14 intervals → 84 h. This is arithmetic, not medical advice.
45. **Square numbers:** 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100.
46. **Matching:** label each pair before choosing the option; do not rush from familiar names to a letter.
47. **Experiment:** independent variable = what you change; dependent variable = what you measure; keep other important conditions comparable.
48. **Scientific temper (శాస్త్రీయ దృక్పథం):** ask for reliable evidence, check sources, consider alternatives and revise when evidence changes.
49. **Cyber safety:** never share OTPs; verify unexpected prize requests independently. Report suspected financial cyber-fraud promptly via bank and 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in.
50. **Earthquake:** drop, cover under sturdy furniture and hold on during shaking when appropriate; follow local emergency guidance.
