## Answers and quick reasoning

1. **Silver.** Tokyo 2020 was gold.
2. **Six:** one silver and five bronze.
3. **29:** seven gold, nine silver and 13 bronze.
4. **Italy.** Milano Cortina 2026 was the Winter Olympics.
5. **Aichi-Nagoya, Japan.**
6. **Chikitha Taniparthi.** Team and mixed-team golds.
7. **New Zealand.** India won the 2026 final.
8. **South Africa.** India's first women's ODI World Cup title.
9. **Francis Halzen; IceCube Neutrino Observatory.**
10. **Navanethem “Navi” Pillay.**
11. **Chandrayaan-3.**
12. **Aditya-L1.** It does not land on the Sun.
13. **Satellite rendezvous and docking/undocking.**
14. **Distance.** It is the distance light travels in vacuum in a year.
15. **Earth's axial tilt and revolution**, changing sunlight angle and day length.
16. **Near a new moon**, with suitable alignment.
17. **Cell membrane.** Plant cells also have a cell wall.
18. **Proteins.**
19. **Yes.** Respiration continues day and night.
20. **Xylem.** Phloem carries sugars.
21. **Carbon dioxide.**
22. **Antibiotics target bacterial structures or processes**, not the influenza virus itself.
23. **Artery.** Direction is relative to the heart; oxygen content varies.
24. **Cerebellum.**
25. **They may increase**, because fewer frogs may eat them; ecosystems are more complex.
26. **Inertia.** The body tends to retain its motion; the bus slows.
27. **Sharp knife.** Smaller area at the same force gives higher pressure.
28. **2 A:** 12/6.
29. **0.5 kWh:** 0.1 kW × 5 h.
30. **68 m:** 340 × 0.4/2; sound goes out and back.
31. **Physical:** ice and liquid water are the same substance.
32. **Ions.**
33. **100%:** the increase is 20, equal to the original 20. The final amount is 200% *of* the original.
34. **₹200:** 1,000 × 0.5 × 0.4.
35. **84 h:** 14 intervals × 6 h.
36. **90:** 5 × 18.
37. **Independent:** it is the factor deliberately changed.
38. **Water is a confounder:** more than one factor changed.
39. **Somatic spinal reflex.**
40. **Don't share it.** Independently verify; report suspected fraud to the bank and appropriate authorities.
