# Telugu literary GK: names, songs, books & clues 📚

Read each arrow both ways: **work → author** and **author → work**. Titles and claims such as “first” or “father” may vary between sources; learn the precise achievement, not just a label.

## Songs: don't mix the writers

1. **జన గణ మన (Jana Gana Mana)** → **రవీంద్రనాథ్ ఠాగూర్ (Rabindranath Tagore)**. India's national anthem; adopted 24 January 1950. [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Jana-Gana-Mana)
2. **వందే మాతరం (Vande Mataram)** → **బంకిమ్ చంద్ర చటర్జీ (Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay)**. India's national song; it appears in *Anandamath*. Do not confuse the author with Tagore.
3. **జయ జయహే తెలంగాణ (Jaya Jaya He Telangana)** → **అందెశ్రీ (Ande Sri)**, lyricist. Telangana's official state song. [Telangana government order](https://www.telangana.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Telangana-State-Song.pdf)
4. **మా తెలుగు తల్లికి (Maa Telugu Talliki)** → **శంకరంబాడి సుందరాచారి (Sankarambadi Sundarachari)**, lyricist; Andhra Pradesh state song. [Reference](https://dbpedia.org/page/Maa_Telugu_Thalliki)
5. **దేశమును ప్రేమించుమన్నా (Desamunu Preminchumanna)** → **గురజాడ అప్పారావు (Gurajada Apparao)**. Known for the idea that a nation is its people, not just its land. [Reference](https://srikaram.org/telugu-sahityamlo-kavitvam/)

## Telugu writers and their work

6. **నన్నయ (Nannaya)** → called **ఆదికవి**; began the Telugu Mahabharata.
7. **కవిత్రయం (Kavitrayam)** → **Nannaya, Tikkana and Errana (Yerrapragada)**, associated with the Telugu Mahabharata. These are three poets, not three separate epics.
8. **పోతన (Bammera Pothana)** → Telugu **ఆంధ్ర మహాభాగవతము**. Do not mix up Bhagavatam with the Mahabharata.
9. **శ్రీకృష్ణదేవరాయలు** → **ఆముక్తమాల్యద**; also a Vijayanagara ruler and patron of literature.
10. **గురజాడ అప్పారావు** → **కన్యాశుల్కం (Kanyasulkam)**, a social play; also *Desamunu Preminchumanna*.
11. **శ్రీశ్రీ (Sri Sri)** → pen name of **శ్రీరంగం శ్రీనివాసరావు**; wrote **మహాప్రస్థానం (Mahaprasthanam)**.
12. **విశ్వనాథ సత్యనారాయణ** → **వేయి పడగలు (Veyi Padagalu)** and **రామాయణ కల్పవృక్షం (Ramayana Kalpavriksham)**.
13. **సి. నారాయణ రెడ్డి (C. Narayana Reddy / సినారె)** → **విశ్వంభర (Viswambhara)**, a major poetic work associated with his Jnanpith award. [Sahitya Akademi](https://sahitya-akademi.gov.in/e-newsletter/may-june_2017_Newsletter.pdf)
14. **దాశరథి కృష్ణమాచార్య (Dasarathi Krishnamacharya)** → Telangana poet; **అగ్నిధార (Agnidhara)** and **గాలిబ్ గీతాలు (Ghalib Geetalu)**; associated with the expression “నా తెలంగాణ కోటి రతనాల వీణ”.
15. **వేమన (Vemana)** → remembered for Telugu moral and social verses, often associated with **విశ్వదాభిరామ వినుర వేమ**.
16. **గిడుగు రామమూర్తి (Gidugu Ramamurthy)** → champion of **వ్యావహారిక భాష** (everyday spoken Telugu in writing and education).
17. **గుర్రం జాషువా (Gurram Jashuva)** → **గబ్బిలం (Gabbilam)**, a work addressing social exclusion and inequality.
18. **కాళోజీ నారాయణరావు (Kaloji Narayana Rao)** → Telangana poet; **నా గొడవ (Naa Godava)**.
19. **భద్రాచల రామదాసు (Bhadrachala Ramadasu / Kancharla Gopanna)** → devotional compositions associated with Bhadrachalam and Rama.
20. **అన్నమాచార్య (Annamacharya)** → Telugu devotional compositions associated with Venkateswara of Tirumala.

## The Telugu ghazal trap ⚠️

- **దాశరథి కృష్ణమాచార్య** is described as an early/first Telugu ghazal poet in [this literary article](https://sirimalle.com/sahiti-prakriya-gazal-01/) and [this retrospective](https://www.ntnews.com/sunday/dasarathi-100th-birth-anniversary-1664919).
- **సి. నారాయణ రెడ్డి (సినారె)** helped develop and popularise the Telugu ghazal; **పి.బి. శ్రీనివాస్** is associated with early singing; **గజల్ శ్రీనివాస్ (Kesiraju Srinivas)** is a later popular ghazal performer. [Literary discussion](https://musimagazine.org/magazine/2024/04/14/%E0%B0%A4%E0%B1%86%E0%B0%B2%E0%B1%81%E0%B0%97%E0%B1%81%E0%B0%B2%E0%B1%8B-%E0%B0%97%E0%B0%9C%E0%B0%B2%E0%B1%8D-%E0%B0%AA%E0%B1%8D%E0%B0%B0%E0%B0%95%E0%B1%8D%E0%B0%B0%E0%B0%BF%E0%B0%AF/)
- **“Father of Telugu ghazal” is an ambiguous title:** we found support for Dasarathi's pioneering authorship, but did not verify that every quiz source uses that exact title for the same person. Ask the teacher for the source/key and pay attention to *poet* versus *singer* versus *populariser*. Do not confuse Telugu ghazal with “father of ghazal in India”.

## Fast retrieval

- National anthem → Tagore. National song → Bankim Chandra.
- Telangana state song → Ande Sri. Andhra Pradesh's *Maa Telugu Talliki* → Sankarambadi.
- *Kanyasulkam* → Gurajada. *Mahaprasthanam* → Sri Sri.
- *Viswambhara* → C. Narayana Reddy. *Gabbilam* → Jashuva.
- Telugu Mahabharata → Nannaya, Tikkana, Errana. Telugu Bhagavatam → Pothana.

Cross-check textbook spellings and local editions. A literary epithet may have multiple usages; these are not claims about a complete Telugu curriculum.
