# Connect the pictures: find the bridge! 🖼️➕🖼️➕🖼️

These are **text descriptions of pictures**, not images. A teammate can sketch each item or show suitable textbook photos; keep its caption hidden if it gives away the answer. A real quiz may use photographs, logos, maps, people or more/fewer clues. Our items are original practice, not real JVV slides.

## The 4-step method

1. Say what each picture shows: person / place / object / event?
2. Look for a shared place, inventor, property, process, theme, book, institution or event.
3. State a **specific** connection and link all clues, not just two. “They are all things” is too broad.
4. Check for another reasonable answer; if the question is unclear, ask for clarification. Some visual connections are conventions or theories, not proven historical facts.

## Team drill

Choose any 10 sets. One person reads only the clues; a second gives a common answer; the third checks **every link** and proposes an alternative. Swap roles. If a photograph is blurry, request a clearer clue rather than invent details. For historical theories say “a traditional explanation” rather than claiming certainty.
