# More knowledge: easy to read, harder to fool 🧠

## Biology and health

1. **Organising life:** cell → tissue → organ → organ system → organism. Heart muscle cells form tissue; the heart is an organ.
2. **Red blood cells:** haemoglobin carries oxygen. Platelets help clot blood; white blood cells help defend against infection.
3. **Heart and lungs:** blood travels from heart to lungs and back, then is pumped to the rest of the body. The pulmonary artery carries oxygen-poor blood away from the heart.
4. **Digestion:** teeth break food into smaller pieces; the stomach helps digestion; most absorption of digested nutrients happens in the small intestine.
5. **Bones and joints:** muscles pull on bones to move them. Calcium and vitamin D support healthy bones; many other nutrients matter too.
6. **Brain and reflex:** a reflex is a rapid automatic response. The knee-jerk reflex is mediated by a spinal reflex arc involving somatic motor neurons—not simply an autonomic reflex.
7. **Diabetes:** insulin helps regulate blood glucose. Diabetes requires proper diagnosis and medical advice; a person cannot diagnose it from one quiz symptom.
8. **Vaccine vs antibiotic:** vaccines train the immune system against specific diseases; antibiotics treat certain bacterial infections, not viral flu.
9. **Nutrition:** protein helps growth and repair; carbohydrates and fats can supply energy; vitamins and minerals support many body functions.
10. **Plants:** xylem moves water/minerals mainly upwards; phloem moves sugars from sources to growing or storage regions.
11. **Plant food:** photosynthesis needs light; plants also carry out cellular respiration, including at night.
12. **Microbes:** some bacteria help make curd and recycle nutrients. Some cause disease; not all microbes are harmful.
13. **Food chain:** arrows from food to consumer indicate energy transfer. Decomposers act on dead organisms and wastes, returning nutrients.
14. **Pollination** transfers pollen to a stigma. **Fertilisation** occurs later when reproductive cells fuse.
15. **Adaptation:** a fish's gills enable gas exchange in water; fins help movement. A whale is a mammal that breathes air through lungs, not a fish.
16. **Biodiversity:** protecting forests, wetlands and native species helps preserve a variety of life; food webs connect organisms.

## Physics and chemistry

17. **Forces:** friction helps shoe soles grip the ground; lubricants reduce friction between moving parts of a machine.
18. **Inertia:** a passenger tends to move forward relative to a suddenly braking bus. Seat belts help restrain passengers.
19. **Pressure:** a sharp knife can cut more easily because force acts over a smaller area. Pressure in a liquid increases with depth.
20. **Buoyancy:** water pushes upward on an immersed object. A ship floats when its total weight is balanced by buoyant force.
21. **Energy:** a raised object has gravitational potential energy relative to a reference level; as it falls, some becomes kinetic energy. Air resistance dissipates some energy.
22. **Power:** power is energy used per unit time. A 60 W bulb used for 5 h consumes 300 Wh = 0.3 kWh, assuming it actually draws 60 W throughout.
23. **Sound:** frequency affects pitch; larger amplitude generally means louder sound. Sound needs a material medium.
24. **Light:** a mirror reflects light; a lens refracts it. A lens can also reflect some light, and real examples may involve more than one process.
25. **Simple machines:** a lever, pulley and inclined plane can make a task easier by changing force direction or how force is applied; no machine produces free energy.
26. **Magnets:** like poles repel and unlike poles attract. A compass needle is a small magnet that responds to Earth's magnetic field.
27. **States of matter:** melting is solid → liquid; evaporation liquid → gas; condensation gas → liquid; freezing liquid → solid.
28. **Separation:** filtration separates insoluble sand from water; evaporation can recover dissolved salt. A filter alone does not remove dissolved salt.
29. **Rust:** oxygen and water contribute to rusting of iron. Paint helps by limiting contact with them.
30. **Acid/base:** blue litmus turns red in acid; red litmus turns blue in base. Never test chemicals by tasting.
31. **Physical vs chemical:** melting wax is a physical change; burning wax involves a chemical change. A single candle can show both.
32. **Fire safety:** burning often needs fuel, oxygen and heat. Different fires need different responses; don't throw water on an electrical fire. Alert an adult and follow local emergency guidance.

## Earth, science method and common traps

33. **Seasons:** mainly due to Earth's axial tilt and its orbit, not simply Earth being closer to or farther from the Sun.
34. **Day and night:** Earth's rotation causes them; a year relates to Earth's orbit around the Sun.
35. **Moon phases:** we see different parts of the Moon's sunlit half. Earth's shadow causes a lunar eclipse during special alignments, not ordinary phases.
36. **Weather vs climate:** weather changes day to day; climate describes patterns over longer periods.
37. **Rain:** water evaporates, vapour condenses into droplets in clouds and precipitation can follow. Clouds are not simply made of invisible water vapour: they contain tiny droplets or ice crystals.
38. **Scientific temper:** ask for evidence, compare alternatives and revise an explanation when better evidence appears. A repeated claim isn't automatically true.
39. **Fair test:** change one chosen factor and keep relevant others similar. Repeat and record measurements instead of guessing the conclusion.
40. **Correlation ≠ causation:** ice-cream sales and heat-related illness can both rise in summer; hot weather can affect both. The association alone doesn't show ice cream causes illness.

## Easy maths: show the steps

41. **Speed:** 150 km in 3 h → 150 ÷ 3 = 50 km/h.
42. **Time:** 120 km at 40 km/h → 120 ÷ 40 = 3 h, assuming constant speed.
43. **Profit:** buy for ₹80, sell for ₹100 → profit ₹20; profit percentage = 20/80 × 100 = 25% of cost price.
44. **Discount:** ₹500 with 20% off → discount ₹100; pay ₹400.
45. **Fraction:** 3/5 of 50 → 50 ÷ 5 × 3 = 30.
46. **Ratio:** share ₹60 in 1:2 → 3 parts; each ₹20; shares ₹20 and ₹40.
47. **Mean:** 12, 14, 16 → (12 + 14 + 16)/3 = 14.
48. **Area:** rectangle 8 cm × 5 cm → 40 cm²; perimeter → 2 × (8 + 5) = 26 cm.
49. **Probability:** a fair die gives 1 or 2 in 2 out of 6 equally likely outcomes → 1/3.
50. **Graph reading:** check title, units and scale; a zero-baseline bar graph and a truncated baseline can create very different visual impressions.

- **Stomata = leaf pores; alveoli = lung air sacs.**
