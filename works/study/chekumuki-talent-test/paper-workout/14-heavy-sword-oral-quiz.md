# Heavy-Sword Oral Quiz: Explain the Trap ⚔️

**Original practice, not official district rules.** Ask your teacher how your district quiz works. For practice, let teammates rotate: one answers, another explains why, and a third checks the units or assumptions. Start without a timer; later try a short timer if it helps. Never experiment with mains or chemicals.

## Questions

1. A battery maintains 12 V across a 4 Ω ohmic resistor. Find the current and power.
2. Two 6 Ω resistors are in parallel across 12 V. What is the total current?
3. A 100 W lamp runs for 5 hours and a 1 kW heater for 30 minutes. Which uses more energy?
4. A car moves around a circular track at constant speed. Why can it still accelerate?
5. A student completes one lap of a circular track and stops at the starting point. Are distance and displacement both zero?
6. Why is the echo distance half the distance calculated from sound speed × total echo time?
7. Why does a boat float higher in salty water than in fresh water, assuming salty water is denser and the boat floats in both?
8. A beam travels from air into glass. What happens to its frequency at the boundary?
9. A solution looks clear. Does this prove it is free from microbes and dissolved chemicals?
10. Can ordinary filtration remove dissolved salt from water? Explain.
11. In a closed system an iron nail rusts. What happens to the total mass of nail plus surrounding contents?
12. What is the difference between gypsum and plaster of Paris in their formulas?
13. Why can dissolved sodium chloride conduct when solid sodium chloride conducts poorly?
14. A leaf closes after touch; does that prove plants have human nervous systems?
15. What is the difference between xylem and phloem?
16. A person says every artery carries oxygen-rich blood. Give a counterexample.
17. How do vaccination and antiserum differ in the source of antibodies or immune response?
18. Why is a similar control group important in a plant experiment?
19. Why is correlation between two trends not enough to prove one causes the other?
20. A price increases from ₹200 to ₹250. Is the increase 25% or 125%?
21. A ₹1,000 item is discounted by 50% and then 60% on the new price. What remains?
22. Six tablets are taken every 6 hours, with the first taken immediately. How long between the first and last? This is arithmetic, not medical advice.
23. What mainly causes seasons: Earth's changing distance from the Sun or its axial tilt and revolution?
24. What does a space mission being one light-day from Earth mean? Is a light-day time or distance?

## Answers and reasoning

1. **3 A and 36 W:** I = 12/4; P = 12 × 3.
2. **4 A:** 2 A in each branch, added together.
3. **Equal:** lamp 0.1 kW × 5 h = 0.5 kWh; heater 1 kW × 0.5 h = 0.5 kWh.
4. **Direction changes:** acceleration is a change in velocity, which includes direction.
5. **No:** distance is the lap length; displacement is zero because start and finish match.
6. **Round trip:** sound travels to the wall and back; divide total travel distance by two.
7. **Greater density:** denser fluid needs less displaced volume to supply the same buoyant force for the same boat weight.
8. **Frequency stays the same:** speed and wavelength change.
9. **No:** contaminants can be invisible; appropriate testing is needed.
10. **No:** dissolved ions pass through an ordinary filter; other techniques are needed.
11. **It stays constant:** oxygen combines with iron but comes from within the closed system.
12. **Gypsum CaSO₄·2H₂O; plaster of Paris CaSO₄·½H₂O.**
13. **Ions become mobile:** ions are fixed in the solid lattice but move in solution.
14. **No:** plants respond to stimuli without a human-like nervous system.
15. **Xylem** transports water and minerals; **phloem** transports sugars.
16. **Pulmonary artery:** it carries oxygen-poor blood away from the heart to the lungs.
17. **Vaccination stimulates an immune response; antiserum supplies preformed antibodies.** Treatment decisions belong to trained professionals.
18. **Comparison:** keeping other conditions similar makes the effect of the changed factor easier to identify.
19. **A third factor, chance or reverse causation may explain the association:** investigate competing explanations.
20. **25% increase:** increase 50 divided by original 200. The final price is 125% of the original, a different statement.
21. **₹200:** 1,000 × 0.5 × 0.4.
22. **30 hours:** six tablets imply five intervals.
23. **Axial tilt and revolution:** they alter sunlight angle and length of day.
24. **Distance:** one light-day is the distance light travels in 24 hours, about 25.9 billion km. In the simple distance sense, a signal moving at light speed takes about a day one way; actual communication depends on changing distances and trajectory.

## Team debrief

Pick three incorrect or uncertain answers. Each teammate explains one in their own words, then compare with the key. Ask your teacher for the official quiz format and whether there are written, visual, oral or activity rounds; the above is only a practice format.
