## A practical week

- **Days 1–2:** Do one paper per day, then explain your mistakes and guesses. Review a weak topic using the [complete Chekumuki pack](../README.md).
- **Days 3–5:** Try one or two papers per day **if you have time**, with a break. Review is more valuable than racing through all ten.
- **Day 6:** Practise the [oral quiz](../16-mandal-paper-analysis-and-district-quiz.md) with your teammates if appropriate. Reattempt only the questions you missed.
- **Day 7:** Light review, prepare what the organiser asks for, and sleep properly. No all-night studying.

If you have only 20 minutes, do 5–10 questions and study their explanations. The paper labels are not a ranking of your ability, and practice results are not predictions of district results.
