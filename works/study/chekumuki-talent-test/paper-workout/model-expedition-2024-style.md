# Expedition Paper A — 2024 school-style mix

**Original unofficial practice**, inspired by the readable portions of the user-linked [2024 school-level paper](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1u1OVsz5Ru0zqW1r_5ygieDYA72iFRFoy/view) and [older mixed paper](../19-old-paper-link-analysis.md). These are not official papers or verified district rules. Forty multiple-choice questions, one point each, no penalty **for practice**. Suggested time: 60 minutes. The 2024 paper shows 60 minutes; the older paper's extracted time is unreadable. Some real source questions use pictures; this model uses written descriptions instead. Covers related ideas and some familiar topics in new questions; not a guarantee of identical difficulty. Do not try hazardous experiments. [Workout index](README.md)

## Questions

1. A shirt costs ₹500 and is reduced by 20%. What is the new price?
   A) ₹600  B) ₹400  C) ₹100  D) ₹480

2. If each letter is replaced by its opposite in the alphabet (A ↔ Z, B ↔ Y), what is CAT?
   A) XBG  B) DZG  C) XZG  D) GZX

3. A straight angle is divided into two angles of 70° and 110°. What is the angle between their internal bisectors?
   A) 80°  B) 70°  C) 90°  D) 110°

4. Match P Vikram Sarabhai, Q Varghese Kurien, R M. S. Swaminathan with 1 Green Revolution, 2 Indian space programme, 3 White Revolution.
   A) P–2, Q–3, R–1  B) P–2, Q–1, R–3  C) P–1, Q–2, R–3  D) P–3, Q–1, R–2

5. A rectangle has length 8 cm and width 4 cm. A semicircle is drawn outside on each side, with that side as diameter. What is the total length of the four curved arcs?
   A) 32π cm  B) 6π cm  C) 24π cm  D) 12π cm

6. Why can a non-contact infrared thermometer estimate skin temperature?
   A) It detects infrared radiation emitted by the skin  B) It measures the person's mass  C) It sends sound into the blood  D) It detects visible colour only

7. A woman pulls a suitcase with wheels along a level surface. Wheels help mainly by reducing:
   A) gravity on Earth  B) resistance to motion compared with sliding  C) the number of atoms  D) the suitcase's mass

8. Which is a clear example of vegetative reproduction in plants?
   A) A hen laying an egg  B) A potato growing from a tuber  C) A mushroom releasing spores  D) A seed forming after fertilisation

9. India's National Space Day commemorates which 2023 event?
   A) Chandrayaan-3's lunar landing  B) The launch of Sputnik 1  C) The launch of Chandrayaan-1  D) The first Indian railway

10. Match P lactic acid, Q citric acid, R acetic acid with 1 vinegar, 2 citrus fruits, 3 curd.
   A) P–2, Q–1, R–3  B) P–1, Q–3, R–2  C) P–3, Q–1, R–2  D) P–3, Q–2, R–1

11. Why can sound not travel from a bell through a perfect vacuum?
   A) Sound requires a material medium  B) Vacuum is a solid  C) Sound is always light  D) The bell has no mass

12. A ratio of 3:20 expressed as a percentage is:
   A) 20%  B) 3%  C) 60%  D) 15%

13. Steam touches a cold lid over a container of hot water. Droplets form underneath because of:
   A) evaporation  B) sublimation of metal  C) combustion  D) condensation

14. Which material can complete a simple low-voltage circuit and let a bulb glow?
   A) Dry plastic  B) Graphite pencil lead  C) Dry rubber  D) Coconut oil

15. Which habit reduces infection risk before eating?
   A) Sharing unwashed utensils  B) Eating uncovered food from a dirty surface  C) Washing hands with soap and safe water  D) Skipping handwashing when hands look clean

16. Which cell structure is especially important for releasing usable energy from food?
   A) Stoma  B) Mitochondrion  C) Nail  D) Cell wall

17. How many perfect squares are there from 1 to 64 inclusive?
   A) 9  B) 7  C) 6  D) 8

18. Which particle pair has equal rest mass?
   A) Electron and proton  B) Electron and positron  C) Proton and neutron  D) Electron and neutron

19. A shop's daily customers rise from 40 to 50. What is the percentage increase?
   A) 40%  B) 20%  C) 25%  D) 125%

20. Which is a reasonable way to treat vegetable peels?
   A) Throw them into a lake  B) Burn them with plastic indoors  C) Mix them with broken batteries  D) Compost suitable biodegradable waste

21. India won which tournament in 2024?
   A) ICC Men's T20 World Cup  B) ICC Men's T20 World Cup in 2022  C) FIFA men's World Cup  D) 2024 Olympic men's football gold

22. What property helps a knife stay in place in tightly packed rice when lifting a container in a carefully supervised demonstration?
   A) Magnetism in all rice  B) Static friction between rice and knife  C) Magical power  D) The knife becomes weightless

23. Which scientist is associated with the discovery of the Raman effect?
   A) C. V. Raman  B) Charles Darwin  C) Isaac Newton  D) Louis Pasteur

24. A plant placed by a window bends towards the light. This is an example of:
   A) blood circulation  B) sound reflection  C) phototropism  D) rusting

25. Which statement about a solar eclipse is correct?
   A) The Moon comes between Earth and the Sun  B) It happens at every full moon  C) The Sun turns into a planet  D) Earth always comes between the Moon and the Sun

26. What is the main role of vitamin K in the body?
   A) It is a type of red blood cell  B) It helps normal blood clotting  C) It directly carries oxygen in blood  D) It makes bones out of iron

27. Which process contributes to a ripe banana tasting sweeter?
   A) Salt becomes iron  B) Sugar becomes plastic  C) Water becomes sunlight  D) Starch breaks down into sugars

28. Which event involves a chemical change?
   A) Paper being cut  B) Water freezing  C) Iron rusting  D) Ice melting

29. Which force pulls a falling mango towards Earth?
   A) Friction away from Earth  B) Upward buoyancy alone  C) Gravitational force  D) Magnetic force in all fruits

30. Which material in a plant cell's wall helps provide rigidity?
   A) Glass  B) Starch dissolved in blood  C) Haemoglobin  D) Cellulose

31. A group of 20 students has 12 who play chess, 10 who play badminton, and 4 who play both. How many play at least one?
   A) 18  B) 26  C) 22  D) 6

32. Which action best shows scientific temper?
   A) Seek evidence and revise a view when reliable evidence changes  B) Forward every rumour immediately  C) Reject all observations  D) Believe a claim only because it is popular

33. At 25°C, which solution is most acidic?
   A) pH 9  B) pH 5  C) pH 7  D) pH 3

34. A bar chart has January 10 books and February 15 books. February sales are what percentage of January sales?
   A) 200%  B) 150%  C) 50%  D) 15%

35. Which is an example of a fungus?
   A) Fern  B) Amoeba  C) Yeast  D) Moss

36. Which statement about planets is most accurate?
   A) They all have identical orbits  B) They all make their own visible light like the Sun  C) They are all stars  D) We normally see them because they reflect sunlight

37. A student tries a new fertiliser on one plant and gives it extra water too. What is the main problem?
   A) A plant cannot be measured  B) Fertilisers are always harmful  C) Two factors changed, making the cause unclear  D) Plants never need water

38. Which molecule contains inherited genetic information in humans?
   A) Carbon dioxide  B) Water alone  C) DNA  D) Table salt

39. The Indian Parliament makes laws. Which action is part of democratic accountability?
   A) Elections are replaced by guessing  B) Citizens question representatives and vote  C) Citizens are never allowed to ask questions  D) All debate is prohibited

40. A 0.2 kg ball travelling east at 3 m/s rebounds west at 2 m/s. What is the magnitude of the change in momentum?
   A) 1 kg·m/s  B) 0.2 kg·m/s  C) 5 kg·m/s  D) 0.6 kg·m/s

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **B — ₹400.** 20% of 500 is 100; subtract to get ₹400.
2. **C — XZG.** C → X, A → Z, T → G.
3. **C — 90°.** Half of 70° plus half of 110° is 35° + 55° = 90°.
4. **A — P–2, Q–3, R–1.** Sarabhai—space; Kurien—dairy; Swaminathan—Green Revolution.
5. **D — 12π cm.** The two arcs on the long sides add to 8π and the two on the short sides add to 4π. The arc lengths are summed, not the rectangle edges.
6. **A — It detects infrared radiation emitted by the skin.** Temperature estimates use detected thermal infrared radiation; emissivity and surroundings can affect readings.
7. **B — resistance to motion compared with sliding.** Rolling typically reduces resistance compared with dragging the suitcase without wheels; gravity remains.
8. **B — A potato growing from a tuber.** A tuber can grow into a new plant without seeds.
9. **A — Chandrayaan-3's lunar landing.** Chandrayaan-3's lander touched down on the Moon on 23 August 2023.
10. **D — P–3, Q–2, R–1.** Lactic acid is associated with curd, citric acid with citrus and acetic acid with vinegar.
11. **A — Sound requires a material medium.** Sound is a mechanical wave; a perfect vacuum has no material to carry it.
12. **D — 15%.** 3/20 × 100 = 15%.
13. **D — condensation.** Water vapour cools at the lid and condenses into liquid.
14. **B — Graphite pencil lead.** Graphite conducts electricity. Never test circuits with mains electricity.
15. **C — Washing hands with soap and safe water.** Handwashing helps remove disease-causing microbes.
16. **B — Mitochondrion.** Mitochondria help cells produce usable energy through respiration.
17. **D — 8.** The squares are 1² through 8², including 64.
18. **B — Electron and positron.** An electron and positron have equal rest mass and opposite electric charge.
19. **C — 25%.** The increase is 10; 10/40 × 100 = 25%.
20. **D — Compost suitable biodegradable waste.** Composting can convert suitable organic waste into compost.
21. **A — ICC Men's T20 World Cup.** India won the 2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup. This is a dated fact, not a current-year prediction.
22. **B — Static friction between rice and knife.** Static friction and forces from the packed rice can support the container if conditions are suitable; do not attempt a risky demonstration.
23. **A — C. V. Raman.** C. V. Raman's work on light scattering is known as the Raman effect.
24. **C — phototropism.** Phototropism is a directional growth response to light.
25. **A — The Moon comes between Earth and the Sun.** A solar eclipse requires appropriate alignment with the Moon between Earth and Sun; never look directly at the Sun.
26. **B — It helps normal blood clotting.** Vitamin K is needed for producing several proteins involved in blood clotting.
27. **D — Starch breaks down into sugars.** Starch converts into simpler sugars during ripening.
28. **C — Iron rusting.** Rusting forms new substances; the others are physical changes.
29. **C — Gravitational force.** Gravity attracts the mango towards Earth.
30. **D — Cellulose.** Plant cell walls contain cellulose.
31. **A — 18.** Add 12 + 10, then subtract the 4 counted twice: 18.
32. **A — Seek evidence and revise a view when reliable evidence changes.** Scientific temper includes questioning, evidence and openness to correction.
33. **D — pH 3.** Lower pH indicates greater hydrogen-ion activity.
34. **B — 150%.** 15/10 × 100 = 150% of the original; the increase itself is 50%.
35. **C — Yeast.** Yeast is a fungus; it can produce carbon dioxide during fermentation.
36. **D — We normally see them because they reflect sunlight.** Planets mainly reflect sunlight in visible light; they also emit thermal radiation.
37. **C — Two factors changed, making the cause unclear.** Fertiliser and water are confounded; use comparable groups and vary one main factor.
38. **C — DNA.** DNA stores genetic information; genes are segments of DNA.
39. **B — Citizens question representatives and vote.** In a democracy, public debate and elections can hold representatives accountable.
40. **A — 1 kg·m/s.** Take east as positive: Δp = 0.2 × (−2 − 3) = −1 kg·m/s, magnitude 1.
