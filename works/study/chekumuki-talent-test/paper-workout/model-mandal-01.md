## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. Alum is sometimes added when muddy water is being treated because it can help: A) fine suspended particles clump and settle B) make any water safe without further treatment C) remove every dissolved poison instantly D) create oxygen from sand.
2. When a moving bus stops suddenly, a passenger tends to lean forward mainly because of: A) inertia of motion B) evaporation C) photosynthesis D) magnetism.
3. During earthquake shaking indoors, a generally recommended action is to: A) stand beside a glass window B) rush into a lift C) drop, cover under a sturdy desk and hold on D) jump from a balcony.
4. Growing pulses in rotation with cereals can help soil fertility because: A) roots create sunlight. B) all crops add unlimited nutrients C) pulses turn soil into metal D) bacteria associated with legumes can fix nitrogen
5. The knee-jerk response is best described as: A) a form of photosynthesis B) a voluntary movement that must take minutes. C) a rapid spinal reflex D) a decision requiring careful thought
6. Which state shares a boundary with Telangana? A) Maharashtra B) Sikkim. C) Kerala D) Punjab
7. Which is a thermosetting plastic commonly used for electrical switches? A) Cotton B) Bakelite C) Wool. D) Polythene
8. In a neuron, the branching structures that typically receive incoming signals are: A) red blood cells. B) xylem C) dendrites D) stomata
9. Dry raisins swell in plain water mainly due to: A) evaporation of water from the raisins B) magnetic attraction. C) osmosis D) combustion
10. Match P Vikram Sarabhai, Q Homi Bhabha, R M. S. Swaminathan with 1 Indian nuclear programme, 2 Green Revolution, 3 Indian space programme. A) P–3, Q–2, R–1. B) P–3, Q–1, R–2 C) P–1, Q–2, R–3 D) P–2, Q–3, R–1
11. Someone says food automatically becomes poisonous during a solar eclipse. What is the best response? A) Look directly at the Sun B) Believe it because many people say it C) Share it as fact. D) Check evidence; the eclipse itself does not poison food
12. In a group of 30 students, 18 play cricket, 12 play volleyball and 5 play both. How many play at least one of the two? A) 5. B) 35 C) 25 D) 30
13. Some security markings on banknotes can fluoresce under: A) sound waves B) radio broadcasts C) visible light only in all cases. D) ultraviolet light
14. Match P root hair, Q xylem, R phloem, S stomata with 1 gas exchange, 2 water absorption, 3 sugar transport, 4 water transport. A) P–4, Q–3, R–1, S–2 B) P–1, Q–2, R–4, S–3 C) P–3, Q–1, R–2, S–4. D) P–2, Q–4, R–3, S–1
15. A message demands a fee to release an unexpected prize. What should you do? A) Send an OTP B) Give bank details C) Forward it to all friends. D) Do not pay; independently verify and report suspected fraud
16. Which blood component primarily carries oxygen? A) Platelets B) Red blood cells C) Plasma only D) White blood cells.
17. A breath analyser detects alcohol in a person's breath. Which conclusion is most appropriate? A) It provides evidence used to estimate alcohol; a trained authority follows the applicable test procedure B) It works by detecting oxygen made by lungs. C) It tests blood sugar directly D) It proves a person has never drunk water
18. Match P baking soda, Q common salt, R bleaching powder, S magnesium hydroxide with 1 antacid, 2 baking, 3 seasoning, 4 disinfection. A) P–3, Q–1, R–2, S–4 B) P–2, Q–3, R–4, S–1 C) P–4, Q–2, R–1, S–3. D) P–1, Q–4, R–2, S–3
19. Air escapes backwards from an inflated balloon and the balloon moves forwards. This illustrates: A) action and reaction B) a decrease in gravity. C) light reflection D) osmosis
20. A ₹1,000 bag has a 20% discount and then a 25% discount on the reduced price. Final price is: A) ₹750 B) ₹600 C) ₹550 D) ₹800.
21. Which is an example of democratic accountability? A) Citizens question representatives and vote in elections B) Public debate is forbidden. C) Elections are permanently cancelled D) Only officials may express opinions
22. In triangle ABC, D lies on AB and E on AC; DE is parallel to BC. AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 6 cm. EC is: A) 4 cm B) 15 cm. C) 9 cm D) 6 cm
23. Why should schools encourage sports for all students? A) Only winners benefit B) They can support fitness, teamwork and enjoyment C) Physical activity replaces all sleep. D) They guarantee everyone wins a medal
24. Chandrayaan-3's 2023 achievement was: A) a soft landing on the Moon B) orbiting the centre of Earth. C) landing on Mars D) landing on the Sun
25. What is 25% of 80? A) 40 B) 25 C) 60. D) 20
26. A student is excluded from school activities because she is menstruating. What is the best response? A) Refuse education B) Treat it as contagious C) Spread embarrassing rumours. D) Explain menstruation is normal and support inclusion
27. Touch-me-not leaves fold when touched. This demonstrates: A) that plants have a human brain B) that leaves are animals. C) response to a stimulus D) that touch stops all plant respiration
28. Which action supports a cleaner local environment? A) Mix batteries with food waste. B) Separate biodegradable and recyclable waste C) Burn plastic in classrooms D) Throw waste into a lake

## Assertion and reason (29–32)

Choose A) Both true, and reason correctly explains assertion. B) Both true, but reason does not correctly explain assertion. C) Assertion true, reason false. D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. Assertion: DNA comparisons can provide evidence of common ancestry. Reason: Related organisms can share inherited similarities in their DNA.
30. Assertion: The sum of the first five odd positive numbers is 25. Reason: The sum of the first five positive integers is 15.
31. Assertion: Adding dilute acid to an alkaline solution generally lowers its pH while neutralisation occurs. Reason: Acid reacts with hydroxide ions.
32. Assertion: An LED can use less power than an incandescent bulb at similar brightness. Reason: An LED generally converts a greater fraction of electrical energy into visible light.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **A:** coagulation can help suspended particles settle, but complete treatment and testing may still be necessary.
2. **A:** the passenger's body tends to retain its previous motion.
3. **C:** drop, cover and hold on; follow local emergency guidance.
4. **D:** nitrogen-fixing bacteria in association with legumes can help enrich soil.
5. **C:** the knee-jerk is a spinal somatic reflex.
6. **A:** Maharashtra borders Telangana.
7. **B:** Bakelite is a thermoset and is useful as an electrical insulator.
8. **C:** dendrites typically receive signals; the axon carries signals away from the cell body.
9. **C:** water enters across a selectively permeable membrane by osmosis.
10. **B:** Sarabhai—space; Bhabha—nuclear programme; Swaminathan—Green Revolution.
11. **D:** an eclipse itself does not poison food. Never look directly at the Sun without appropriate certified protection and expert guidance.
12. **C:** 18 + 12 − 5 = 25; subtract the overlap once.
13. **D:** ultraviolet light can reveal fluorescent markings.
14. **D:** root hair absorbs water; xylem transports water; phloem transports sugars; stomata allow gas exchange.
15. **D:** verify using an independently obtained official contact. In India suspected financial cyber-fraud can be reported via 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
16. **B:** red blood cells carry oxygen mainly through haemoglobin.
17. **A:** alcohol in breath can be used to estimate alcohol level under appropriate procedures.
18. **B:** baking soda—baking; salt—seasoning; bleaching powder—disinfection; magnesium hydroxide—antacid.
19. **A:** the balloon and escaping air exert forces in opposite directions.
20. **B:** 1,000 × 0.8 × 0.75 = ₹600.
21. **A:** debate and voting are among the ways citizens hold representatives accountable.
22. **C:** AD/DB = AE/EC; 4/6 = 6/EC, so EC = 9 cm.
23. **B:** sports can support health, inclusion and teamwork.
24. **A:** Chandrayaan-3 landed on the Moon in 2023.
25. **D:** 80/4 = 20.
26. **D:** menstruation is normal; education and inclusion matter.
27. **C:** folding is a response to touch, not evidence of a human nervous system.
28. **B:** sorting waste can help responsible composting and recycling.
29. **A:** both true; inherited similarities help explain why DNA comparisons are evidence.
30. **B:** both are true, but the ordinary sum does not by itself explain the odd-number sum.
31. **A:** neutralisation reduces hydroxide concentration, lowering pH in this context.
32. **A:** greater efficiency can give similar light output with less electrical power.
33. **Carbon dioxide / CO₂.**
34. **Boiling.**
35. **Current:** overheating depends on the element's resistance, design and heat loss; do not test with mains electricity.
36. **Buoyancy / upthrust / buoyant force.**
37. **Cell wall.**
38. **People.**
39. **16:** total = 5 × 16 = 80; first four total 64; 80 − 64 = 16.
40. **Water / H₂O.**
