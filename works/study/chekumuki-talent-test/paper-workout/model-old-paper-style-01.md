# Old-paper-style model 1 — mixed science and reasoning ⚔️

**New unofficial practice; not a past paper or a CCMB selection test.** Inspired by the [readable portions of your linked old paper](../19-old-paper-link-analysis.md): mixed science, everyday applications, GK, simple and occasional harder maths, matching and reasoning. It is not guaranteed to match the original's exact difficulty. The source's time is unreadable; **60 minutes is only a suggested practice time**, not a verified source or district rule. Forty MCQs; use one point each, no negative marks for practice. Answer key follows all questions.

## Questions

1. A viral post says an eclipse makes all food poisonous. What is the most scientific response? A) Accept it because it is widely shared B) Stop asking questions. C) Check credible evidence; the eclipse itself does not poison food D) Look directly at the Sun
2. The aircraft flight recorder often called a “black box” is commonly painted: A) bright orange B) transparent C) black D) green.
3. If x + 1/x = 2 and x ≠ 0, what is x² + 1/x²? A) 2 B) 8. C) 4 D) 0
4. A prism separates white light into colours because different colours generally: A) have no wavelength B) travel at the same speed in every medium C) bend by different amounts in the prism D) are created from darkness.
5. A potted plant kept in sunlight makes starch. What is one source of carbon for its sugars? A) Nitrogen gas B) Carbon dioxide C) Oxygen D) Iron filings.
6. Which brain region helps regulate body temperature? A) Spinal nerve only. B) Retina C) Cerebellum D) Hypothalamus
7. When a drop of ink spreads in still water, which process contributes to the spreading? A) Nuclear fission B) Diffusion C) Magnetism. D) Photosynthesis
8. If M = 25, the next positive perfect square is: A) 36 B) 50 C) 625. D) 30
9. In the chain grass → grasshopper → frog → snake, if frogs decline, which is a plausible short-term effect, other things being equal? A) Grasshoppers may increase B) Grass becomes a predator C) Energy disappears instantly. D) All snakes immediately become plants
10. A uniform 20 Ω resistor is cut in half; one half is connected across the same ideal voltage. Assuming resistivity stays constant, its power compared with the original is: A) twice B) one quarter C) half D) unchanged.
11. Who is associated with the Indian space programme? A) Florence Nightingale. B) Gregor Mendel C) M. S. Swaminathan D) Vikram Sarabhai
12. Which numbers are both factors of 210? A) 4 and 8 B) 11 and 12. C) 9 and 10 D) 6 and 7
13. Which part of the blood transports most oxygen? A) Red blood cells B) White blood cells C) Plasma alone. D) Platelets
14. Which list contains only matter? A) Sunlight, sand, air B) Vacuum, wood, light. C) Sound, air, water D) Air, water, sand
15. A test of a new claim should preferably: A) ignore results that disagree B) reject all questions. C) rely only on an influencer D) use evidence, a suitable comparison and repeatable measurements
16. The Centre for Cellular and Molecular Biology (CCMB) is in: A) Kolkata B) Hyderabad C) Mumbai D) Jaipur.
17. Which metal reacts vigorously with cold water and should only be handled under trained supervision? A) Gold. B) Copper C) Sodium D) Silver
18. Which organism is a fungus? A) Mango tree B) Moss C) Mushroom D) Fern.
19. Particle X has 8 protons and 8 neutrons; Y has 7 protons and 9 neutrons. Their relationship is: A) Isotopes of the same element B) Identical atoms C) Isobars D) Both have seven protons.
20. A neutral atom gains one electron. It becomes: A) a negatively charged ion B) electrically neutral by definition. C) an atom with one more proton D) a positively charged ion
21. Match symbols: P alpha, Q delta, R omega with 1 δ, 2 ω, 3 α. A) P–2, Q–3, R–1 B) P–1, Q–2, R–3 C) P–3, Q–1, R–2 D) P–3, Q–2, R–1.
22. A banana generally becomes sweeter as it ripens partly because: A) it stops containing any water B) sugars become metals C) some starch changes into sugars D) sunlight turns directly into salt.
23. If a planet's mass stayed fixed but its radius doubled, a body's weight at its surface would become, in a simplified spherical model: A) half. B) four times C) twice D) one quarter
24. An echo returns 0.2 seconds after a sound. If sound travels at 340 m/s, the reflecting wall is: A) 68 m away B) 170 m away. C) 17 m away D) 34 m away
25. The Indian organisation that conducts civilian space missions including Chandrayaan-3 is: A) WHO B) UNESCO. C) RBI D) ISRO
26. Influenza is caused by a: A) rock. B) virus C) metal D) vitamin
27. During an earthquake indoors, a generally recommended response is to: A) run towards a glass window. B) drop, cover and hold on C) use a lift D) jump from a balcony
28. A person claims a lucky charm guarantees an exam result. What is the best response? A) Stop studying B) Ask for reliable evidence and consider other explanations C) Accept the claim without testing D) Threaten people who disagree.
29. The centroid G of triangle ABC divides its area into three equal-area triangles GAB, GBC and GCA. If ABC has area 60 cm², GBC has area: A) 30 cm² B) 40 cm². C) 10 cm² D) 20 cm²
30. A 0.1 kg ball moving at 5 m/s rebounds at 5 m/s in the opposite direction. What is the magnitude of the change in its momentum? A) 0 B) 5 kg m/s. C) 1 kg m/s D) 0.5 kg m/s
31. Which device can form a real image of a suitably placed real object? A) A convex lens B) A concave lens alone. C) A convex mirror alone D) A plane mirror alone
32. A square is divided by one vertical and one horizontal line through its centre into four equal squares. How many squares are present in total? A) 4 B) 5 C) 6 D) 8.
33. Which is the next term: 1, 4, 9, 16, ...? A) 32. B) 20 C) 24 D) 25
34. Match P cell membrane, Q ribosome, R nucleus with 1 protein assembly, 2 selective boundary, 3 contains most DNA in typical plant/animal cells. A) P–1, Q–3, R–2 B) P–3, Q–2, R–1 C) P–2, Q–1, R–3 D) P–2, Q–3, R–1.
35. Many fruity smells in fruits and perfumes are associated with: A) sand B) esters C) sodium chloride only. D) iron filings
36. If x + y = −z, the value of (x + y)² − z² is: A) z B) 0 C) 2z. D) 1
37. Which activity best tests whether light affects seedling growth? A) Look at one plant once B) Select only results that support a guess. C) Compare similar groups with different light, keeping water and other conditions similar D) Give different plants different water and light
38. A comet's nucleus includes: A) radio waves alone B) ice and dust C) only liquid iron. D) fire alone
39. A solution has pH 4 and another has pH 5. Which has greater hydrogen-ion activity? A) pH 4 B) Both the same C) pH 5 D) Neither contains water.
40. A bag marked ₹1,000 is discounted by 20%, then by 25% on the reduced price. What is the final price? A) ₹600 B) ₹750 C) ₹550 D) ₹800.

---

## Answer key and explanations

1. **C:** check evidence; do not look directly at the Sun without appropriate certified protection and expert guidance.
2. **A:** flight recorders are typically bright orange for visibility.
3. **A:** square both sides: x² + 2 + 1/x² = 4, so the result is 2. Alternatively x = 1.
4. **C:** different wavelengths generally refract by different amounts in a prism.
5. **B:** plants obtain carbon for sugars from CO₂.
6. **D:** the hypothalamus is important for temperature regulation.
7. **B:** particles diffuse; even in still water convection may also contribute.
8. **A:** 25 = 5²; the next square is 6² = 36.
9. **A:** fewer predators may lead to more grasshoppers, subject to other ecological factors.
10. **A:** halving the resistance at fixed voltage doubles power P = V²/R, under the stated ideal assumptions.
11. **D:** Vikram Sarabhai was a leading figure in India's space programme.
12. **D:** 210/6 = 35 and 210/7 = 30.
13. **A:** haemoglobin in red blood cells carries most oxygen.
14. **D:** air, water and sand have mass and occupy space.
15. **D:** evidence and appropriate comparisons help test claims.
16. **B:** CCMB is in Hyderabad.
17. **C:** sodium reacts vigorously with cold water; never try this at home.
18. **C:** mushrooms are fungi.
19. **C:** both have mass number 16, but different proton numbers; they are isobars.
20. **A:** adding a negative electron makes the ion negatively charged.
21. **C:** alpha α, delta δ, omega ω.
22. **C:** starch is broken down into sugars during ripening.
23. **D:** W = GMm/R²; doubling R divides W by 4 if M is fixed.
24. **D:** sound travels to the wall and back, so distance = 340 × 0.2/2 = 34 m.
25. **D:** the Indian Space Research Organisation conducts these missions.
26. **B:** influenza is a viral infection; antibiotics do not treat the virus itself.
27. **B:** drop, cover and hold on; follow local safety instructions.
28. **B:** a claim requires evidence; confidence or popularity alone is not enough.
29. **D:** 60/3 = 20 cm².
30. **C:** choosing the initial direction as positive, Δp = 0.1 × (−5 − 5) = −1 kg m/s; magnitude is 1.
31. **A:** a convex lens forms a real image when a real object is placed beyond its focal length.
32. **B:** four small squares plus one large square = five.
33. **D:** consecutive positive squares: 1², 2², 3², 4², 5².
34. **C:** membrane—boundary; ribosome—protein assembly; nucleus—most DNA in typical plant/animal cells.
35. **B:** many esters have fruity odours; not every perfume smell is an ester.
36. **B:** (−z)² − z² = 0.
37. **C:** a comparable group helps isolate the factor of interest; replicate plants too.
38. **B:** comet nuclei include ice and dust, and can also contain organic materials.
39. **A:** one pH unit lower corresponds to tenfold greater hydrogen-ion activity.
40. **A:** 1,000 × 0.8 × 0.75 = ₹600; discounts are sequential, not simply added.
