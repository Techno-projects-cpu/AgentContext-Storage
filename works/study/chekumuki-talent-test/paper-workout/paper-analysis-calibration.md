# What your school and mandal papers actually test

## Source and limits

This analysis uses **your supplied photographs**, already preserved in the repository as exact, unmodified image copies: [school paper](original-school-facsimile.md) and [mandal paper](original-mandal-facsimile.md). These are photographed papers with handwriting, not clean retypeset copies, and we have no official answer key. They are two examples, not enough to establish every year's or district's blueprint. The [previous mandal analysis](../16-mandal-paper-analysis-and-district-quiz.md) gives more detail.

## Visible shared structure

Both papers say **40 marks, 60 minutes, one mark each and no negative marks**. They each contain **28 MCQs**, **4 assertion–reason questions** and **8 fill-in-the-blanks**. These are rules on the photographed school/mandal papers, not verified district rules. Both say to mark/fill answers in a response sheet.

## What to imitate

| Feature | Evidence in the supplied papers | Practice implication |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday science | School: sharp knife and pressure, seat belt, food chain; mandal: swollen raisins, balloon, pressure cooker | Ask for the principle in a familiar situation, not only a definition. |
| Scientific temper and safety | School: evaluate a sensational message; mandal: eclipse claim and cyber-fraud | Include evidence, safety and debunking without conducting risky demonstrations. |
| Simple maths plus reasoning | School: LCM/GCD, chart and tablets; mandal: discounts, similar triangles, averages | Mostly short calculations, sometimes one extra step; no Olympiad-style algebra requirement. |
| Read and match | Both use scientist/plant-part/food or mission matching and diagrams | Include matching and text-described visual questions; actual photos are essential for real diagram practice. |
| Biology and society | Food, organs, photosynthesis, menstruation, democracy and sports | Mix science with health, inclusion, civic ideas and general knowledge. |
| Recent facts | School asks about an athlete and Nobel Prize; mandal asks about a space milestone | Dated current-affairs questions can age; verify the year and official results before the test. |
| Question type | 28 MCQ, 4 assertion–reason, 8 fill-ins | Keep the same layout, with a key at the end. |

School includes a graph, picture-based questions and circuit diagrams; mandal includes a Venn diagram, labelled neuron/plant images and a geometry diagram. Our new papers use simple text diagrams and descriptions, **not reproductions of the original graphics**. Some questions rely on general knowledge, while others require interpreting a scenario, diagram or data. Exact proportions and difficulty cannot be established from two samples.

## Careful checking

- School Q5 asks about a percentage increase in bars that appear to go from 20 to 40: mathematically the increase is **100%**, while 40 is **200% of** 20. The supplied options do not seem to include 100%; ask the organiser for the intended key.
- Mandal Q5's knee-jerk-reflex wording and Q35's fuse explanation are imprecise; see the [mandal analysis](../16-mandal-paper-analysis-and-district-quiz.md).
- Do not treat pencil marks in photos as an official key or automatically as final responses. The user's confirmed mandal score is **34/40**; the school score is unconfirmed.
