## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. A mixture contains sand and dissolved salt in water. What removes the sand first?

   A) Freezing the sand at room temperature  B) Filtration  C) A magnet always attracts both  D) Ordinary filtration removes the salt only

2. Which structure conducts nerve impulses away from a neuron's cell body?

   A) Alveolus  B) Axon  C) Xylem  D) Root hair

3. Two identical 6 Ω resistors are in parallel across an ideal 12 V source. Their total current is:

   A) 12 A  B) 1 A  C) 2 A  D) 4 A

4. Divide 121 in the ratio 5:6. What is the first share?

   A) 66  B) 55  C) 11  D) 121

5. A high fever is always caused by a virus. Is this statement reliable?

   A) No; fever has multiple possible causes  B) Only during summer  C) Yes, if antibiotics work  D) Yes, always

6. A lunar eclipse requires:

   A) Earth between Sun and Moon  B) Sun between Earth and Moon  C) a new Moon every day  D) Moon between Sun and Earth

7. A book priced ₹600 has a 10% discount. What is the sale price?

   A) ₹540  B) ₹600  C) ₹660  D) ₹60

8. During an earthquake inside a classroom, the safest immediate action among these is:

   A) run to a window  B) rush down stairs while shaking  C) take cover under a sturdy desk, protect your head and hold on  D) use a lift

9. Isotopes of the same element have the same number of:

   A) nucleons  B) protons  C) neutrons  D) neutrons and electrons in every ion

10. Earth's seasons are mainly due to

   A) Daily rotation only  B) Distance alone  C) The Moon's phases  D) Axial tilt and revolution

11. Vitamin C deficiency can cause

   A) Tuberculosis  B) Rickets only  C) Malaria  D) Scurvy

12. Acid reacting with a carbonate usually releases

   A) Carbon dioxide  B) Oxygen  C) Hydrogen  D) Nitrogen

13. A message asks for your login OTP. What is safest?

   A) Share it with the caller  B) Post it publicly  C) Do not share it; verify through an official channel  D) Send it to a stranger

14. A book priced ₹300 has a 20% discount. What is the sale price?

   A) ₹60  B) ₹240  C) ₹300  D) ₹360

15. Plaster of Paris is

   A) CaSO₄·2H₂O  B) Na₂CO₃·10H₂O  C) NaHCO₃  D) CaSO₄·½H₂O

16. A ₹1,000 item is discounted by 50%, and then the reduced price is discounted by 60%. Final price:

   A) ₹500  B) ₹200  C) ₹800  D) ₹300

17. A 80 W lamp runs at constant power for 4 hours. How much energy does it use?

   A) 80 Wh  B) 84 Wh  C) 320 Wh  D) 3200 Wh

18. The perimeter of a rectangle 6 cm long and 2 cm wide is:

   A) 16 cm  B) 24 cm²  C) 12 cm  D) 8 cm

19. In parallel branches connected to an ideal source, each branch has the same:

   A) power  B) voltage  C) current  D) resistance

20. Why can sound not travel through a vacuum?

   A) It is light  B) It is too slow  C) It needs a material medium  D) It travels only in water

21. Which blood vessels carry blood away from the heart?

   A) Arteries  B) Lymph vessels  C) Capillaries only  D) Veins

22. Which is a gene?

   A) A species of bacterium  B) A segment of DNA associated with inherited functions or traits  C) A blood vessel  D) An entire ecosystem

23. A shiny surface gives a clear image mainly because it produces

   A) Only refraction  B) Regular reflection  C) Only absorption  D) No scattering

24. A sound echo returns after 0.2 seconds. If sound travels at 340 m/s, the wall is about:

   A) 340 m  B) 68 m  C) 34 m  D) 17 m

25. The Moon shines mainly because it

   A) Reflects sunlight  B) Produces its own sunlight  C) Is a star  D) Is made of fire

26. If a pendulum slows due to friction, its mechanical energy:

   A) vanishes  B) increases forever  C) becomes massless  D) partly transfers to surroundings

27. Pitch mainly depends on sound wave

   A) Temperature only  B) Mass of listener  C) Colour  D) Frequency

28. Phloem mainly transports

   A) Urine  B) Sugars and other organic nutrients  C) Only oxygen  D) Sound

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 3 Ω resistor connected across 6 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 6/3 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 4 positive odd numbers is 16. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 6 V component transferring 6 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 6 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 110 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **B** — filtration removes insoluble sand, not dissolved salt.
2. **B** — the axon carries impulses away from the cell body.
3. **D** — 2 A in each branch, 4 A total.
4. **B** — Total parts = 11; each part is 11; first share = 5 × 11 = 55.
5. **A** — Fever can have several causes; seek medical guidance rather than assuming one.
6. **A** — Earth casts its shadow on the Moon.
7. **A** — The discount is 10% of ₹600 = ₹60; the price is ₹540.
8. **C** — drop, cover and hold on; follow local safety guidance.
9. **B** — an element is identified by proton number.
10. **D** — The tilted axis changes the angle and duration of sunlight during the year.
11. **D** — Scurvy is associated with vitamin C deficiency.
12. **A** — Acid + carbonate usually releases carbon dioxide.
13. **C** — OTPs are private; verify suspicious requests independently.
14. **B** — The discount is 20% of ₹300 = ₹60; the price is ₹240.
15. **D** — Plaster of Paris is calcium sulfate hemihydrate.
16. **B** — 50% off ₹1,000 leaves ₹500; 60% off ₹500 leaves ₹200. Successive discounts are not added.
17. **C** — Energy = power × time = 80 × 4 = 320 Wh.
18. **A** — 2 × (6 + 2) = 16 cm.
19. **B** — parallel branches share the same voltage; their currents can differ.
20. **C** — Sound is a mechanical wave that requires a medium.
21. **A** — Arteries are defined by direction away from the heart.
22. **B** — genes are DNA segments, though traits can involve several genes and environmental influences.
23. **B** — A smooth surface produces more regular reflection.
24. **C** — 340 × 0.2 / 2 = 34 m, because sound travels out and back.
25. **A** — The Moon reflects sunlight.
26. **D** — mechanical energy is transferred, mainly as thermal energy.
27. **D** — Frequency corresponds to pitch.
28. **B** — Phloem transports sugars and other organic substances.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **newton (N)** — Accepted answer: newton (N)
34. **kilowatt-hour (kWh)** — Accepted answer: kilowatt-hour (kWh)
35. **pressure** — Accepted answer: pressure
36. **buoyancy / buoyant force** — Accepted answer: buoyancy / buoyant force
37. **17** — Accepted answer: 17
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **220** — Accepted answer: 220
40. **13** — Accepted answer: 13
