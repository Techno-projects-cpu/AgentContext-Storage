## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. Two lamps are connected in parallel. If one branch opens, the other can remain lit because:

   A) it has a separate conducting path  B) both lamps must have equal power  C) resistance is zero  D) the battery has no voltage

2. Which is a mixture?

   A) Pure water  B) Air  C) Oxygen gas  D) Sodium chloride compound

3. IMD is associated with:

   A) creating the Moon  B) building all schools  C) weather services  D) manufacturing all medicines

4. Which organisation is India's space agency?

   A) IMD  B) ISRO  C) WHO  D) CPCB

5. Divide 221 in the ratio 8:5. What is the first share?

   A) 221  B) 17  C) 85  D) 136

6. Solid sodium chloride conducts poorly mainly because:

   A) its ions are not free to move through the solid  B) it contains no ions  C) electrons are flowing freely  D) it contains no sodium

7. Which organelle is mainly responsible for photosynthesis in green plant cells?

   A) Ribosome  B) Chloroplast  C) Nucleus  D) Cell wall

8. A student wants to test if extra water changes plant growth. Which should they vary?

   A) The reported result  B) Both water and plant species  C) Every condition  D) Amount of water

9. SI unit of force?

   A) Watt  B) Newton  C) Pascal  D) Joule

10. Most nutrient absorption during digestion occurs in the:

   A) oesophagus  B) small intestine  C) large intestine  D) stomach

11. Which organ makes bile?

   A) Lung  B) Liver  C) Heart  D) Kidney

12. A 22 V DC source is connected across an ohmic 11 Ω resistor. Find the current.

   A) 3 A  B) 11 A  C) 22 A  D) 2 A

13. A student touches a touch-me-not plant and its leaves fold. This demonstrates:

   A) response to a stimulus  B) a human nervous system  C) loss of gravity  D) reproduction

14. A 140 W lamp runs at constant power for 4 hours. How much energy does it use?

   A) 5600 Wh  B) 140 Wh  C) 144 Wh  D) 560 Wh

15. A fair six-sided die is rolled once. Chance of an even number?

   A) 1/6  B) 1  C) 1/2  D) 1/3

16. Which process produces a new substance?

   A) Melting ice  B) Cutting paper  C) Boiling water  D) Rusting iron

17. Seasons are mainly caused by:

   A) the Moon's phases  B) Earth's axial tilt and revolution  C) Earth's daily rotation alone  D) changing distance from the Sun alone

18. Sweating cools skin mainly through

   A) Combustion  B) Condensation  C) Freezing  D) Evaporation

19. Which metal is liquid at ordinary room temperature?

   A) Iron  B) Aluminium  C) Mercury  D) Zinc

20. A book priced ₹500 has a 40% discount. What is the sale price?

   A) ₹700  B) ₹300  C) ₹200  D) ₹500

21. Two 6 Ω resistors are in series across an ideal 12 V source. The current in the single loop is:

   A) 4 A  B) 2 A  C) 1 A  D) 12 A

22. Divide 81 in the ratio 4:5. What is the first share?

   A) 36  B) 9  C) 81  D) 45

23. A fair die is rolled. Probability of a number greater than 4 is:

   A) 1/2  B) 2/3  C) 1/3  D) 1/6

24. A solid has mass 100 g and volume 20 cm³. Density?

   A) 120 g/cm³  B) 5 g/cm³  C) 80 g/cm³  D) 2,000 g/cm³

25. A 40 V DC source is connected across an ohmic 10 Ω resistor. Find the current.

   A) 5 A  B) 40 A  C) 10 A  D) 4 A

26. A concave lens is commonly used to correct

   A) Hypermetropia  B) Colour blindness  C) Deafness  D) Myopia

27. pH 7 at 25°C is

   A) Neutral  B) A metal  C) Always strongly basic  D) Acidic

28. In an ecosystem, decomposers help:

   A) stop energy transfer  B) recycle nutrients  C) prevent all decay  D) produce sunlight

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 4 Ω resistor connected across 8 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 8/4 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 5 positive odd numbers is 25. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 8 V component transferring 8 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 8 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 120 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **A** — each branch has its own path across the source.
2. **B** — Air is a mixture of gases.
3. **C** — IMD provides meteorological services.
4. **B** — ISRO is the Indian Space Research Organisation.
5. **D** — Total parts = 13; each part is 17; first share = 8 × 17 = 136.
6. **A** — mobile ions carry charge in molten or dissolved salt, but are fixed in the solid lattice.
7. **B** — chloroplasts perform photosynthesis.
8. **D** — Vary the amount of water while controlling relevant other conditions.
9. **B** — Force is measured in newtons.
10. **B** — most absorption occurs in the small intestine.
11. **B** — The liver makes bile; bile helps emulsify fats.
12. **D** — I = V/R = 22/11 = 2 A.
13. **A** — plants respond to stimuli without a human nervous system.
14. **D** — Energy = power × time = 140 × 4 = 560 Wh.
15. **C** — There are three even faces out of six: 3/6 = 1/2.
16. **D** — Rusting is a chemical change producing iron oxides.
17. **B** — tilt changes the angle and duration of sunlight through the year.
18. **D** — Evaporation carries energy away from the skin.
19. **C** — Mercury is liquid at ordinary room temperature.
20. **B** — The discount is 40% of ₹500 = ₹200; the price is ₹300.
21. **C** — total resistance 12 Ω; I = 12/12 = 1 A.
22. **A** — Total parts = 9; each part is 9; first share = 4 × 9 = 36.
23. **C** — outcomes 5 and 6 give 2/6 = 1/3.
24. **B** — Density = 100/20 = 5 g/cm³.
25. **D** — I = V/R = 40/10 = 4 A.
26. **D** — A diverging lens helps correct myopia.
27. **A** — At 25°C pH 7 is neutral.
28. **B** — decomposers return nutrients to ecosystems.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **inertia** — Accepted answer: inertia
34. **reflection** — Accepted answer: reflection
35. **refraction** — Accepted answer: refraction
36. **dispersion** — Accepted answer: dispersion
37. **19** — Accepted answer: 19
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **240** — Accepted answer: 240
40. **14** — Accepted answer: 14
