## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. A 100 W lamp works for 2 hours at constant power. Energy used is:

   A) 200 kWh  B) 20 kWh  C) 2 kWh  D) 0.2 kWh

2. The oxygen released in photosynthesis comes primarily from:

   A) the sugar produced  B) water  C) soil nitrogen  D) chlorophyll alone

3. A 16 V DC source is connected across an ohmic 4 Ω resistor. Find the current.

   A) 16 A  B) 105 A  C) 4 A  D) 5 A

4. A 60 W lamp runs at constant power for 3 hours. How much energy does it use?

   A) 180 Wh  B) 63 Wh  C) 60 Wh  D) 1800 Wh

5. Total internal reflection requires light to travel

   A) From air into glass at any angle  B) From higher to lower refractive index, with incidence above critical angle  C) Only in vacuum  D) From lower to higher refractive index at any angle

6. A 100 W lamp runs at constant power for 2 hours. How much energy does it use?

   A) 2000 Wh  B) 102 Wh  C) 200 Wh  D) 100 Wh

7. A cyclist travels 75 km in 3 hours. What is the average speed?

   A) 78 km/h  B) 75 km/h  C) 25 km/h  D) 28 km/h

8. Which is true of current in a simple series loop with a battery and bulb?

   A) Current only flows through one wire  B) Current is the same through the battery and bulb in steady operation  C) The bulb uses up the charge  D) The bulb must supply more energy than the battery

9. A cyclist travels 240 km in 4 hours. What is the average speed?

   A) 60 km/h  B) 64 km/h  C) 244 km/h  D) 240 km/h

10. Rusting generally requires:

   A) a vacuum  B) oxygen and moisture  C) sunlight only  D) nitrogen only

11. A light ray enters glass from air. At the boundary, which remains unchanged?

   A) Speed  B) Wavelength  C) Direction in every case  D) Frequency

12. A car moves at constant speed around a circular track. Which changes?

   A) Mass  B) Velocity direction  C) Speed necessarily  D) Distance travelled becomes zero

13. Xylem and phloem respectively mainly transport:

   A) seeds; roots  B) water and minerals; sugars  C) oxygen only; nitrogen only  D) sugars; water and minerals

14. Which statement about antibiotics is correct?

   A) They cure every fever  B) They treat certain bacterial infections, not viral influenza  C) Resistance means a person becomes immune to antibiotics  D) They work on all viruses

15. Which Indian organisation is associated with weather services?

   A) WHO  B) IMD  C) FIFA  D) ISRO only

16. An observation shows ice-cream sales and drowning incidents both increase in summer. What is justified?

   A) The two measurements must be identical  B) A seasonal factor may affect both; correlation alone doesn't prove cause  C) Drowning causes ice-cream sales  D) Ice cream causes drowning

17. A 100 W lamp runs for 3 hours at constant power. Energy used is:

   A) 0.3 kWh  B) 30 kWh  C) 0.03 kWh  D) 3 kWh

18. Which of the following is an example of directional plant growth in response to touch?

   A) A rock falling  B) Blood clotting  C) A leaf reflecting light  D) A tendril coiling around a support

19. In an experiment, a conclusion disagrees with a prediction. What should a good scientist do?

   A) Check methods and evidence, and revise the explanation if needed  B) Hide the result  C) Change measurements to match  D) Assume the hypothesis is automatically true

20. For the same momentum change, increasing stopping time generally makes average force

   A) Larger  B) Smaller  C) Independent of time  D) Exactly zero

21. Why does a lunar eclipse not occur every full moon?

   A) Earth has no shadow  B) Its orbit is tilted, so exact alignment usually does not occur  C) The Moon does not reflect sunlight  D) Every full moon is a solar eclipse

22. Why can a steel ship float?

   A) Water has no weight  B) Buoyancy only acts on hollow objects  C) Steel has no mass  D) The ship's overall average density, including enclosed air, can be less than water's

23. In a simple Tt × Tt cross, assuming full dominance, the expected recessive phenotype fraction is

   A) 3/4  B) 1  C) 1/2  D) 1/4

24. A catalyst

   A) Creates atoms  B) Changes the reaction rate without being consumed overall  C) Is always a reactant used up  D) Makes mass disappear

25. A packet uses nitrogen gas to reduce spoilage. One reason is that nitrogen is relatively unreactive and can:

   A) make oxygen heavier  B) reduce oxidation of food  C) eliminate every possible cause of spoilage  D) turn food into metal

26. An orbiting astronaut experiences apparent weightlessness primarily because

   A) There is no mass in orbit  B) Earth’s gravity is absent  C) The astronaut and spacecraft are in continuous free fall together  D) Air pressure pushes upward

27. An experiment varies light AND fertiliser between groups. Main problem?

   A) It is hard to identify which change caused any difference  B) Plants cannot be measured  C) It always proves causation  D) A control group becomes impossible in every experiment

28. The basic functional filtering units of the kidneys are

   A) Alveoli  B) Nephrons  C) Neurons  D) Stomata

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 9 Ω resistor connected across 18 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 18/9 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 10 positive odd numbers is 100. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 18 V component transferring 18 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 18 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 170 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Fill in the blanks (33–40)

33. The gas released by yeast that makes dough rise is ___.

34. Proteins that speed up biological reactions are often called ___.

35. An element is identified by its number of ___.

36. The formula NaCl refers to sodium ___.

37. A 10% discount on ₹290 is ₹___.

38. A 27 V source across an ohmic 9 Ω resistor gives a current of ___ A.

39. A 170 W device running for 2 hours uses ___ Wh.

40. The average of 17, 19 and 21 is ___.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **D** — 100 W × 2 h = 200 Wh = 0.2 kWh. A watt is power; a watt-hour measures energy.
2. **B** — oxygen gas originates from splitting water.
3. **C** — I = V/R = 16/4 = 4 A.
4. **A** — Energy = power × time = 60 × 3 = 180 Wh.
5. **B** — Both the direction of travel and the critical angle condition matter.
6. **C** — Energy = power × time = 100 × 2 = 200 Wh.
7. **C** — Average speed = distance/time = 75/3 = 25 km/h.
8. **B** — Charge is not used up; the bulb transfers energy. In steady operation the same current flows through each part of one series loop.
9. **A** — Average speed = distance/time = 240/4 = 60 km/h.
10. **B** — ordinary rusting requires oxygen and moisture.
11. **D** — frequency is unchanged; speed and wavelength change.
12. **B** — Velocity includes direction, which changes on the circular path.
13. **B** — Xylem carries water and minerals; phloem transports sugars and other organic substances.
14. **B** — Antibiotic resistance involves microbes becoming less susceptible to drugs.
15. **B** — The India Meteorological Department provides weather and climate services.
16. **B** — Both could rise because of summer weather or other factors. The association does not establish that one causes the other.
17. **A** — 100 × 3 = 300 Wh = 0.3 kWh.
18. **D** — the tendril's directional growth is thigmotropism.
19. **A** — Unexpected evidence calls for checking methods and revising explanations when justified—not hiding data.
20. **B** — Average force is change in momentum divided by time.
21. **B** — The Moon's orbit is tilted, so the Sun, Earth and Moon generally do not align closely enough at full moon.
22. **D** — A floating ship displaces water whose weight balances its own; its overall average density includes enclosed air.
23. **D** — The expected genotypes are TT, Tt, Tt and tt; only tt is recessive.
24. **B** — Catalysts change the reaction pathway and are regenerated overall.
25. **B** — nitrogen can help reduce oxidative spoilage, but it does not prevent every kind.
26. **C** — Gravity remains significant in orbit; shared free fall produces weightlessness.
27. **A** — Changing more than one relevant factor makes causal attribution difficult.
28. **B** — Nephrons filter blood and reabsorb useful substances.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **carbon dioxide** — Accepted answer: carbon dioxide
34. **enzymes** — Accepted answer: enzymes
35. **protons** — Accepted answer: protons
36. **chloride** — Accepted answer: chloride
37. **29** — Accepted answer: 29
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **340** — Accepted answer: 340
40. **19** — Accepted answer: 19
