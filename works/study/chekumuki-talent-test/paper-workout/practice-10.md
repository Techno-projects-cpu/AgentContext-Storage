## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. A charged object and an uncharged object are compared. Which is true?

   A) A neutral object has no electrons  B) A neutral object has no charged particles  C) Every charged object contains no protons  D) A neutral object has equal total positive and negative charge

2. A 12 V source is connected to a 4 Ω ohmic resistor. The current is:

   A) 0.33 A  B) 8 A  C) 48 A  D) 3 A

3. Who is associated with India's space programme?

   A) Gregor Mendel only  B) William Shakespeare  C) Euclid only  D) Vikram Sarabhai

4. A stone travels one full lap of a circular path and returns to its starting point. Its displacement is:

   A) the circle's diameter  B) zero  C) always greater than its distance travelled  D) equal to the lap length

5. Why does a metal spoon feel colder than wood in the same room?

   A) Metal generally conducts heat away from skin faster  B) Metal has no internal energy  C) Metal must be at a lower temperature  D) Wood produces heat

6. Divide 49 in the ratio 3:4. What is the first share?

   A) 7  B) 21  C) 28  D) 49

7. A cyclist covers equal distances at 60 km/h and 30 km/h. What is average speed?

   A) 40 km/h  B) 45 km/h  C) 30 km/h  D) 90 km/h

8. A geostationary satellite appears fixed above a point on:

   A) either pole  B) the Moon  C) the equator  D) any latitude

9. A car moves around a curve at constant speed. Its acceleration is:

   A) zero because speed is constant  B) impossible to discuss  C) nonzero because direction of velocity changes  D) always straight ahead

10. Which statement best describes science?

   A) Claims never need testing  B) A single experiment proves every hypothesis forever  C) Evidence, careful observation and revision of explanations matter  D) A popular belief is always correct

11. A scientific theory is best described as

   A) A random guess  B) A well-supported explanatory framework  C) An opinion without evidence  D) A law that can never be tested

12. A dominant allele is necessarily

   A) Always superior  B) Neither necessarily common nor beneficial  C) Always common in the population  D) Present in every person

13. A book priced ₹200 has a 10% discount. What is the sale price?

   A) ₹220  B) ₹200  C) ₹180  D) ₹20

14. Two 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. Combined resistance?

   A) 6 Ω  B) 12 Ω  C) 36 Ω  D) 3 Ω

15. Two neutral atoms each have 6 protons, but one has 6 neutrons and the other 8. They are:

   A) isotopes of the same element  B) identical in mass  C) oppositely charged ions  D) different elements

16. If a fair coin comes up heads three times, the next independent toss has probability of heads

   A) 3/4  B) 1/8  C) 1/2  D) 0

17. When an inflated balloon is released, air rushes backward and the balloon moves forward. The best explanation is:

   A) conservation of momentum  B) light reflection  C) magnetic repulsion only  D) osmosis

18. Divide 96 in the ratio 5:3. The larger part is:

   A) 36  B) 48  C) 60  D) 80

19. Divide 345 in the ratio 11:4. What is the first share?

   A) 92  B) 345  C) 23  D) 253

20. A 1 kW heater operates at constant power for 30 minutes. Energy transferred is:

   A) 30 kWh  B) 0.5 kWh  C) 1 kWh  D) 60 kWh

21. A 160 W lamp runs at constant power for 2 hours. How much energy does it use?

   A) 320 Wh  B) 3200 Wh  C) 160 Wh  D) 162 Wh

22. Which statement about a catalyst is best?

   A) It is a reactant that always disappears completely  B) It creates atoms  C) It changes every reaction's equilibrium position  D) It helps a reaction follow a different pathway and is regenerated overall

23. Which vessel carries oxygen-poor blood from the human heart to the lungs?

   A) Renal vein  B) Pulmonary vein  C) Aorta  D) Pulmonary artery

24. A 27 V DC source is connected across an ohmic 9 Ω resistor. Find the current.

   A) 9 A  B) 3 A  C) 4 A  D) 27 A

25. A steel ship floats because:

   A) its overall average density including enclosed air can be lower than water's  B) steel has no mass  C) the sea has no gravity  D) buoyancy works only on wood

26. A student's scores in four tests are 10, 14, 16 and 20. The average is:

   A) 16  B) 20  C) 12  D) 15

27. The main reason for Earth's seasons is:

   A) day and night alone  B) Earth being much closer to the Sun every summer  C) the Moon's changing shape  D) axial tilt combined with Earth's revolution

28. A ray hits a plane mirror perpendicular to its surface. Angle of incidence measured from normal?

   A) 90°  B) 180°  C) 45°  D) 0°

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true, and the reason correctly explains the assertion.
B) Both true, but the reason does not correctly explain the assertion.
C) Assertion true, reason false.
D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. **Assertion:** An ohmic 11 Ω resistor connected across 22 V carries 2 A. **Reason:** For an ohmic resistor I = V/R, so 22/11 = 2 A.

30. **Assertion:** The sum of the first 12 positive odd numbers is 144. **Reason:** The Earth revolves around the Sun.

31. **Assertion:** A 22 V component transferring 22 J of energy to each coulomb has a potential difference of 22 V. **Reason:** A volt is a coulomb per joule.

32. **Assertion:** A sound wave of 190 Hz can travel through a vacuum. **Reason:** Sound is a mechanical wave that needs a material medium.

## Fill in the blanks (33–40)

33. The main cause of seasons includes Earth’s axial ___.

34. The Indian agency associated with space missions is ___.

35. India’s meteorological department is abbreviated ___.

36. The first words of the Constitution’s Preamble are We, the ___ of India.

37. A 10% discount on ₹330 is ₹___.

38. A 33 V source across an ohmic 11 Ω resistor gives a current of ___ A.

39. A 190 W device running for 2 hours uses ___ Wh.

40. The average of 19, 21 and 23 is ___.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **D** — Neutral means net charge is zero, not absence of charges.
2. **D** — I = 12/4 = 3 A.
3. **D** — Sarabhai was a leader in India's space programme.
4. **B** — Displacement is change in position, so it is zero after returning to the start. Distance is the full path length.
5. **A** — The objects can be at the same temperature but transfer heat at different rates.
6. **B** — Total parts = 7; each part is 7; first share = 3 × 7 = 21.
7. **A** — For 60 km each: total 120 km in 1+2=3 h; average 40 km/h.
8. **C** — A geostationary satellite is in an equatorial orbit, travelling with Earth's rotation and matching its rotational period.
9. **C** — Velocity includes direction. Turning changes velocity even if speed stays constant.
10. **C** — scientific explanations are tested against evidence.
11. **B** — Scientific theories explain evidence and are tested and refined.
12. **B** — Dominance concerns expression in a heterozygote, not frequency or value.
13. **C** — The discount is 10% of ₹200 = ₹20; the price is ₹180.
14. **D** — 1/R = 1/6 + 1/6 = 1/3, so R = 3 Ω.
15. **A** — Same proton number means the same element; a different neutron number means different isotopes.
16. **C** — Each independent fair coin toss has a 1/2 probability of heads.
17. **A** — the escaping gas and balloon gain opposite momentum; external forces can affect the real motion.
18. **C** — There are 8 parts, 96/8 = 12 each, so 5 parts = 60.
19. **D** — Total parts = 15; each part is 23; first share = 11 × 23 = 253.
20. **B** — 30 minutes = 0.5 h; energy = 1 kW × 0.5 h.
21. **A** — Energy = power × time = 160 × 2 = 320 Wh.
22. **D** — A catalyst offers another reaction pathway and is regenerated overall; it does not create matter or change equilibrium position under the same conditions.
23. **D** — Arteries carry blood away from the heart; the pulmonary artery goes to the lungs.
24. **B** — I = V/R = 27/9 = 3 A.
25. **A** — a floating ship displaces water whose weight balances its own.
26. **D** — 60/4 = 15.
27. **D** — Earth's axial tilt and revolution change sunlight angle and day length over the year.
28. **D** — A perpendicular ray travels along the normal.
29. **A** — Both are true; Ohm’s law explains the current.
30. **B** — Both statements are true, but the reason is unrelated to the sum.
31. **C** — A volt is one joule per coulomb, not the inverse.
32. **D** — Sound cannot travel through vacuum; it requires a medium.
33. **tilt** — Accepted answer: tilt
34. **ISRO** — Accepted answer: ISRO
35. **IMD** — Accepted answer: IMD
36. **people** — Accepted answer: people
37. **33** — Accepted answer: 33
38. **3** — Accepted answer: 3
39. **380** — Accepted answer: 380
40. **21** — Accepted answer: 21
