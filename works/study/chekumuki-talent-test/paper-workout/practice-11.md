## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. A 12 V ideal battery is connected to two resistors, 2 Ω and 4 Ω, in series. The voltage across the 4 Ω resistor is: A) 2 V B) 4 V C) 8 V D) 12 V.
2. Two resistors, 6 Ω and 3 Ω, are connected in parallel across an ideal 12 V supply. Total current is: A) 2 A B) 4 A C) 6 A D) 9 A.
3. A 60 W lamp and a 100 W lamp each operate at their rated power for 5 h. Their combined energy use is: A) 0.08 kWh B) 0.8 kWh C) 1.6 kWh D) 8 kWh.
4. An ohmic resistor is connected to an ideal source. If its voltage doubles while resistance stays constant, its power becomes: A) half B) twice C) four times D) unchanged.
5. A passenger leans forward when a moving bus brakes. The best explanation is: A) the body tends to retain its previous motion while the bus slows B) gravity points forward C) the bus loses all mass D) the passenger is pulled forward by a new permanent force.
6. A student walks 30 m east, then 40 m west. Distance and displacement magnitude respectively are: A) 10 m, 70 m B) 70 m, 10 m C) 70 m, 70 m D) 10 m, 10 m.
7. A car covers 60 km at 60 km/h and another 60 km at 30 km/h. Average speed is: A) 30 km/h B) 40 km/h C) 45 km/h D) 50 km/h.
8. A floating object displaces 200 g of water. Its mass is: A) 100 g B) 200 g C) 400 g D) impossible to infer even when floating at rest.
9. A ray moves from glass to air. Total internal reflection requires: A) incidence angle greater than the critical angle B) any angle including zero C) light moving from air to glass D) light to have zero frequency.
10. A sound echo returns after 0.4 s; sound speed is 340 m/s. Distance to the wall is: A) 34 m B) 68 m C) 136 m D) 170 m.
11. A vessel contains sand, salt and water. Which sequence can recover sand and then crystals of salt? A) Filtration, then suitable crystallisation B) Filtration alone C) Magnetic separation of salt, then filtration D) Sieving out dissolved ions.
12. Isotopes have the same atomic number but different mass numbers because they differ in: A) protons B) neutrons C) chemical symbols D) proton number and element.
13. Zinc is placed in copper sulfate solution. Which observation is consistent with zinc being more reactive than copper? A) Copper is deposited and zinc enters solution B) Zinc forms from copper C) No change is possible D) All atoms disappear.
14. Which is balanced? A) 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO B) Mg + O₂ → MgO C) 2Mg + 2O₂ → MgO D) Mg + O₂ → 2MgO.
15. A student claims a clear liquid must be safe drinking water. Best response: A) clear means sterile B) invisible microbes or dissolved contaminants can still be present C) dissolved salt can always be seen D) any clear liquid is pure H₂O.
16. An acid is added gradually to sodium hydroxide solution. While the solution remains basic, its pH generally: A) increases B) decreases C) remains exactly 14 D) becomes negative instantly.
17. A potted plant is placed in darkness overnight and tested for starch before an experiment. This helps mainly to: A) reduce previously stored leaf starch B) stop all respiration forever C) add carbon dioxide D) create chlorophyll in darkness.
18. A plant leaf exposed to light in an atmosphere without available carbon dioxide produces no starch in a properly controlled experiment. This supports: A) CO₂ is needed for photosynthesis B) oxygen is the only raw material C) light is unnecessary D) all plant respiration stops.
19. A touch-me-not leaf closes when touched; a tendril coils around a support. Which is correct? A) Both prove a human-like nervous system B) Leaf closing is a rapid touch response; tendril coiling is directional growth C) Both are photosynthesis D) Only animals respond to stimuli.
20. Which route correctly carries oxygen-poor blood from the heart towards the lungs? A) Pulmonary artery B) Pulmonary vein C) Aorta D) Coronary vein towards lungs.
21. A food chain is grass → grasshopper → frog → snake. If frogs sharply decline, which is a plausible short-term effect, other things being equal? A) Grasshoppers may increase B) Grasshoppers must disappear immediately C) Snakes become producers D) Energy moves backwards from snake to grass.
22. A false science claim is widely shared by many people. Which response best shows scientific temper? A) Accept it because it is popular B) Examine evidence, source and alternative explanations C) Reject all evidence D) Share it immediately.
23. A student is excluded from school during menstruation. The best response is: A) support exclusion B) explain that menstruation is normal and support access to school C) claim it is contagious D) refuse to discuss health.
24. A student receives an unexpected message asking for an OTP to release a prize. Best first action: A) give the OTP B) do not share it and verify independently with a trusted adult C) pay first D) send bank credentials.
25. A shirt costs ₹800. It is discounted by 25%, then by a further 10% on the reduced price. Final cost: A) ₹480 B) ₹540 C) ₹520 D) ₹600.
26. Five equally weighted tests have average 16. Four scores are 12, 15, 17 and 20. The fifth score is: A) 12 B) 14 C) 16 D) 20.
27. Two whole-number quantities have HCF 6 and LCM 120. If their sum is 54, their difference is: A) 6 B) 12 C) 18 D) 24.
28. A die is fair. What is the probability of rolling a number that is both even and greater than 3? A) 1/6 B) 1/3 C) 1/2 D) 2/3.

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true; reason correctly explains assertion. B) Both true; reason does not correctly explain assertion. C) Assertion true, reason false. D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. Assertion: Two resistors in series carry the same steady current. Reason: The circuit provides only one path for charge flow through both resistors.
30. Assertion: The first five odd positive numbers sum to 25. Reason: The Earth orbits the Sun.
31. Assertion: An LED can use less power than an incandescent bulb at similar brightness. Reason: All electrical energy in an incandescent bulb becomes visible light.
32. Assertion: The pulmonary artery carries blood towards the heart. Reason: Arteries carry blood away from the heart.

34. A 1.5 kW heater runs for 2 h at constant power. It uses ___ kWh.
35. A tablet is taken immediately and then every 6 h. The fifth tablet is taken ___ hours after the first.
36. The oxygen released during photosynthesis comes mainly from ___ molecules.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **C:** total resistance 6 Ω; current 12/6 = 2 A; voltage across 4 Ω is 2 × 4 = 8 V.
2. **C:** 12/6 = 2 A and 12/3 = 4 A; total 6 A.
3. **B:** (60 + 100) W × 5 h = 800 Wh = 0.8 kWh.
4. **C:** P = V²/R; doubling V at fixed R multiplies P by 4.
5. **A:** inertia is resistance to a change in motion.
6. **B:** path = 30 + 40 = 70 m; final position is 10 m west of start.
7. **B:** 60 km/60 km/h = 1 h; 60/30 = 2 h; 120/3 = 40 km/h.
8. **B:** when floating in balance, displaced water's weight equals the object's weight; at the same gravitational field their masses are equal.
9. **A:** light must travel from higher to lower refractive index and exceed the critical angle.
10. **B:** 340 × 0.4 / 2 = 68 m; the sound travels out and back.
11. **A:** filter insoluble sand, then crystallise salt from the solution under suitable conditions.
12. **B:** same element means same proton count; isotopes differ in neutron count.
13. **A:** zinc displaces copper from copper sulfate under suitable conditions.
14. **A:** both sides contain 2 magnesium and 2 oxygen atoms.
15. **B:** clarity does not demonstrate safety; treatment depends on contaminants.
16. **B:** neutralisation decreases hydroxide concentration in this simplified situation.
17. **A:** destarching reduces stored starch, making a later starch test more informative.
18. **A:** a control with CO₂ available is needed for a persuasive comparison.
19. **B:** the responses differ; plants respond without a human-like nervous system.
20. **A:** arteries carry blood away from the heart; pulmonary arteries go to the lungs.
21. **A:** fewer frogs may leave more grasshoppers; food webs and later effects are complex.
22. **B:** popularity is not proof; test claims against evidence.
23. **B:** menstruation is normal and should not prevent access to education.
24. **B:** do not share OTPs; verify via an independent official channel.
25. **B:** ₹800 × 0.75 = ₹600, then × 0.9 = ₹540.
26. **C:** five-score total 80; known scores total 64; fifth = 16.
27. **A:** product = 6 × 120 = 720; 24 and 30 sum to 54, difference 6.
28. **B:** favourable outcomes 4 and 6: 2/6 = 1/3.
29. **A:** both true, and the single-path reason explains equal current.
30. **B:** both true, but Earth's orbit does not explain the sum.
31. **C:** LEDs can be more efficient; incandescent bulbs also produce substantial heat.
32. **D:** pulmonary arteries go away from the heart; the reason is true.
33. **8 Ω:** R = V/I = 24/3.
34. **3 kWh:** 1.5 × 2.
35. **24 h:** five tablets have four intervals, 4 × 6.
36. **Water / H₂O.**
37. **18:** (12 + 16 + 20 + 24)/4.
38. **Phloem.**
39. **₹400:** 1,000 × 0.5 × 0.8.
40. **Coulomb.**
