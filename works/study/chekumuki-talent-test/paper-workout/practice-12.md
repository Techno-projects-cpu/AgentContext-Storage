# Practice Paper 12 — Heavy Sword: Reason, Don't Guess ⚔️

**Original challenge, not an official district paper.** Suggested practice only: 60 minutes, 40 marks, no negative marks; verify the actual district rules. Includes 28 MCQs, 4 assertion–reason and 8 fill-ins. All answers and explanations follow. [Workout index](README.md)

## Multiple choice (1–28)

1. A 6 Ω resistor is connected across a 12 V DC source. It operates for 10 s at constant conditions. Energy transferred is: A) 24 J B) 120 J C) 240 J D) 720 J.
2. Two identical ohmic resistors are connected first in series and then in parallel across the same ideal battery. The ratio of total current in parallel to total current in series is: A) 1:4 B) 1:2 C) 2:1 D) 4:1.
3. An ammeter should be connected to measure current through a component: A) in series B) across the battery only C) in parallel across the component D) without a circuit.
4. A 1 kW heater operates for 15 min and a 100 W bulb operates for 2 h, each at constant power. Their total energy is: A) 0.25 kWh B) 0.45 kWh C) 1.2 kWh D) 1.45 kWh.
5. A 2 kg object changes velocity from 3 m/s east to 5 m/s east. Its momentum change is: A) 2 kg m/s east B) 4 kg m/s east C) 6 kg m/s west D) 10 kg m/s east.
6. A moving car has zero net force for a short interval. Ignoring other complications, it: A) must stop instantly B) continues with constant velocity C) moves in a circle at constant speed D) accelerates forward.
7. A force of 20 N acts uniformly over 0.5 m². Pressure is: A) 10 Pa B) 20 Pa C) 40 Pa D) 100 Pa.
8. A 150 g object occupies 50 cm³. Density is: A) 0.3 g/cm³ B) 3 g/cm³ C) 100 g/cm³ D) 200 g/cm³.
9. The same object is heated while melting at constant pressure. During the phase transition, the energy supplied can: A) change state while temperature remains constant B) disappear C) make mass exactly zero D) necessarily lower the temperature continuously.
10. A wave travels at 340 m/s with frequency 170 Hz. Wavelength is: A) 0.5 m B) 2 m C) 170 m D) 510 m.
11. Which process gives the most direct evidence that a chemical change has occurred? A) A new substance forms B) A material is cut C) A substance melts D) Water is poured into a cup.
12. Iron rusts in an open dish and gains mass. What best explains this? A) Oxygen from air combines with iron B) Mass is created from nothing C) Iron loses all its atoms D) Temperature always becomes zero.
13. At 25°C, which is more acidic? A) pH 3 B) pH 5 C) both equally acidic D) pH cannot compare acidity at the same conditions.
14. In water, common salt conducts electricity primarily because: A) it produces moving ions B) the salt disappears completely C) electrons flow freely through pure plastic D) light carries the charge.
15. A catalyst: A) offers an alternative reaction pathway and is regenerated overall B) creates atoms C) must change the final equilibrium position D) is always a fuel.
16. A solution contains dissolved copper sulfate. To obtain crystals, a suitable school-level separation method is: A) crystallisation B) ordinary filtration alone C) magnetic separation D) sieving individual ions.
17. Which of the following is an appropriate control when testing whether light is required for starch formation in leaves? A) A similar leaf exposed to light under comparable conditions B) A rock in darkness C) A plant with different soil, species and water D) A leaf tested before any preparation only.
18. In an experiment, three plants in one group receive more light; three similar plants receive less. Water, soil and time are kept similar. The dependent variable is: A) light exposure B) measured plant growth C) pot colour alone D) the researcher's name.
19. Antibiotics are not effective against influenza virus because: A) viruses differ from bacteria and antibiotics target specific bacterial structures/processes B) viruses are always larger than bacteria C) antibiotics are vitamins D) fever is never caused by infection.
20. Why does blood from the lungs reach the heart via the pulmonary vein? A) Veins carry blood towards the heart B) All veins carry oxygen-poor blood C) Arteries always carry blood towards the heart D) Lungs have no blood vessels.
21. A plant's xylem is severely damaged. Which function is most directly affected? A) Transport of water and dissolved minerals B) Transport of sugars only C) Production of seeds in every case D) Escape of sound waves.
22. A person touches a hot surface and quickly withdraws a hand. Which statement best describes the immediate response? A) A reflex arc can produce a rapid involuntary response B) Plants produced the movement C) The response proves the person has no spinal cord D) The hand has no sensory receptors.
23. Earthquakes are best investigated by: A) measured seismic data and independently checked observations B) forwarding an unverified rumour C) predicting exact dates from dreams D) ignoring instruments.
24. The 2024 FIDE World Championship match in Singapore was won by: A) Gukesh D B) Ding Liren C) C. V. Raman D) Vikram Sarabhai.
25. Two quantities are in the ratio 3:5 and total 96. The larger is: A) 36 B) 48 C) 60 D) 80.
26. In triangle ABC, D on AB and E on AC, DE is parallel to BC. AD = 4 cm, DB = 6 cm and AE = 6 cm. EC is: A) 4 cm B) 6 cm C) 9 cm D) 15 cm.
27. A student takes the first of 12 tablets immediately and one every 8 hours. Time from the first to the last tablet is: A) 80 h B) 88 h C) 96 h D) 104 h. This is a maths question, not medical advice.
28. A price increases from ₹200 to ₹250. Percentage increase is: A) 20% B) 25% C) 50% D) 125%.

## Assertion–reason (29–32)

A) Both true; reason correctly explains assertion. B) Both true; reason does not correctly explain assertion. C) Assertion true, reason false. D) Assertion false, reason true.

29. Assertion: Dissolved sodium chloride can conduct electricity. Reason: Its ions can move through the solution.
30. Assertion: Red blood cells transport oxygen. Reason: Earth revolves around the Sun.
31. Assertion: A convex lens may produce a real image for a suitably placed real object. Reason: A real image is formed by rays that only appear to meet.
32. Assertion: All arteries carry oxygen-rich blood. Reason: Arteries carry blood away from the heart.

## Answer sheet and explanations

1. **C:** I = 12/6 = 2 A; P = VI = 24 W; E = 24 × 10 = 240 J.
2. **D:** series resistance is 2R, parallel is R/2; V/(R/2) divided by V/(2R) is 4.
3. **A:** current is measured through the component, so the ammeter joins the path.
4. **B:** 1 × 0.25 + 0.1 × 2 = 0.25 + 0.2 = 0.45 kWh.
5. **B:** Δp = mΔv = 2 × (5 − 3) = 4 kg m/s east.
6. **B:** zero net force gives zero acceleration, so velocity is constant.
7. **C:** P = F/A = 20/0.5 = 40 Pa.
8. **B:** density = 150/50 = 3 g/cm³.
9. **A:** energy can change state without changing temperature during the transition at constant pressure.
10. **B:** wavelength = speed/frequency = 340/170 = 2 m.
11. **A:** a chemical reaction forms new substance(s); the other examples are physical changes.
12. **A:** oxygen joins the iron; total mass is conserved when the surroundings are included.
13. **A:** lower pH means greater hydrogen-ion activity on the pH scale.
14. **A:** dissolved ions carry charge.
15. **A:** catalysts change pathways and are regenerated overall; they do not shift equilibrium by themselves.
16. **A:** crystallisation can recover crystals; ordinary filtration cannot catch dissolved ions.
17. **A:** a comparable light-exposed control isolates the light condition more effectively.
18. **B:** growth is the measured response; light is the independent variable.
19. **A:** antibiotics target bacterial structures or processes; influenza is viral.
20. **A:** vein and artery are defined by direction relative to the heart.
21. **A:** xylem carries water and dissolved minerals.
22. **A:** reflexes can act rapidly through a neural pathway; seek adult medical help for burns.
23. **A:** evidence and independent verification are essential.
24. **A:** Gukesh defeated Ding Liren in the 2024 match; do not infer the result of later championships.
25. **C:** 96/8 = 12 per part; 5 × 12 = 60.
26. **C:** AD/DB = AE/EC gives 4/6 = 6/EC; EC = 9 cm.
27. **B:** 12 tablets involve 11 intervals after the first; 11 × 8 = 88 h.
28. **B:** increase is 50; 50/200 × 100 = 25%.
29. **A:** both true, and mobile ions explain conductivity.
30. **B:** both true, but Earth's orbit does not explain oxygen transport.
31. **C:** real image rays actually meet; virtual rays appear to meet.
32. **D:** pulmonary arteries carry oxygen-poor blood away from the heart.
33. **6 Ω:** R = 18/3.
34. **300 Wh:** 75 × 4.
35. **40 km/h:** 120/3.
36. **Cellulose.**
37. **10:** one pH unit corresponds to a tenfold change in hydrogen-ion activity.
38. **Decomposers.**
39. **Full:** alignment is also required, which is why eclipses do not occur every full moon.
