# 2. Officials, constitutional provisions and laws

## Who does what?

**Do not memorise a single rigid chain:** roll administration and election conduct are related but distinct. Depending on the constituency, an officer may hold more than one designation; there are statutory reporting relationships and ECI supervision rather than a simple BLO → Presiding Officer → ERO chain.

| Role | Telugu / key responsibility | Common confusion |
|---|---|---|
| Election Commission of India (ECI) | భారత ఎన్నికల సంఘం; superintendence, direction and control of electoral rolls and elections for Parliament, state legislatures and President/Vice-President | State Election Commissions handle local-body elections under Arts. 243K/243ZA. |
| Chief Electoral Officer (CEO) | రాష్ట్ర ముఖ్య ఎన్నికల అధికారి; state-level election and roll supervision under ECI | Not the Chief Election Commissioner (CEC). |
| District Election Officer (DEO) | జిల్లా ఎన్నికల అధికారి; coordinates/supervises district election work; provides stations with ECI approval, appoints polling staff | Normally District Collector/DM; not necessarily RO for every constituency. |
| Electoral Registration Officer (ERO) | ఓటరు నమోదు అధికారి; prepares and maintains roll, decides claims and objections under law | RO conducts the constituency election; ERO maintains the roll. |
| Assistant ERO (AERO) | సహాయ ఓటరు నమోదు అధికారి; assists ERO, may exercise ERO functions subject to ERO control | AERO is not ARO. |
| Booth Level Officer (BLO) | బూత్ స్థాయి అధికారి; local field verification, roll assistance and voter outreach under roll machinery | Not the polling-station chief or a party representative. |
| Returning Officer (RO) | రిటర్నింగ్ అధికారి / ఎన్నికల నిర్వహణ అధికారి; accepts and scrutinises nominations, conducts counting, declares constituency result | Not the person responsible for maintaining all rolls. |
| Assistant RO (ARO) | సహాయ రిటర్నింగ్ అధికారి; assists RO; scrutiny normally reserved for RO unless RO unavoidably prevented | ARO ≠ AERO. |
| Presiding Officer (PrO) | పోలింగ్ కేంద్ర అధ్యక్ష అధికారి; in charge of one polling station, fair poll, order, sealing and Form 17C | PrO ≠ President of India; not RO. |
| Polling officers | పోలింగ్ అధికారులు; verify identity/roll, ink, register, operate control unit as assigned | Individual assignments can vary. |
| Sector officer | సెక్టార్ అధికారి; monitors a group of booths, reports problems, coordinates support and replacement equipment per instructions | Supports the process, not the officer who declares the constituency result. |
| Observer | పరిశీలకుడు; ECI-appointed oversight, reports and interventions; statutory observers under RPA 1951 §20B have specified powers | Independent monitoring, not a candidate's agent. |
| Micro-observer | సూక్ష్మ పరిశీలకుడు; observes prescribed proceedings at assigned booth and reports through observer system | Not the Presiding Officer. |
| Candidate's polling / counting agent | అభ్యర్థి పోలింగ్ / లెక్కింపు ప్రతినిధి; witnesses booth / counting processes and raises permissible objections | Represents candidate, is not a government election officer. |
| Booth Level Agent (BLA) | పార్టీ బూత్ స్థాయి ప్రతినిధి; party-side assistance regarding rolls | BLA ≠ BLO. |

Roll functions: **ECI → CEO → district coordination → ERO → AERO/BLO**. Conduct functions: **ECI / CEO / district coordination → RO / ARO → polling and counting arrangements; PrO leads each booth**. These are memory aids, not exhaustive legal command charts. RPA 1950 §13CC and RPA 1951 §28A put relevant staff on deemed deputation to ECI during election duties.

## Constitution: high-yield articles

| Article | What to remember |
|---|---|
| 324 | ECI's superintendence, direction and control of electoral rolls and elections to Parliament/state legislatures and offices of President/Vice-President. Power operates within law, not above it. |
| 325 | One general electoral roll per territorial constituency; no exclusion or special roll solely on religion, race, caste or sex. |
| 326 | Elections to Lok Sabha and state assemblies on adult suffrage, subject to lawful disqualifications; **18** from the 61st Amendment (1988). |
| 327 / 328 | Parliament / state legislature may legislate on elections within constitutional limits; state law subject to parliamentary law. |
| 329 | Judicial intervention in electoral matters is restricted; elections are ordinarily challenged by election petition as provided by law, not interrupted at each step. It is not a blanket ban on all judicial review. |
| 81 / 170 | Composition and territorial constituencies of Lok Sabha / state Assemblies; subject to relevant constitutional provisions. |
| 99 / 188 | Oath or affirmation by elected MPs / MLAs before taking a seat. |
| 243K / 243ZA | State Election Commission for Panchayat / municipal elections, not the ECI's usual direct-election remit. |

## The legislation and rules

| Instrument | Focus and useful provisions |
|---|---|
| **RPA 1950** | Seats, electoral rolls, registration. §§13A, 13AA, 13B, 13C and 13CC: CEO, DEO, ERO, AERO, deputation. §14: qualifying dates. §§19–20: registration eligibility/ordinary residence. §21: preparation/revision, including special revision under §21(3) with recorded reasons. §§22–23: correction and inclusion, with safeguards and election-period restrictions. |
| **Registration of Electors Rules 1960** | Process for roll preparation/revision, claims, objections and prescribed voter forms. ERO decides under applicable law. |
| **RPA 1951** | Election conduct, qualifications, corrupt practices, offences, disputes. §§20B–28A: observers, RO/ARO, stations and poll personnel; §§30–39: schedule, nominations, scrutiny and withdrawal; §§33A, 34: disclosure and deposit; §61A EVMs; §65 tie; §66 result; §§77–78 candidate election accounts; §10A disqualification for failure to lodge accounts; §123 corrupt practices; §§126, 126A silence and exit polls; §128 secrecy; §151A by-election timing (six months, subject to exceptions). |
| **Conduct of Elections Rules 1961** | Operational forms and procedures. Rule 4A/Form 26 affidavit; Rule 54A postal votes; Rule 56D application for VVPAT paper slips; Rule 63 recount; Rule 64 declaration/return; Rule 66 certificate. ECI instructions and judicial directions supplement these. |
| **MCC** | Agreed behavioural code; generally applies from schedule announcement to completion. Not itself a single statute, but related conduct can violate enforceable laws, and ECI can act under its authority and directions. |
| **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023**, Chapter IX | Election offences include bribery (§170), undue influence (§171) and personation (§172). RPA corrupt practice and criminal offence are distinct legal concepts, even where conduct overlaps. |

### Candidate expenses: frequently tested

RPA 1951 §77 requires a separate, correct account of election expenses; §78 generally requires lodging the account with the DEO within **30 days after election of the returned candidate**. Failure without good reason can lead to ECI disqualification under §10A for **three years**. The 2022 ceilings include **₹95 lakh Lok Sabha / ₹40 lakh Assembly** for larger states including Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, and **₹75 lakh / ₹28 lakh** in specified smaller states/UTs; verify the applicable current schedule and exceptions. These are **candidate ceilings**, not a universal party expenditure cap.

### ECI composition and independence

The Constitution permits a Chief Election Commissioner and other Election Commissioners as fixed by the President. It has been a three-member body since 1993. Under the 2023 appointment law, the selection committee comprises the Prime Minister, Leader of Opposition in Lok Sabha (or leader of the largest opposition party where there is no recognised LoP), and a Union Cabinet Minister nominated by the PM. Members serve six years or until 65, whichever earlier, subject to the Act. The **CEC's removal** is like a Supreme Court judge's; another EC cannot be removed except on the CEC's recommendation (Article 324). Independence also requires impartial staff, transparent process and effective remedies.
