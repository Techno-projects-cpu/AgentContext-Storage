# 6. Essay toolkit

## A winning structure

**Introduction:** define the issue and state your argument. **Body:** walk through institutions/process, safeguards and challenges, then practical improvements. **Conclusion:** return to constitutional values and citizen responsibility. Use headings if allowed. Explain terms on first use; do not stuff an essay with unverified numbers. For a quiz, say the exact article/form only when confident.

### Outline 1: From announcement to result
1. ECI and Article 324; roll preparation before announcement.
2. Announcement/MCC versus formal notification.
3. Nomination → scrutiny → withdrawal → final list.
4. Campaign, silence period, inclusive polling, mock poll, EVM/VVPAT and secrecy.
5. Secure storage, postal and EVM counting, Form 17C/20, VVPAT checks, declaration by RO.
6. Government formation and oath are distinct; importance of transparency and participation.

### Outline 2: Officials as guardians of a fair election
1. Constitutional independence and decentralised administration.
2. CEO/DEO coordinate; ERO/AERO/BLO maintain rolls; RO/ARO manage elections.
3. Presiding and polling officers at booth; sector staff, observers and security.
4. Distinguish candidate agents from public officials; maintain neutrality.
5. Training, accountability, inclusion, timely remedies and oversight.

### Outline 3: Technology, integrity and inclusion
1. Free choice requires a correct roll, secrecy and credible count.
2. EVM and VVPAT, mock poll, seals, 17C, strong rooms, sample verification and agents.
3. Benefits: speed and audit trail; concerns: trust, access, administrative error.
4. Improve disclosure, independent oversight, accessibility, grievance redress and public explanation.
5. End with technology as a tool, not a substitute for institutions and citizen trust.

## Model essay 1 — about 120 words

### From announcement to winner

India's elections turn millions of individual choices into democratic representation. The Election Commission of India announces the schedule, bringing the Model Code of Conduct into operation. The formal notification calls the election. Candidates submit nominations, which the Returning Officer scrutinises; valid candidates may then withdraw by the deadline.

During campaigning, legal restrictions and the code protect fair competition. At the polling station, officials verify voters and protect the secrecy of the vote. Electronic Voting Machines record votes, while VVPAT provides a paper audit trail. After polling, sealed equipment is stored securely. Postal votes and electronic votes are counted under prescribed procedures, with checks and authorised observers. The Returning Officer declares the candidate with the most valid votes elected. Transparent procedures and informed participation make every stage meaningful.

## Model essay 2 — about 250 words

### The officials behind a free and fair election

A free and fair election requires more than a polling day. It depends on a network of impartial officials, clear legal duties and public confidence. Article 324 entrusts the Election Commission of India with superintendence, direction and control over elections to Parliament and state legislatures.

Before the election, the Electoral Registration Officer maintains the electoral roll, assisted by Assistant Electoral Registration Officers and Booth Level Officers. BLOs help verify details locally, but they do not declare winners. The Chief Electoral Officer supervises election work at state level, while the District Election Officer coordinates district arrangements, including polling personnel and stations.

The Returning Officer is responsible for conducting the constituency election. The RO receives and scrutinises nominations, manages the election process, supervises counting and declares the result. Assistant Returning Officers help within their authorised powers. At each polling station the Presiding Officer maintains order and ensures a fair poll, assisted by polling officers who check identity, maintain the register and operate equipment. A candidate's polling agent is a witness and representative, not a polling official.

Observers and sector officers add monitoring and support. After polling, accounts and sealed equipment are secured; during counting, agents may observe and raise permitted objections. Accurate records, the secrecy of voting, accessibility for persons with disabilities and timely remedies are as important as speed.

These responsibilities are complementary, not a simple ladder in which every official reports to the next. Together, an independent commission and trained, neutral staff help turn the right to vote into a credible result.

## Model essay 3 — about 450 words

### Indian elections: procedure, integrity and the citizen

An election is a chain of public responsibilities, not a single day of voting. In a democracy, its purpose is to translate citizens' choices into representation through an inclusive, transparent and peaceful process. India's Constitution and election laws distribute these responsibilities among the Election Commission of India, electoral officers, candidates and voters.

The groundwork begins with the electoral roll. The Electoral Registration Officer, supported by local Booth Level Officers, maintains the list and deals with claims and objections according to law. An eligible citizen should check that their name is enrolled: an identity card alone cannot replace a roll entry. Article 326 provides for adult suffrage, while Article 325 protects the principle of a general roll without exclusion solely on specified grounds.

The Commission announces the election schedule, and the Model Code of Conduct ordinarily begins to apply. The statutory notification then formally calls the election. Candidates submit nomination papers, required deposits and disclosure affidavits. The Returning Officer scrutinises these papers, and valid nominees can withdraw before the deadline. The resulting list tells electors who is contesting. During the campaign, the code and the law seek to check misuse of official power, inducement and other unfair practices. Specified restrictions apply during the 48 hours ending with the close of polling.

On polling day, the Presiding Officer and polling officers manage each station. A mock poll tests the equipment before voting begins. Staff verify each elector and obtain a signature or thumb impression in Form 17A. The voter chooses privately on the ballot unit; the VVPAT briefly shows a printed record before it falls into a sealed box. Eligible categories may use postal ballots according to the applicable rules. These arrangements must protect both access and secrecy.

At the end of polling, officials prepare the station's account in Form 17C and secure machines and papers. On counting day, postal ballots and EVM votes are counted according to the rules, with VVPAT verification under applicable directions. The result is consolidated in Form 20. The Returning Officer resolves valid requests under the rules and declares the candidate who has the most valid votes elected. The declaration is different from provisional media trends and from the subsequent formation of a government.

Yet a well-designed process must be implemented fairly. Mistakes in rolls, misinformation, money power and barriers for vulnerable voters can weaken trust. Clear explanations, trained neutral officials, accessible booths, genuine opportunities to correct errors and effective legal remedies help address these risks. Citizens also have a duty to verify information, resist inducements and respect the choice of others. When institutions and citizens perform these roles responsibly, an election becomes an expression of constitutional democracy.

### Last-minute essay checklist

- Use **Election Commission**, **Returning Officer**, **electoral roll**, **secrecy** and **free and fair** correctly.
- Distinguish announcement from notification, and highest votes from majority.
- Give a balanced paragraph on both safeguards and challenges.
- Never invent a current result, statutory number or quotation to sound impressive.
