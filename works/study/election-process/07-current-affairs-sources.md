## Stable historical anchors

- **25 January 1950:** ECI established; 25 January is National Voters' Day.
- **1982:** EVMs first trialled at selected stations in the Paravur Assembly by-election in Kerala; **2013:** VVPAT first used in the Noksen by-election in Nagaland. [EVM history](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/electronic-voting-machine-evm/).
- **2013:** Supreme Court's *PUCL* decision directed a NOTA option protecting secrecy. [Judgment](https://pucl.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Peoples_Union_for_Civil_Liberties_and_Ors_vs_Unions130911COM43225.pdf).
- **2024 general election:** 543 Lok Sabha constituencies; approximately **96.88 crore registered electors** in the pre-election roll. The reported **65.79% polling-station turnout excluded postal ballots** at the time of reporting; don't confuse it with final all-votes turnout. [Contemporaneous report](https://www.business-standard.com/elections/lok-sabha-election/65-79-voter-turnout-recorded-in-2024-elections-postal-ballot-pending-124060600746_1.html).
- **1 August 2022:** revised voter forms and four qualifying dates made enrolment and correction easier. [Qualifying dates background](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/kerala/four-chances-a-year-to-enrol-in-electoral-roll/article65762589.ece).

## Primary-source toolkit

1. [ECI official site](https://www.eci.gov.in/) — latest instructions, press notes, handbooks and election schedules.
2. [ECI official results](https://results.eci.gov.in/) — constituency and party results; note the election and whether data is final.
3. [India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/) — Constitution, Acts and current statutory text. Verify the amendment and commencement date; older handbooks can be superseded.
4. [RPA 1950, CEO Delhi](https://ceodelhi.gov.in/WriteReadData/ManualElectionLaw/REPRESENTATION%20OF%20THE%20PEOPLE%20ACT,%201950.pdf); [RPA 1951, CEO Kerala](https://www.ceo.kerala.gov.in/ceokerala/backend/public/uploads/pdfs/representation-of-the-people-acti-1951.pdf).
5. [Presiding Officer handbook](https://ceogoa.nic.in/pdf/ECIH/Presiding%20Officer%20Handbook.pdf); [polling agent handbook](https://ceogoa.nic.in/pdf/ImportantDocs/Handbook%20for%20Polling%20Agent.pdf); [ERO handbook](https://ceo.mizoram.gov.in/files/HB_for_ERO.pdf); [official RO forms](https://ceo.gujarat.gov.in/Forms-for-Returning-Officers).
6. [Voters' Services Portal](https://voters.eci.gov.in/) — roll search and services; the voter helpline is **1950**. For AP or Telangana use the relevant official CEO/DEO notice for local deadlines.

**Source discipline:** This pack is study material, not an official legal instruction. Newspaper articles support dated reports, not legal authority. For a competition, the most recent official gazette, ECI direction, result sheet and binding judgment prevail over older guides or preliminary news.
