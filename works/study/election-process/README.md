## 30-second memory map

**Announcement/MCC → notification → nominations → scrutiny → withdrawal → final candidates & symbols → campaign → poll → strong room → counting → RO declares result → certificate → oath.** Preparation of electoral rolls and polling arrangements begins earlier and continues as allowed by law. Campaigning may begin before nominations and overlap other stages.

**Three anchors:** ERO = roll; Presiding Officer = booth; Returning Officer = election and result. ECI supervises; DEO coordinates the district; CEO supervises in the state.

**Paper trail:** Form 17A (voters' register) → 17C (poll account/results of counting) → 20 (final result sheet) → 21C/21D (declaration, as applicable) → 21E (return) → 22 (certificate). Each has its own purpose; this is not a single sheet passed down a chain.

## Suggested use

- **Day 1:** Timeline + officials. Explain a booth's day aloud without notes.
- **Day 2:** Law and forms; create flashcards for Articles 324–329 and the two RP Acts.
- **Day 3:** Attempt MCQs 1–60; check explanations and revisit mistakes.
- **Day 4:** Attempt 61–120; write a 250-word essay in 20 minutes.
- **Day 5:** Revise current affairs against official updates; do a timed oral recap.

**Scope warning:** Presidential and Rajya Sabha elections use different electoral colleges and voting methods; local bodies are supervised by State Election Commissions, not the ECI. Do not apply the Lok Sabha/Assembly first-past-the-post rules blindly to them.
