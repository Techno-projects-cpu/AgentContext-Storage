# 2. Officials — who does what?

## Remember these three first

| Official | Think | Main job |
|---|---|---|
| **ERO** — Electoral Registration Officer | **Roll** | Maintains the constituency's voter list (ఓటర్ల జాబితా). |
| **Presiding Officer** | **Booth** | Heads one polling station and ensures orderly, fair polling. |
| **RO** — Returning Officer | **Result** | Conducts the constituency election, scrutinises nominations and declares the result. |

**Memory line: ERO = roll; Presiding Officer = booth; RO = result.**

## The supporting team

| Official | Main job |
|---|---|
| **ECI** — Election Commission of India | Supervises elections to Parliament and State Assemblies across India. |
| **CEO** — Chief Electoral Officer | Supervises election work in the state under ECI. |
| **DEO** — District Election Officer | Coordinates district election work and appoints polling staff. |
| **BLO** — Booth Level Officer | Helps with local voter-list verification and voter assistance. |
| **Polling Officer** | Helps at the booth: identity checks, voter register, ink or machine operation, as assigned. |
| **Observer** | Monitors the election and reports under ECI's arrangements. |
| **Polling / counting agent** | Represents a candidate and observes proceedings; **not** a government election officer. |

**Not a single ladder:** Roll work and election conduct are related but separate; a BLO does not simply report to the Presiding Officer as part of a universal chain.

### Don't mix these up

- **BLO** = election field official; **BLA** = political party's booth-level agent.
- **AERO** assists the ERO; **ARO** assists the RO.
- **ECI** handles Lok Sabha/Assembly elections; the **State Election Commission** ordinarily handles Panchayat/municipal elections.

**Quick test:** Who runs your polling station? **Presiding Officer.** Who maintains the roll? **ERO.** Who declares the winner? **RO.**
