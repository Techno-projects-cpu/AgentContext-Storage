# 4. Voting and counting — from button to result

## At the polling station

1. Officials check the voter's **roll entry and identity**. An EPIC card alone is not enough if the person's name is absent from the relevant roll.
2. Voter signs or gives a thumb impression in **Form 17A** (register of voters).
3. Voter presses a choice privately on the **ballot unit**. The **control unit** records the electronic vote.
4. **VVPAT** briefly displays a printed slip, about **7 seconds**; it falls into a sealed box. The voter does not take it home.
5. At close of poll, the Presiding Officer prepares **Form 17C Part I**, the account of votes recorded, and machines are sealed and stored securely.

**Before voting:** a mock poll checks the equipment; mock votes are cleared before the actual poll.

## Counting and result

- Postal ballots are taken up first under the rules; EVM results are counted according to ECI instructions.
- **Form 17C Part II** records the counting result for the polling station.
- **Form 20** consolidates the constituency result, including postal votes.
- Required VVPAT verification and any valid recount requests are dealt with; the **RO declares the result**.
- **Form 21C** declares a contested general-election result; **21D** a contested by-election result. **Form 22** is the certificate of election.

**Form memory:** **17A = attendance/register; 17C = booth account; 20 = total; 22 = certificate.** Form 21E is the return of election.

## Two frequent traps

- **NOTA** means “None of the Above” (పైవారిలో ఎవరూ కాదు). It is not a candidate and does **not** automatically cause a new Lok Sabha/Assembly election if it gets the most votes.
- Five randomly selected polling stations per Assembly constituency or Assembly segment are generally checked through VVPAT under applicable directions. This is separate from a written request for slip counting or recount.

**Quick test:** Which form is the voter register? **17A.** Which is the booth vote account? **17C.** Who announces the official winner? **RO.**
