# 2. Indian history & cultural heritage

This syllabus is very broad: these are **high-yield anchors**, not every event in Indian history. Cultural heritage (సాంస్కృతిక వారసత్వం) includes monuments, languages, music, dance, craft, knowledge and living traditions.

## ⭐ Learn these 20 first

1. **Indus Valley Civilisation:** Harappa and Mohenjo-daro; planned cities, drainage and the Great Bath at Mohenjo-daro.
2. **Vedas:** the Rigveda is the oldest of the four Vedas; Vedic literature is in Sanskrit.
3. **Gautama Buddha:** enlightenment at Bodh Gaya; first sermon at **Sarnath**.
4. **Mahavira:** 24th Tirthankara of Jainism; ahimsa (non-violence) is central.
5. **Mauryan Empire:** Chandragupta Maurya founded it; Ashoka later embraced Buddhism and spread messages of dhamma through edicts.
6. **Kalinga war:** associated with Emperor Ashoka and his turn toward dhamma.
7. **Gupta period:** known for important developments in mathematics, science, literature and art; Aryabhata was a mathematician-astronomer.
8. **Cholas:** South Indian dynasty noted for temple architecture and maritime activity; Brihadisvara Temple is at Thanjavur.
9. **Taj Mahal:** Agra, built under Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal.
10. **1857 uprising:** important anti-British rebellion; Mangal Pandey, Rani Lakshmibai and Bahadur Shah Zafar are associated with it.
11. **Indian National Congress:** founded in **1885**; A. O. Hume was associated with its founding.
12. **Jallianwala Bagh massacre:** **1919**, Amritsar.
13. **Dandi Salt March:** led by Mahatma Gandhi in **1930**.
14. **Quit India Movement:** launched in **1942**.
15. **Independence:** **15 August 1947**; Constitution adopted **26 November 1949**, effective **26 January 1950**.
16. **Constitution Drafting Committee chair:** **Dr B. R. Ambedkar**; Constituent Assembly president: **Dr Rajendra Prasad**.
17. **National anthem:** Jana Gana Mana, composed by **Rabindranath Tagore**. National song: Vande Mataram, by **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay**.
18. **UNESCO** = United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization; it recognises World Heritage sites.
19. **Ramappa Temple:** Telangana, Kakatiya heritage, UNESCO World Heritage inscription **2021**.
20. **Sarnath:** ancient Buddhist site in Uttar Pradesh; UNESCO inscription **2026**. [UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/927/).

## C. National symbols and cultural forms

- **Flag:** saffron, white and green; navy-blue Ashoka Chakra with **24 spokes**.
- **National emblem:** adaptation of Ashoka's Lion Capital at Sarnath; motto **Satyameva Jayate** from the Mundaka Upanishad.
- **National animal:** tiger; **national bird:** Indian peacock; **national flower:** lotus.
- **Heritage:** tangible = buildings/objects; intangible = living skills, festivals, oral traditions and performing arts.
