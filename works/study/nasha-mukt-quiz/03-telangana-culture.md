# 3. Telangana & Khammam cultural heritage

## ⭐ Learn these 20 first

1. **Telangana State Formation Day:** **2 June 2014**. Capital: **Hyderabad**.
2. Historic dynasties: **Satavahanas, Kakatiyas, Qutb Shahis, Asaf Jahis (Nizams)**.
3. **Kakatiyas:** associated with Warangal, forts, temples, lakes and distinctive architecture.
4. **Rani Rudrama Devi:** prominent Kakatiya woman ruler.
5. **Warangal Fort:** known for the **Kakatiya Kala Thoranam** (ornamental stone gateway).
6. **Ramappa Temple:** also **Kakatiya Rudreshwara Temple**, at **Palampet, Mulugu district**.
7. Ramappa: Kakatiya-era monument, associated with general **Recharla Rudra**; UNESCO World Heritage **2021**. It is known for sculptural art and lightweight bricks.
8. **Thousand Pillar Temple:** Hanamkonda; Kakatiya architecture.
9. **Charminar:** Hyderabad; built by **Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah**, **1591**.
10. **Golconda Fort:** major fort associated with the Qutb Shahi rulers.
11. **Bathukamma (బతుకమ్మ):** Telangana's floral festival, associated with women, seasonal flowers and songs.
12. **Bonalu (బోనాలు):** festival of offerings associated with Goddess Mahakali.
13. **Sammakka–Saralamma Jatara:** major tribal festival at **Medaram**, Mulugu district; held biennially.
14. **Perini Shivatandavam:** vigorous dance associated with Kakatiya-era warrior tradition and Shiva.
15. **Oggu Katha:** traditional storytelling performance involving song and music.
16. **Pochampally Ikat:** resist-dyed textile tradition associated with **Bhoodan Pochampally**.
17. **Cheriyal paintings:** painted scroll tradition used for storytelling.
18. **Khammam Fort:** historic hill fort in Khammam town. Sources differ on its exact early construction date: remember the place and heritage, not a disputed year.
19. **Nelakondapalli:** Khammam district; Buddhist archaeological heritage and association with **Bhakta Ramadasu / Kancherla Gopanna**.
20. **Bhadrachalam:** Sri Sita Ramachandra Swamy temple on the Godavari, in present-day **Bhadradri Kothagudem district**, not present-day Khammam district.

## C. Telangana and nearby Khammam connections

- **Khammam district:** district headquarters is Khammam. The district site says it was formed on **1 October 1953**; reorganised in 2016.
- **Khammam Fort:** local heritage landmark in the city.
- **Nelakondapalli:** Buddhist stupa/archaeological site; Bhakta Ramadasu memorial association.
- **Kusumanchi Sivalayam:** Kakatiya-period temple in Khammam district.
- **Bhadrachalam:** in **Bhadradri Kothagudem**, a separate district created in the 2016 reorganisation. The Godavari flows by the temple town.
- **Kinnerasani:** reservoir and wildlife area in the Bhadradri Kothagudem region.
- **Medaram:** Mulugu district, not Khammam, although people travel to its Jatara from many districts.

### Easy comparisons

- **Bathukamma = flowers**; **Bonalu = offerings to Mahakali**.
- **Ramappa = Palampet, Mulugu, UNESCO 2021**; **Thousand Pillar Temple = Hanamkonda**.
- **Kakatiya = Warangal / Ramappa**; **Qutb Shahi = Charminar / Golconda**.
- **Khammam = fort / Nelakondapalli**; **Bhadrachalam = Bhadradri Kothagudem**.
