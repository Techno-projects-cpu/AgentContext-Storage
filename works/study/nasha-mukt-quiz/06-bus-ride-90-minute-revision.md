# 🚌 The 90-minute bus-ride quiz workout

**How to use this:** Six rounds of about 15 minutes. For each round, read the bullets, cover the answers with your hand, then answer aloud or in your head. Repeat anything you miss. The times are suggestions, not a race. If you feel carsick, put the phone away and recall facts aloud instead!

**Remember:** the poster lists **three** areas: Drug-Free India, Indian history and cultural heritage, and Telangana cultural heritage. Questions here are practice, not the actual quiz. Read this page **once before boarding** if internet might be patchy, or download/save it offline.

## Round 1 · Minutes 0–15: The campaign and its people

### Learn

- **NMBA:** Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan. Launched **15 August 2020** by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
- Coverage: **272 districts in 2020 → 372 in 2022 → all districts from 15 August 2023**. Each figure refers to a different stage.
- **NAPDDR:** National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction; NMBA is part of it.
- Three linked actions: **reduce illegal supply**, **reduce demand through awareness**, and **treat/support rehabilitation**.
- **MSJE:** leads the campaign and awareness; **MHA:** supervises the NCB; **health departments:** medical care; **district collector:** local campaign coordination.
- **NCB:** Narcotics Control Bureau, established **17 March 1986**, is a central drug-law enforcement and coordination agency.
- **TGANB:** Telangana Anti-Narcotics Bureau, a state agency; the **Khammam Prohibition and Excise Department** is identified as the event organiser on your poster.
- **Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance:** narcotics policy and NDPS administration responsibilities. This is **not the same question** as “Which ministry is NCB under?”
- **Master Volunteers:** trained people who spread awareness in communities. **NGOs** and schools can support the campaign too; they do not replace doctors or the police.
- **NDPS Act, 1985:** Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act. It regulates controlled substances; authorised medical/scientific use is different from illegal trafficking or misuse.

### Recall (answer before peeking)

1. What do NMBA and NAPDDR stand for?
2. Which ministry leads NMBA, and which ministry oversees NCB?
3. What do 272, 372 and “all districts” refer to?
4. Who normally heads the district Nasha Mukt committee?
5. Which body is the Telangana specialist anti-narcotics agency?
6. What are the three broad approaches of NMBA?
7. What is the full name and year of the main narcotics Act?
8. Does the district collector personally do every raid and every treatment?
9. Which central department has narcotics-policy and NDPS-administration responsibilities?
10. Who organises the event according to your poster?

**Answers:** 1. Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan; National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction. 2. Social Justice and Empowerment; Home Affairs. 3. Campaign coverage in 2020, 2022 and from August 2023, respectively. 4. District Collector/District Magistrate. 5. TGANB. 6. Supply control, demand reduction/awareness, treatment/rehabilitation. 7. Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. 8. No; different officials and professionals have different roles. 9. Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance. 10. Prohibition and Excise Department, Khammam District.

## Round 2 · Minutes 15–30: Helplines, health and tricky distinctions

### Learn

- **14446 = de-addiction:** information, counselling, guidance and referral.
- **1933 = MANAS:** narcotics reporting and support; MANAS and Tele-MANAS are different services.
- **14416 = Tele-MANAS:** mental-health support.
- **112 = emergency response**; **108 = ambulance in Telangana**. If a person is unconscious or breathing slowly, call immediately and follow dispatcher instructions.
- **26 June:** International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking. **31 May:** World No Tobacco Day.
- **UNODC:** United Nations Office on Drugs and Crime. **WHO:** World Health Organization.
- **Nicotine:** addictive chemical in tobacco. Smoking and smokeless tobacco can cause serious disease.
- **Alcohol:** depressant; can impair judgment and coordination. Alcohol and tobacco are regulated differently from NDPS-controlled substances.
- **Opioids:** includes heroin and prescribed medical opioids. Overdose can slow breathing; **naloxone** can reverse opioid effects, but emergency care is still required.
- **Cocaine:** stimulant. Substances have different effects; don't label every drug a stimulant or an opioid.
- **Tolerance:** needing more for the same effect; **withdrawal:** symptoms after reducing/stopping some substances. **Addiction is treatable** and should not be met with stigma or shame.
- **Say no, leave, tell a trusted adult.** Help a friend get confidential professional guidance; do not handle dangerous situations alone.

### Recall

11. Which number helps with de-addiction?
12. Which number reports narcotics-related activity through MANAS?
13. What does 14416 connect you to?
14. Name the two international days and their dates.
15. What is addictive in tobacco?
16. Is alcohol primarily a stimulant or a depressant?
17. What dangerous effect can opioid overdose have on breathing?
18. Is naloxone a replacement for an ambulance or medical care?
19. What is the difference between tolerance and withdrawal?
20. Does a person with addiction deserve blame or access to care?

**Answers:** 11. 14446. 12. 1933. 13. Tele-MANAS mental-health support. 14. 26 June: International Day against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking; 31 May: World No Tobacco Day. 15. Nicotine. 16. Depressant. 17. Breathing can slow dangerously or stop. 18. No; call emergency services and seek medical care. 19. Tolerance is a reduced effect at the same dose; withdrawal is symptoms after reducing or stopping a substance. 20. Compassionate care and support.

## Round 3 · Minutes 30–45: Sports stories and sport agencies

### Learn

- **Doping (డోపింగ్):** breaking anti-doping rules, which cover prohibited substances/methods and other violations. Rules and lists change.
- **WADA:** World Anti-Doping Agency; publishes the global Prohibited List. **NADA India:** national anti-doping programme. **NDTL:** National Dope Testing Laboratory, analyses samples. **AIU:** Athletics Integrity Unit, handles integrity in international athletics.
- **Ben Johnson:** Canadian sprinter, **Seoul Olympics 1988**, positive steroid test and disqualified; **Carl Lewis** received 100-metre gold.
- **Kamalpreet Kaur:** Indian discus thrower; 2022 AIU decision recorded a **three-year** sanction for stanozolol. That is a past decision, not a claim she remains banned.
- **Sha’Carri Richardson:** US sprinter, **2021**, one-month sanction following a cannabis metabolite result. USADA said use was outside competition and unrelated to performance. **Do not infer addiction.**
- **Kirti Bhoite / coach Mickey Menezes:** Indian anti-doping panel sanctioned the coach for administering a prohibited steroid to the athlete. Athlete support personnel can be accountable too.
- **Neeraj Chopra:** positive example—his foundation and the Indian Olympic Association announced clean-sport education cooperation in **2026**; not an allegation against him.
- **TUE = Therapeutic Use Exemption:** possible approval under strict conditions for medically necessary treatment involving a prohibited substance.
- **National Anti-Doping Act 2022** concerns sports anti-doping; **NDPS Act 1985** concerns controlled narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. They are not the same law.
- A positive result is not by itself proof of **addiction**, a **criminal conviction**, or intentional performance enhancement. Medical need, contamination and applicable procedures matter.

### Recall

21. What is WADA's role? What is NADA India's role?
22. Which laboratory analyses dope-testing samples in India?
23. Which sprinter lost Olympic 100-metre gold after a steroid-positive test in 1988?
24. Who received that gold after the disqualification?
25. Which Indian discus thrower was sanctioned for stanozolol in 2022?
26. Which American sprinter accepted a one-month cannabis-related sanction in 2021?
27. Can an athlete-support person face anti-doping action?
28. What does TUE mean?
29. Which law relates to India's sports anti-doping framework: 1985 NDPS or National Anti-Doping Act 2022?
30. Does a positive sports test prove a person is addicted?

**Answers:** 21. WADA coordinates international standards and publishes the list; NADA India runs national anti-doping activities. 22. NDTL. 23. Ben Johnson. 24. Carl Lewis. 25. Kamalpreet Kaur. 26. Sha’Carri Richardson. 27. Yes; coach Mickey Menezes is a documented example. 28. Therapeutic Use Exemption. 29. National Anti-Doping Act 2022. 30. No.

## Round 4 · Minutes 45–60: Indian history and culture

### Learn

- **Indus civilisation:** Harappa and Mohenjo-daro; organised streets and drainage. **Great Bath → Mohenjo-daro.**
- The four Vedas are **Rig, Sama, Yajur, Atharva**; **Rigveda** is the oldest.
- **Buddha:** enlightenment at **Bodh Gaya**, first sermon at **Sarnath**, mahaparinirvana at **Kushinagar**.
- **Mahavira:** 24th Jain Tirthankara; **ahimsa** means non-violence.
- **Chandragupta Maurya:** founded the Mauryan Empire; **Ashoka:** Mauryan emperor associated with the Kalinga war and edicts.
- **Chandragupta II:** Gupta ruler generally identified with “Chandra” in Delhi's Iron Pillar inscription. Do not confuse him with **Chandragupta Maurya**.
- **Samudragupta:** associated with the Allahabad/Prayagraj Pillar inscription; **Ashoka** also has edicts on pillars. Different inscriptions, different contexts.
- Freedom sequence: **INC 1885 → Jallianwala Bagh 1919 → Dandi March 1930 → Quit India 1942 → Independence 1947 → Constitution in force 1950**.
- **Dr B. R. Ambedkar:** Drafting Committee chair; **Dr Rajendra Prasad:** Constituent Assembly president.
- **Jana Gana Mana:** Rabindranath Tagore; **Vande Mataram:** Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay.
- Classical dance pairs: **Bharatanatyam–Tamil Nadu; Kathakali/Mohiniyattam–Kerala; Kuchipudi–Andhra Pradesh; Odissi–Odisha; Sattriya–Assam; Manipuri–Manipur; Kathak–North India.**
- **UNESCO** recognises World Heritage properties. **Sarnath** was inscribed in 2026; **Ramappa** in 2021. A UNESCO inscription year is not the year a monument was built.

### Recall

31. Which Veda is the oldest?
32. Where did Buddha deliver his first sermon?
33. Which Mauryan ruler is associated with the Kalinga war?
34. Who founded the Mauryan Empire?
35. Which Gupta ruler is generally identified with “Chandra” of the Iron Pillar?
36. Which ruler is associated with the Allahabad Pillar inscription?
37. Put these in order: Quit India, INC founding, Dandi March, Jallianwala Bagh.
38. Who chaired the Constitution's Drafting Committee?
39. Pair Sattriya and Kuchipudi with their states.
40. Who composed the national anthem, and who wrote the national song?

**Answers:** 31. Rigveda. 32. Sarnath. 33. Ashoka. 34. Chandragupta Maurya. 35. Chandragupta II. 36. Samudragupta. 37. INC 1885 → Jallianwala Bagh 1919 → Dandi March 1930 → Quit India 1942. 38. B. R. Ambedkar. 39. Assam; Andhra Pradesh. 40. Rabindranath Tagore; Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay.

## Round 5 · Minutes 60–75: Telangana and Khammam

### Learn

- **Telangana formation:** 2 June 2014; **Hyderabad** is the capital.
- **Kakatiya dynasty:** Warangal region, temple and fort heritage; **Rudrama Devi** was a prominent ruler.
- **Ramappa Temple:** Palampet, **Mulugu district**; Kakatiya-era Shiva temple. Construction began around **1213**, by general **Recharla Rudra**, associated with ruler **Ganapati Deva**. UNESCO World Heritage **2021**.
- **Thousand Pillar Temple:** **Hanamkonda**, associated with Kakatiya ruler **Rudra Deva**. Its alternative name Rudreshwara can be confused with Ramappa's—identify the **location**.
- **Charminar:** Hyderabad; **Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah**, **1591**. **Golconda Fort:** closely associated with Qutb Shahi history.
- **Bathukamma:** floral festival associated with Gauri. **Bonalu:** festival associated with Mahakali. **Medaram Jatara:** honours Sammakka and Saralamma, in Mulugu district, biennially.
- **Perini Shivatandavam:** warrior-associated Kakatiya tradition. **Oggu Katha:** song, music and storytelling. **Gussadi:** dance associated with Gond/Adivasi communities.
- **Pochampally:** ikat textiles; **Cheriyal:** narrative scroll painting; **Pembarthi:** metal craft; **Nirmal:** painted crafts.
- **Khammam Fort** is in Khammam. **Nelakondapalli** is connected with Buddhist heritage and Bhakta Ramadasu / Kancherla Gopanna.
- **Bhadrachalam:** on the Godavari in present-day **Bhadradri Kothagudem district**, not present-day Khammam district.
- **Komaram Bheem:** Adivasi leader associated with tribal rights; **Chakali Ailamma:** peasant resistance figure.

### Recall

41. Which dynasty is associated with Perini?
42. Is Ramappa in Mulugu or Hanamkonda district?
43. Name the ruler and general associated with Ramappa.
44. When was Ramappa inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List?
45. Where is the Thousand Pillar Temple?
46. Which festival is the flower festival: Bonalu or Bathukamma?
47. Which festival is associated with Mahakali?
48. Where is Sammakka–Saralamma Jatara held, and how often?
49. Which Khammam locality has Buddhist heritage and a Bhakta Ramadasu connection?
50. Which present-day district contains Bhadrachalam?

**Answers:** 41. Kakatiya. 42. Mulugu: Palampet village. 43. Ganapati Deva; Recharla Rudra. 44. 2021. 45. Hanamkonda. 46. Bathukamma. 47. Bonalu. 48. Medaram, Mulugu district; every two years. 49. Nelakondapalli. 50. Bhadradri Kothagudem.
