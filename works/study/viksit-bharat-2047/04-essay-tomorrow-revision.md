# Viksit Bharat 2047 — Tomorrow's Essay Revision

**First priority:** your essay is tomorrow. This is a compact revision sheet, followed by a one-page model you can learn from. The poster's prompt is: **“Five things I will do to make India the most developed country in the world by 2047.”**

Use your own words and follow your competition's rules about outside help. Handwriting and margins vary, so test the length on your own A4 sheet.

## Your five points in 20 seconds

**Honesty → Water → Education → Green innovation → Jobs**

1. **Honest governance:** fight corruption, make public decisions transparent and involve qualified experts.
2. **Safe water:** collect rainwater, fix leaks, treat water and protect rivers.
3. **Education and talent:** teach strong basics while giving students real opportunities in science, sports, arts or other interests.
4. **Science and greener industry:** support research, make more essential goods in India and reduce pollution while protecting nature.
5. **Dignified jobs:** expand training and work opportunities, ensure fair conditions and support people unable to work.

Your seven original ideas fit into five because **science, industry, self-reliance and environmental protection are one connected point**.

## What to say—and why

### 1. Honesty and capable leaders

Corruption can divert money meant for schools, roads and water. Transparent spending, independent checks and fair enforcement make officials answerable. An agriculture minister can seek advice from agricultural experts **and farmers**. Relevant qualifications matter, but a degree alone does not guarantee honesty.

**Recall phrase:** “Public money should serve the public.”

### 2. Safe water without harming rivers

Rainwater harvesting, pipe repairs, water treatment and careful distribution can improve water access. Rivers also carry sediment and support wetlands, fish and people downstream: **water reaching the sea is not automatically wasted**. Consider canals where appropriate, but assess environmental costs and share water fairly.

**Recall phrase:** “Save water, treat water, protect rivers.”

### 3. Education that develops talent

Every child deserves good foundational learning and a chance to explore interests. A child who loves sports should not lose the chance to train simply because of family income. You can advocate free, high-quality sports coaching as your goal; don't claim it already exists everywhere.

**Recall phrase:** “A child's future should be shaped by talent, not only by family income.”

### 4. Science, industry and every living thing

Research can support healthcare, farming, energy and education. India can make more essential products while trading for what it needs. Factories can use energy efficiently, treat wastewater and reduce waste; forests, wetlands and wildlife need protection too. A project that creates jobs but poisons a river is not complete progress.

**Recall phrase:** “Produce more, pollute less, protect life.”

### 5. Work with dignity

Skills training, support for businesses and investment can create opportunities. Jobs should be safe, fairly paid and not demand excessive hours. Your idea is opportunity for those **who want and are able to work**, not forced work; people unable to work deserve support.

**Recall phrase:** “A good job should provide both livelihood and dignity.”

## A simple 30-minute plan for tonight

1. **5 minutes:** read the five recall phrases and explain each without looking.
2. **10 minutes:** read the sample once, then close it and outline your own introduction, five points and conclusion.
3. **10 minutes:** write your own version by hand on one sheet. Use one action and one benefit for each point.
4. **5 minutes:** check spelling, count the five points, improve your opening and conclusion, and make sure the writing fits. Rest afterwards.

If you have more time, practise explaining how your points connect: **good governance helps fund education and water; educated people build cleaner technology; responsible industry creates jobs.**

## Model essay — read, understand, then write your own

### Five Steps Towards Viksit Bharat 2047

India will complete one hundred years of independence in 2047. My dream is of a nation that is not only prosperous and technologically advanced, but also honest, compassionate and environmentally responsible. I will work towards this future in five ways.

First, I will promote honest and capable government. Public money must serve the public, and leaders should make transparent decisions while listening to qualified experts and the people affected by their policies.

Second, I will work towards safe drinking water for every family. Rainwater harvesting, repairing leaks and treating water can help us use it wisely. At the same time, we must protect our rivers and the living things that depend on them.

Third, I will support education that develops each child's potential. All children need strong foundations, but they should also have opportunities to explore their own interests. A talented student should be able to pursue sports, science or the arts regardless of family income.

Fourth, I will encourage scientific research and responsible Indian manufacturing. Our industries can produce more essential goods while using energy efficiently, treating waste and protecting wildlife. Self-reliance can go hand in hand with useful trade and cooperation.

Finally, I will support dignified jobs through skills training and opportunities for those who want and are able to work. Fair wages, safe conditions and support for those unable to work are essential.

A truly developed India is one where people and nature thrive together. If we combine honesty, knowledge and compassion with action, we can build a better Bharat for everyone.

## Last check before handing in

- **Five** points, not seven separate ones?
- Did I use **“I will”** and give practical actions?
- Did I protect nature as well as encourage industry?
- Did I leave a few minutes to check and finish the conclusion?
- Is it my own work, in line with the competition rules?
